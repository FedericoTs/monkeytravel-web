# Wanderlog Alternative 2026: The Best Free AI Trip Planner

Wanderlog is one of the most established trip planners on the internet — a genuinely good mobile app, deep collaboration, and a place database that's hard to fault. If you like to build a trip by hand, it's excellent.

But a lot of people arrive at Wanderlog expecting an AI trip planner and discover two things: the planning is mostly manual, and the free AI assistant is capped (a February 2026 hands-on counted about 5 AI messages per trip) and **unlimited AI is a $39.99/year Pro feature**. If what you wanted was to have the plan *built for you*, that's a different tool than you thought you were getting.

This is an honest look at what Wanderlog does well, what its Pro plan costs, and the best free, AI-first alternative if you'd rather skip the manual work.

We're not affiliated with Wanderlog. MonkeyTravel is our own product, and it's the alternative we'll put forward — and we'll be clear about where Wanderlog still wins.

## Quick Verdict

Wanderlog is the better tool if you enjoy hand-building a detailed trip with friends, want strong booking-import and mobile features, and don't mind paying $39.99/year for unlimited AI help.

If you want an AI to generate a complete day-by-day itinerary for free — in 30 seconds, without signing up, and without an AI message cap — you want an AI-first tool instead. That's the gap MonkeyTravel fills.

The core manual planner is genuinely free and generous. The catch is that *unlimited AI* — the reason many people search for a "trip planner" in 2026 — is the paid part.

## Where Wanderlog Wins

**Maturity and polish.** Wanderlog has been refined over years. The mobile app is one of the best in the category, and the whole experience feels solid.

**Collaboration and booking import.** Multiple people can build a trip together in real time, and Wanderlog can pull in your hotel and flight confirmations automatically. For a hands-on group that likes to co-build, it's strong.

**A vast places database.** Restaurants, attractions, reviews — the underlying data is deep and reliable.

## Where Wanderlog Falls Short (for AI-first travelers)

**The free AI is capped.** About five AI messages per trip (the count from a February 2026 hands-on) isn't a real AI planner — it's a teaser. Unlimited AI assistance, route optimization, and Google Maps export all need the $39.99/year Pro plan.

**It's manual-first.** Wanderlog expects you to do the building. That's a feature if you love planning and a chore if you just want a solid first draft to react to.

**Built for co-editing, not deciding.** If you have a group that needs to *vote* to settle disagreements rather than all edit the same list, Wanderlog isn't built for that. (Language isn't the gap: Wanderlog's website is available in 40 languages, though its iOS app lists English.)
