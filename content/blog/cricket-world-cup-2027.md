# Cricket World Cup 2027: Tickets, Schedule, Visas and How to Plan Your Trip

The ICC Men's Cricket World Cup runs from 2 October to 21 November 2027 at 12 grounds in South Africa, Zimbabwe and Namibia. For now the only way to get tickets is the ICC's free ballot at tickets.cricketworldcup.com, which closes at 23:59 South African time on Saturday 21 November 2026 (03:29 on 22 November in India). India open against Australia in Cape Town on Thursday 7 October and play Pakistan at the Wanderers in Johannesburg on Sunday 10 October.

This guide is for fans deciding whether to enter the ballot, and planning the trip once results arrive. Every date, rule and price was read on the organiser's, a government's or an operator's own page on 7 November 2026; press reports are marked. Times are local; South Africa runs on UTC+2 ([NMISA](http://time.nmisa.org/time/)), three and a half hours behind India.

## How do Cricket World Cup 2027 tickets work?

All tickets so far go through one ballot, for every match in all three countries and for fans from anywhere ([ICC ballot FAQ](https://images.icc-cricket.com/image/private/s--LFoa6CxI--/v1790853500/prd/assets/assets/CWC_Ballot_FAQ.pdf)). The ticket site names Ticketpro as the seller.

1. **Create and verify an ICC account** at [tickets.cricketworldcup.com](https://tickets.cricketworldcup.com/). You must be 18 or over; children's tickets go on a parent's entry.
2. **Enter each match you want**, up to four tickets per match. Only one entry per person per match counts: duplicates disqualify you, and nobody else can enter for you. Tick accessible seating at checkout if you need it.
3. **Pay nothing yet.** Entry is free, no bank details are asked for, and an entry can't be edited once submitted.

It isn't first come, first served, so entering early gains nothing. Matches with more requests than seats go to a random draw; for the rest, every entrant gets to buy, and leftovers may go on general sale later.

**After the close:** results arrive by email within two weeks, so by about 5 December 2026. Winning is an invitation to buy within a set purchase window, possibly for fewer tickets than you asked for, and unpaid tickets may go back into the pool. Payment methods, international and local, will be announced before sales. If you miss out, the ICC says more tickets may come in later sales phases, announced by official email.

**Prices:** the [ICC](https://www.icc-cricket.com/media-releases/icc-men-s-cricket-world-cup-2027-schedule-revealed-and-ballot-system-opens) prices tickets in rand, from R158 for entry-level seats to R514 for the top-tier matches; its own dollar conversions run from about US$9.50 to US$31 as the exchange rate moves. At the [European Central Bank](https://www.ecb.europa.eu/stats/policy_and_exchange_rates/euro_reference_exchange_rates/html/index.en.html)'s rates of 6 November 2026, that's about ₹900 to ₹2,900, £7 to £23 or A$14 to A$45. Family bundles and prices for students and pensioners are mentioned, without details yet.

**Demand:** by 2 November the ballot had [more than 120 million ticket requests from 2.8 million fans in 193 countries](https://www.icc-cricket.com/tournaments/icc-cricket-world-cup-2027/news/cwc27-fever-builds-as-ticket-ballot-applications-soar). India v Pakistan drew 8,397,294 and the final 7,900,944. Other matches drew far fewer: Zimbabwe v India more than 3.3 million, England v Bangladesh in Windhoek nearly 900,000.

**Resale and unofficial sellers:** no resale or transfer rules are out yet; the FAQ says the full terms "will be available" on the ticket site and that fans should use only that site. The ballot exists partly to stop bots buying tickets to resell at higher prices, so treat any ticket or "ballot place" sold elsewhere as unofficial. For questions, use the Ticketpro support address in the FAQ. Official travel packages come through the ICC's own travel programme, [ICC Travel & Tours](https://icctravelandtours.com/), which had none on sale for this tournament on 7 November 2026, only a form to register interest; we found no official hospitality packages either.

**India's Super 7 dates are slots.** India are pre-seeded A1, so their six Super 7 matches are already dated. If India don't qualify, the team that does takes the A1 slot ([ICC](https://www.icc-cricket.com/tournaments/icc-cricket-world-cup-2027/news/when-does-the-2027-cricket-world-cup-start-key-details-ahead-of-tournament)).

**The trade-off:** the ballot is free and fair, but nothing is yours until you pay inside the window, and results land in early December, so flights and visas wait until then.

## Do Indians need a visa for South Africa, Zimbabwe and Namibia?

Yes, you need permission for all three: South Africa's ETA and Namibia's e-visa online before you fly, and Zimbabwe's visa on arrival.

**South Africa: apply for an ETA.** India was one of four pilot countries for South Africa's Electronic Travel Authorisation, launched officially on 12 August 2026. Register at [eta.dha.gov.za](https://eta.dha.gov.za/), scan your passport, upload a selfie and pay; Home Affairs says travellers "will receive their ETA within 24 hours" ([launch speech](https://www.gov.za/news/speeches/minister-leon-schreiber-launch-south-africa%E2%80%99s-electronic-travel-authorisation-12-aug)). It covers stays of up to 90 days, extendable by up to 90 more, and for now works only for arrivals at OR Tambo (Johannesburg), Cape Town, King Shaka (Durban) and Lanseria airports. Home Affairs set a R500 processing fee for the ETA (about ₹2,840) from 17 August 2026 ([Government Gazette](https://www.dha.gov.za/images/gazettes/2026/55209-17-8-26.pdf)), and Indian passports are exempt from the separate visa fee ([Home Affairs](https://www.dha.gov.za/index.php/immigration-services/exempt-countries)). The speech didn't say whether one ETA covers leaving for Victoria Falls and coming back, so check your approval before planning a second entry.

**Zimbabwe: visa on arrival.** Indian passports are in Category B: no application before travel, you pay at the border. You can also apply at [evisa.gov.zw](https://www.evisa.gov.zw/faq.html), where processing averages seven working days and you should apply at least three weeks ahead. Fees aren't published; the approval states the cost. A single-entry visa allows one entry, so leaving Zimbabwe and coming back needs a double-entry visa or a new one.

**Namibia: e-visa before you fly.** Indian ordinary passports aren't on Namibia's visa-on-arrival list, so you need an e-visa from [eservices.mhaiss.gov.na](https://eservices.mhaiss.gov.na/): N$1,600 for anyone 12 or over (about US$90, by the ministry's conversion), N$800 for ages 6 to 11, free under 6, with fees "subject to review" ([fact sheet](https://namibiaembassyusa.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/09/FactSheetVisaRequirementstoNamibia.pdf)). India play no matches in Namibia.

**Zambia and the KAZA UNIVISA.** The US$50 [KAZA UNIVISA](https://www.zambiaimmigration.gov.zm/kaza-univisa/) covers Zambia and Zimbabwe for up to 30 days, plus day trips to Botswana through Kazungula, but Indian passports aren't on its eligible list. To see the falls from the Zambian side, Indians need a Zambian visa [approved online before travel](https://www.zambiaimmigration.gov.zm/nationals-requiring-visa-prior-to-travel/).

**Other passports:**
- **UK, Australia and New Zealand:** no visa for South Africa for up to 90 days ([Home Affairs](https://www.dha.gov.za/index.php/immigration-services/exempt-countries)); visa on arrival or e-visa for Namibia. UK and Australian passports get a visa on arrival in Zimbabwe and can use the KAZA UNIVISA; UK passports can also buy a multiple-entry visa on arrival.
- **Pakistan:** a South African visa is needed even to change planes there. Zimbabwe (Category C), Namibia and Zambia all need visas before travel.
- **South Africa:** no visa for Zimbabwe or Namibia.

Apply only on the government sites linked here.

**The trade-off:** the ETA makes South Africa simple, but it is new and airport-only, and Zimbabwe's default visa is single-entry, so a route that crosses borders twice needs planning.

## How do you fly from India to South Africa?

There's no nonstop flight. South African Airways stopped flying to Mumbai in 2015 and its planned return slipped into 2026 with no date, while Ethiopian Airlines, via Addis Ababa, dominated the Johannesburg–Mumbai route with about 20,000 passengers a year and one of the lowest average fares (reported by [Travel News](https://www.travelnews.co.za/article/india-route-demand-rises-but-viability-questioned), February 2026). [Emirates](https://www.emirates.com/english/destinations/bom/jnb/flights-from-mumbai-to-johannesburg/) sells Mumbai to Johannesburg with a change in Dubai; a sample itinerary on its route page takes 15 hours 55 minutes. India start in Cape Town and play Pakistan in Johannesburg three days later, so price a ticket into one city and out of the other.

**The yellow fever catch.** Ethiopia and Kenya are on [India's yellow fever list](https://ihpoe.mohfw.gov.in/vaccination.php); South Africa, Zimbabwe, Namibia and Zambia aren't. India's health ministry wants a vaccination certificate if you left the airport's transit area in a listed country, though [WHO's list](https://cdn.who.int/media/docs/default-source/travel-and-health/countries-with-risk-of-yellow-fever-transmission.pdf) says India applies the rule to any transit. South Africa, Zimbabwe and Namibia ask for proof after a transit of more than 12 hours at such an airport. Without it, India can quarantine you for up to six days. Connect through the Gulf, or get the vaccine at a designated centre at least 10 days before you travel; the certificate lasts for life, and India rejects photocopies and digital versions.

**The trade-off:** Addis Ababa comes with one of the route's lowest average fares but brings the yellow fever paperwork; a Gulf connection avoids it.

Fly the long legs and use the train around Johannesburg.

Distances are our calculation between the cities' weather stations, mostly at airports; roads are longer. FlySafair, Airlink, SAA, CemAir and LIFT are among the airlines listed at [OR Tambo](https://www.airports.co.za/airports/or-tambo-international-airport/At-the-airport/airlines).

**Gautrain.** The [Gautrain](https://www.gautrain.co.za/) links OR Tambo airport, Sandton, Rosebank and Park with Centurion, Pretoria and Hatfield. Sandton to Centurion costs R79 (about ₹450) at peak fares, which apply all day at weekends and on public holidays ([fares from 1 June 2025](https://bombelacontent.s3.eu-west-1.amazonaws.com/15305+BOMBELA+FARE+GUIDE+BROCHURE+-+2025+Digital.pdf)). The UK's [FCDO](https://www.gov.uk/foreign-travel-advice/south-africa/safety-and-security) calls it generally safer than other public transport but advises against walking to or from stations after dark.

**Victoria Falls.** [fastjet](https://www.fastjet.com/en/flights-from-victoria-falls) flies from Victoria Falls to Johannesburg, Harare, Bulawayo and Kruger Mbombela, and [SAA](https://www.flysaa.com/en/flights-to-victoria-falls) from Johannesburg.

**Coaches, trains and Paarl.** Long-distance coaches link the cities, but the FCDO lists long-distance coaches and trains, minibus taxis and Metrorail among the higher-risk ways to travel. For Boland Park in Paarl, plan a car or a booked transfer from Cape Town.

**The trade-off:** flying is the only way to keep up with India's schedule, and each leg adds a fare and an airport run.

## Where should you base yourself?

**Johannesburg (Sandton or Rosebank).** The Wanderers hosts India v Pakistan, an India Super 7 slot on 31 October and the final, and the Gautrain reaches Centurion for India v Afghanistan and the second semi-final. **The trade-off:** the FCDO warns of robbers following people from OR Tambo and of carjacking after dark, so book airport pick-ups and don't walk at night.

**Cape Town.** Newlands has India's opener, the possible India v Pakistan Super 7 match and the first semi-final, and Paarl hosts India's 4 November slot. It is also the driest South African host in October and November. **The trade-off:** the FCDO reports muggings on Lion's Head and Signal Hill, advises hiking in groups of four or more, and says to take the M3 and N2 from the airport, not the R300.

**Victoria Falls.** India play there on 24 October and, if they reach the Super 7, on 28 October, so you can stay the four nights in between. Zambezi National Park, 530 km² (205 sq mi), borders the town ([ZimParks](https://www.zimparks.org.zw/wp-content/uploads/2024/07/Zambezi-Victoria-Falls-GMP_April_2024-Final.pdf)). **The trade-off:** the falls flow lowest in October and November, it is the hottest time of year, and Zimbabwe is short of US dollar cash.

**Bloemfontein, Gqeberha and Durban.** One or two nights around a match is plenty. **The trade-off:** each is a separate flight or a long drive from the bases above.

## Is it safe, and what should you know before you go?

The [FCDO](https://www.gov.uk/foreign-travel-advice/south-africa/safety-and-security) describes "a high crime rate in South Africa", mostly in townships, informal settlements and high-density areas, and higher after dark. It warns of robbers following travellers from OR Tambo, carjacking at junctions and ATM scams. Use a licensed taxi or an established e-hailing app and check the car and driver match the app; visit townships only with a reliable guide. Power cuts still happen, so carry a power bank.

In Zimbabwe, the [FCDO](https://www.gov.uk/foreign-travel-advice/zimbabwe/safety-and-security) says the US dollar is used for most payments, but physical dollar cash is short, international cards may not work at ATMs and "change is rarely available". Bring small, clean notes, don't photograph police, government buildings or airports, and carry a copy of your passport and visa for roadblocks.

We found no current Indian government advisory for South Africa. The [High Commission in Pretoria](https://www.hcipretoria.gov.in/page/emergency-cases-and-contacts/) runs a 24-hour line for emergencies only, +27 82 867 6851 by call or WhatsApp, and an online registration form for Indian nationals living in South Africa.

South Africa's [health department](https://www.health.gov.za/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/Protocols-for-Travellers.pdf) lists KwaZulu-Natal, Mpumalanga and Limpopo as malaria provinces, so ask a travel clinic about tablets if you're going on safari or to Victoria Falls. The same protocol says medicine for personal use needs authorisation from SAHPRA, the medicines regulator, so check before you pack prescription drugs. We found no 2027 stadium entry rules (bags, flags, power banks) yet; look for them in the ticket terms.

## What to do first, and by when

1. **By 23:59 SAST on 21 November 2026 (03:29 IST on the 22nd):** enter the ballot for every match you'd travel for, plus a few with lower demand.
2. **Now:** check your passport. Namibia's e-visa asks for six months' validity and three blank pages.
3. **By about 5 December 2026:** watch for the result email, spam folder included, and pay inside the window.
4. **After the results:** book flights, then beds for the busiest dates: India v Pakistan, the semi-finals and the final.
5. **Before you fly:** get the South African ETA and any e-visas you need (Zimbabwe's three weeks ahead). If you'll connect in Addis Ababa or Nairobi, have the yellow fever jab at least 10 days before you travel.
