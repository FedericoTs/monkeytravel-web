# Where to Go in November 2026: 12 Destinations Compared

November is the month the travel calendar forgets. It sits in the gap between the autumn-colour rush and the Christmas-market surge, and the summer crowds are gone from every headline destination. The one exception that matters, for anyone flying from the United States, is Thanksgiving week, 23–27 November 2026. Outside it, the world is quietly on sale.

November is also the pivot month of the global weather map. The southwest monsoon has withdrawn and the northeast monsoon has taken over, flipping mainland Southeast Asia into its long dry season — and shifting the rain onto Thailand's Gulf coast and India's Tamil Nadu coast instead. The Middle East and North Africa cool from furnace to comfortable, and below the equator it is late spring: jacarandas going purple in Buenos Aires, the wind warming up over Cape Town, days stretching past fourteen hours in New Zealand. Here are twelve places where November is the point, with sourced climate normals, the 2026 dates that matter, and the honest trade-off.

## November 2026 at a glance

- **Sunday 1 and Monday 2 November:** Día de Muertos in Mexico (gob.mx); book those Oaxaca nights well ahead or arrive later in the week.
- **Sunday 1 November:** the Routeburn Track's Great Walks season opens in New Zealand (the Kepler opened on Tuesday 27 October).
- **Friday 6 to Tuesday 10 November:** the Diwali run from Dhanteras to Bhai Dooj, with Lakshmi Puja on Sunday 8 November (Drik Panchang). Fly into Kerala before the Friday or after the Tuesday to dodge the domestic-travel surge.
- **Saturday 21 to Monday 23 November:** Argentina observes Día de la Soberanía Nacional on Monday 23 November (the 20th is a Friday), a three-day weekend in Buenos Aires; book domestic flights out of the capital early.
- **Tuesday 24 November:** the twelfth-month full moon (21:53 Thailand time, US Naval Observatory) sets Loy Krathong nationwide and Yi Peng in Chiang Mai, which usually runs the nights either side; book Chiang Mai beds for the festival nights well ahead.
- **Thursday 26 November:** US Thanksgiving. The fare spike runs the week of 23–27 November, so leave on an earlier weekend or stay abroad through it (see the FAQ).

## 1. Thailand — The Cool Dry Season Opens

The northeast monsoon starts pulling the rain off the Andaman coast, so Phuket, Krabi, and the Phi Phi islands are drying out through November but are not yet reliably dry — showers are still common, and the settled season arrives in December. Phuket's 1991–2020 normal for the month is 178.2 mm of rain on 12.9 days, under a 32.1 °C average high and 25.2 °C low. Chiang Mai's nights turn cool: a 19.9 °C average low under a 30.8 °C high, 40.9 mm of rain for the month and 229 hours of sunshine. It is festival season too: Loy Krathong and Chiang Mai's Yi Peng lantern release fall around the twelfth-month full moon on Tuesday 24 November in 2026 (Chiang Mai's Yi Peng usually runs the nights either side).

The entry rules changed this autumn: since 15 September 2026 the visa exemption for passport holders of 60 countries and territories (including the US, UK, Canada, Australia, Italy, Spain, Germany and France) allows up to 30 days, not the old 60, and every visitor must file the free Thailand Digital Arrival Card online within the 3 days before arrival (TAT Newsroom; GOV.UK).

**Cost reality:** mid-range travellers in Phuket spend about $134 per person per day (Budget Your Trip average); the islands cost more than the north, and prices climb all month as high season ramps toward December.
**The trade-off:** the Gulf islands (Koh Samui, Koh Phangan, Koh Tao) run the opposite rain calendar and are still in their wet season in November. Go Andaman side, not Gulf side.

## 2. Kerala & Southern India — The Post-Monsoon Payoff

The southwest monsoon is long over and the northeast monsoon, which arrives in mid-October, is tailing off through November: expect some showers, mostly in the south of the state, but far fewer than October. The backwaters are full and green and the humidity is easing. Kochi's airport station averages a 32.6 °C high and 22.9 °C low, with 175.9 mm of rain over 11 rain days (0.3 mm or more, 1991–2020 airport normal; the older city-station normals are far wetter). The Western Ghats' tea hills around Munnar are back to walkable, and the true dry season begins in December.

Diwali's Lakshmi Puja falls on [Sunday 8 November in 2026](https://www.drikpanchang.com/diwali/diwali-puja-calendar.html?year=2026), with the festive run from Friday 6 to Tuesday 10 November. India's e-Tourist Visa must be applied for online at least 4 days before arrival (Indian Visa Online).

**Cost reality:** Kochi is one of the best-value bases on this list at about $40 per person per day for a mid-range traveller (Budget Your Trip average); a night on a backwater houseboat is the splurge and still modest.
**The trade-off:** the far southeast, the Tamil Nadu coast around Chennai, catches the *northeast* monsoon in November, so the rain simply shifts to the opposite coast. Stay on the Kerala side and in the Ghats.

## 3. Oman — The Furnace Switches Off

From November the Gulf heat breaks. Muscat averages a 30.3 °C high and 20.8 °C low with 1.6 mm of rain for the whole month (Oman Directorate General of Meteorology normals via the WMO), the wadis are swimmable again and desert nights turn cool enough for a fire. It opens Oman's short, near-perfect window: the canyons of Jebel Shams, the Musandam fjords, and long empty beaches. Entry got easier this year: visitors from more than 100 countries and regions are visa-exempt for up to 14 days, and a free 14-day tourist visa for nationals designated by the Royal Oman Police came into force on 3 August 2026 (Oman Ministry of Foreign Affairs).

**Cost reality:** Budget Your Trip has no Muscat page; its country-level average for Oman is about $301 per person per day for a mid-range traveller, the highest sourced figure on this list, and a 4x4 is close to mandatory for the interior.
**The trade-off:** distances are big and public transport is thin, so budget for the rental and fuel. Travellers who want a beach and nothing else will find it very quiet.

## 4. Jordan (Petra & Wadi Rum) — Autumn on the Desert Trails

November is Jordan's sweet-spot shoulder. Aqaba, the nearest official station to Wadi Rum, averages a 27.0 °C high and 14.9 °C low with 3.0 mm of rain (Jordan Meteorological Department via the WMO), and up at Wadi Rum itself, at 952 m, the FAO climate table gives a 21.1 °C high and 9.2 °C low. That makes Petra's back-country loop and Wadi Rum genuinely pleasant, and the Dana-to-Petra trek is in its best window of the year.

**Cost reality:** mid-range travellers in Petra spend about $153 per person per day (Budget Your Trip average); Petra passes and guides push this up. The Jordan Pass waives the 40 JOD visa fee (only if you buy it before you land and stay at least 2 nights) and covers over 40 attractions including Petra, so it pays for itself fast; it costs 70, 75 or 80 JOD for one, two or three consecutive days at Petra.
**The trade-off:** desert nights get cold fast, and a Wadi Rum camp can drop into single digits (°C) after dark. Pack real layers, not just a swimsuit for the Dead Sea.

## 5. Egypt — The Nile at Its Best

November opens Egypt's prime season. The 1991–2020 normals put Luxor at a 29.1 °C high and 13.4 °C low with 0.5 mm of rain, and Aswan at 29.8 °C and 16.2 °C with no measurable rain — warm but no longer punishing, so the Valley of the Kings is doable even at midday. The Nile-cruise boats are in their main season and the Red Sea resorts stay diveable.

A visa on arrival costs 30 US dollars in cash; since 1 August 2026 Cairo airport has also sold a card-paid QR-code version for 36 dollars. The official e-visa at visa2egypt.gov.eg is 30 dollars single-entry and must be applied for at least 7 days before departure (GOV.UK; VisasNews). The Grand Egyptian Museum sells tickets only through tickets.gem.eg for a chosen, non-refundable entry slot; galleries open 9:00–18:00 five days a week and 9:00–21:00 on Wednesday and Saturday.

**Cost reality:** Luxor is the cheapest base on this list at about $31 per person per day for a mid-range traveller (Budget Your Trip average); a multi-night Nile cruise is the big-ticket item.
**The trade-off:** November is the start of Egypt's peak season — crowds at Giza and along the Nile grow through the month and keep building into winter — so reserve the Nile cruise and internal flights ahead.

## 6. Marrakech, Morocco — The Souks Without the Furnace

Summer Marrakech averages 37–38 °C by day and pushes past 40 °C in heatwaves; November brings it back to a walkable 23.1 °C average high with nights down to 10.7 °C, 29.8 mm of rain on 3.8 days (1991–2020 normals), and clear light on the snow-dusted High Atlas. The medina, the Jardin Majorelle and day trips to the Ourika Valley or Essaouira are at their most pleasant. British passport holders can visit visa-free for up to 90 days with a passport valid at least 3 months beyond arrival (GOV.UK).

**Cost reality:** mid-range travellers in Marrakech spend about $101 per person per day (Budget Your Trip average); a riad with a courtyard is the classic mid-range stay.
**The trade-off:** nights get genuinely cold and many riads heat their rooms poorly, so ask about heating before you book. Early snow can also close the High Atlas passes if you're chasing the Sahara beyond the mountains.

## 7. Madeira, Portugal — The Atlantic's Evergreen Spring

While the rest of Europe reaches for a coat, Funchal averages a 22.8 °C high and 17.0 °C low, with 80.3 mm of rain on 7.3 days (IPMA 1991–2020 normal), its *levada* trails green and walkable. Dolphins and whales are still offshore, and off-season short-haul flights from Western Europe are a fraction of the summer fare. Stay to the end of the month and you catch Funchal's Christmas lights switching on Tuesday 1 December (Events Madeira).

For non-EU visitors the EU Entry/Exit System has been fully operational since 10 April 2026, and ETIAS is not yet in operation — the EU moved its launch from 2026 to 2027 in July and has since put it back with no date set — so there is nothing to apply for (European Commission; Travel Market Report).

**Cost reality:** mid-range travellers in Funchal spend about $135 per person per day (Budget Your Trip average): European prices, but well below peak; the flights are where the saving shows.
**The trade-off:** it's an Atlantic island, so weather is changeable and the north coast catches more cloud and rain than the south. Build flexible trail days and follow the sun.

## 8. Oaxaca, Mexico — Día de Muertos

The first days of November are Oaxaca's moment. Día de Muertos falls on Sunday 1 November (Todos los Santos, for deceased children) and Monday 2 November 2026 (Fieles Difuntos, for adults), and the city fills with marigold-draped altars, candlelit cemetery vigils, and *comparsas* winding through the streets. Beyond the festival it is dry-season Oaxaca: a 29.0 °C average high and 12.3 °C low, 9.1 mm of rain on 3.5 rain days (Servicio Meteorológico Nacional 1991–2020 normal), clear skies and mezcal country. Tourists can be granted up to 180 days in Mexico, but the officer writes the days actually granted on the stamp, so check it (GOV.UK).

**Cost reality:** mid-range travellers in Oaxaca de Juárez spend about $56 per person per day (Budget Your Trip average) outside festival week, when lodging spikes.
**The trade-off:** for Día de Muertos itself, book as early as you can: Oaxaca sells out and prices spike for those specific nights. Arrive a few days after and you get the same city, quiet and cheap.

## 9. Buenos Aires & Argentina — Late Spring, Jacarandas Out

November is late spring in Argentina. Buenos Aires averages a 26.0 °C high and 16.1 °C low with 246 hours of sunshine, though it is not a dry month: 117.6 mm of rain falls on 7.2 days (1991–2020 normals). The jacarandas turn the avenues purple and the city is still full, before the summer exodus empties it in January. Día de la Soberanía Nacional moves to Monday 23 November in 2026, making a three-day weekend. British passport holders get 90 days visa-free (GOV.UK). It is shoulder season for Mendoza's wine country and the very start of the Patagonia season. Across the border in Chile, Torres del Paine's winter guide requirement ended on 15 September, but CONAF still requires confirmed reservations for the W and O circuits.

**Cost reality:** mid-range travellers in Buenos Aires spend about $55 per person per day (Budget Your Trip average, data updated 1 August 2026), but Argentina's prices move quickly with inflation, so treat any quoted budget as a snapshot. The old cash-for-blue-dollar trick is gone — as of September 2026 the street rate sits within a few percent of the official rate, so a fee-free card covers most spending; keep a little cash for small vendors and taxis.
**The trade-off:** Patagonia in November is early season. Torres del Paine is open year-round and the main refugios and campsites are already trading by November, but spring weather is volatile and famously windy, so treat any multi-day trek as weather-dependent and book huts early; from December the conditions are steadier. November is safest for the cities and the wine.

## 10. Cape Town, South Africa — Late Spring Before the Peak

November is the last month before Cape Town's December price surge: a 23.7 °C average high at the airport (1991–2020), nights at 13.4 °C, only 25.3 mm of rain and 302.8 hours of sunshine. The beaches and Winelands are full but not yet packed, and the peninsula's hiking is at its best. British passport holders get 90 days visa-free with 2 blank pages (GOV.UK); the Electronic Travel Authorisation launched on 12 August 2026 with a 500 rand fee stays voluntary for visa-exempt nationals (SAnews; VisasNews).

**Cost reality:** mid-range travellers in Cape Town spend about $188 per person per day (Budget Your Trip average); a rand-priced meal with local wine is a genuine bargain, though safaris add up separately.
**The trade-off:** the famous southeaster, the "Cape Doctor", starts blowing in late spring and can shut the Table Mountain cableway for days at a stretch, so keep the mountain flexible.

## 11. Vietnam's South & the Mekong — The Rainy Season Winds Down

As central Vietnam catches the last of its rains, the south flips. Ho Chi Minh City, the Mekong Delta, and Phu Quoc island are winding down their rainy season — still in the low 30s °C, but November is a transition month with showers on roughly a third of days, tapering sharply toward the dry season proper that settles in from December. Late-month trips get the best of it: the delta's floating markets are busy and the island's beaches are clearing up. The city's 1991–2020 normals, via the WMO, are a 32.4 °C high, a 24.9 °C low and 128 mm of rain on 9 days. British passport holders get 45 days visa-free; longer stays need the 90-day e-visa from evisa.gov.vn (GOV.UK).

**Cost reality:** mid-range travellers in Ho Chi Minh City spend about $88 per person per day (Budget Your Trip average); street food keeps the day cheap, and our [cheapest destinations in Asia guide](/blog/cheapest-destinations-in-asia) shows how to stretch it further.
**The trade-off:** central Vietnam (Hoi An, Hue, Da Nang) is the exception. It runs its wettest weeks in October and November and can flood, so don't build the classic north-to-south loop around it this month.

## 12. New Zealand's South Island — Late Spring, Empty Trails

Flip the hemisphere. November is late spring in New Zealand: days stretching toward fifteen hours by late month, snow still capping the Southern Alps, and the Great Walks (the Routeburn, the Kepler) reopening for the season without the December–January crush. Queenstown averages a 17.5 °C high and 6.2 °C low, 60.7 mm of rain on 7.7 days and 217.9 hours of sunshine (NIWA 1991–2020 normals), which makes it the coolest entry on this list. Queenstown, Wanaka and Fiordland are green and uncrowded. Visa-waiver visitors need an NZeTA before boarding (from NZD 17, valid 2 years; Immigration New Zealand says to allow 72 hours).

**Cost reality:** mid-range travellers in Queenstown spend about $131 per person per day (Budget Your Trip average); New Zealand is a long-haul splurge, but November undercuts the summer rates.
**The trade-off:** spring weather here is genuinely four-seasons-in-a-day. The Kepler and the Routeburn open for their booked season on 27 October and 1 November 2026 (DOC), but DOC notes avalanche risk can extend into December and may close alpine sections such as Luxmore–Iris Burn on the Kepler, so check the track alerts before committing to any multi-day hike.

## Practical Tips for November Travel

- **This is the value month, with one exception.** No Northern Hemisphere holiday anchors November apart from US Thanksgiving in its last week, so flight and hotel prices tend to soften between the autumn school holidays and December's spike. If you fly from the United States, avoid 23–27 November 2026: leave before it or stay abroad through it.
- **Follow the monsoon flip, not the brochure.** November splits Asia cleanly: Thailand's Andaman coast, Kerala, and southern Vietnam are drying out, while the Thai Gulf islands, Tamil Nadu, and central Vietnam catch rain. Our [monsoon-season guide](/blog/monsoon-season-where-to-go-and-avoid) maps the whole calendar so you land on the drier side.
- **The Middle East and North Africa are at their peak now.** Egypt, Jordan, Oman, and Morocco all switch from unbearable to ideal in November; pack layers for the desert nights.
- **Book the festival dates, wing the rest.** Three things sell out early this month: Oaxaca's Día de Muertos (1–2 November), [Diwali across India](https://www.drikpanchang.com/diwali/diwali-puja-calendar.html?year=2026) (8 November in 2026), and Thailand's lantern festivals around 24 November. Diwali week is a heavy domestic-travel period in India, so book internal flights early.
- **Compare it against peak season and you'll never go back.** The same latitudes that cost a fortune in [August](/blog/where-to-go-in-august) are calm and cheap now; see how the [2026 travel calendar](/blog/2026-travel-calendar) stacks up before you lock dates.

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### Where is the best place to go in November 2026?

For weather per dollar, Luxor, Kochi, Buenos Aires and Oaxaca are the cheapest bases on this list (figures in the cost question below). For a once-a-year experience, Oaxaca's Día de Muertos (Sunday 1 and Monday 2 November) and Thailand's lantern festivals around the full moon of Tuesday 24 November are hard to beat.

### Where is it warm in November?

Cross the equator or head to the desert. The hottest normals on this list are Kochi (32.6 °C average high), Ho Chi Minh City (32.4 °C) and Phuket (32.1 °C), all still with some rain; the desert belt is warm and dry, with Muscat at 30.3 °C and Luxor at 29.1 °C. South of the equator, Buenos Aires (26.0 °C) and Cape Town (23.7 °C) run late-spring days, while Queenstown at 17.5 °C is spring, not summer.

### Is November a cheap time to travel?

For flights and hotels in the Northern Hemisphere it tends to be a soft window; the exceptions are Thanksgiving week (23–27 November) for anyone flying from the United States and the few nights around Oaxaca's Día de Muertos. On the ground, Budget Your Trip's mid-range averages run from about $31 a day in Luxor and $40 in Kochi up to $188 in Cape Town and $301 country-wide in Oman, so the destination matters more than the month. [Plan your November trip](/trips/new) around the shoulder dates and the savings compound.

### Which destinations should I avoid in November?

Anywhere still catching a monsoon: Thailand's Gulf islands (Koh Samui, Koh Phangan), central Vietnam (Hoi An, Hue, which can flood), and India's Tamil Nadu coast. Patagonia is open, but November is early season with volatile, windy spring weather — go with flexible dates rather than a fixed trekking itinerary. The drier alternatives are the Thai Andaman coast (drying, not yet dry), southern Vietnam, and Kerala.

### When is Thanksgiving 2026, and which days are the worst to fly?

Thanksgiving is Thursday 26 November 2026, and the fare spike runs the week of 23–27 November (US Office of Personnel Management holiday dates), so those are the days to avoid. The weekends of 7–8, 14–15 and 21–22 November are the weekends to fly out on; if you are already abroad, stay through the holiday weekend and fly home the following week.

### Early November or late November: which is better?

In Southeast Asia and South India, later is drier: Phuket, Ho Chi Minh City and Kochi all taper toward a December dry season, and Thailand's lantern nights fall on Tuesday 24 November. In Europe the Christmas layer starts mid-month (Vienna's Rathausplatz market opens on Friday 13 November) and Tromsø's polar night begins on Friday 27 November. The Middle East, Oaxaca after the 1–2 November festival, and the Southern Hemisphere are good all month.

### Where should I go for one week only?

Pick a single base rather than a loop. From Europe, Madeira and Marrakech are short flights with settled November normals. From North America, Oaxaca fills a week on its own, and a Luxor-and-Aswan week covers the Nile without a Cairo add-on. Save Thailand, Vietnam and New Zealand for two weeks or more.

### Is Europe worth it in November?

Yes, for two kinds of trip. Madeira is the one European entry on this list with a beach-and-hiking climate: a 22.8 °C average high and 80.3 mm of rain across 7.3 days. Mainland Europe is a city month: Vienna's Christkindlmarkt runs from Friday 13 November to Saturday 26 December. It is not yet a mainstream ski month: Ski Arlberg, for one, opens for winter on Wednesday 2 December 2026.
