# Carnival 2027 in Spain: Dates for Cádiz, Tenerife, Las Palmas, Badajoz and More

Carnival Tuesday (Martes de Carnaval) falls on 9 February 2027 and Ash Wednesday on 10 February, so carnival week runs from Thursday 4 to Wednesday 10 February. Cádiz carries on to 14 February and Badajoz runs from 5 to 14 February. The two big Canary carnivals run later: Santa Cruz de Tenerife's street carnival is on 19–28 February, and Las Palmas de Gran Canaria's programme goes on to 28 February.

This guide gives each carnival's 2027 dates as its organiser or town hall publishes them, the local holidays from the official bulletins, and what to book first. Where a 2027 date isn't announced, we say so and give the 2026 pattern. Every page was read on 20 November 2026. Prices are what each source showed that day, mostly for 2026. Times are local: mainland Spain is on CET, and the Canary Islands are one hour behind.

## When is Carnival 2027 in Spain?

Carnival hangs on Easter, which falls on Sunday 28 March 2027: the [BOE](https://www.boe.es/diario_boe/txt.php?id=BOE-A-2026-21254) lists Good Friday on 26 March and Easter Monday on 29 March. The days that matter:

- **Thursday 4 February:** Jueves Lardero, or Dijous Gras in Catalan. Sitges and Solsona start that day.
- **Friday 5 to Tuesday 9 February:** the long carnival weekend. Ciudad Rodrigo's carnival runs exactly these days, and Badajoz starts on the Friday.
- **Monday 8 February:** carnival Monday, a local holiday in Cádiz and Vilanova i la Geltrú.
- **Tuesday 9 February:** Martes de Carnaval. The Canary Islands' [official holiday order](https://www.gobiernodecanarias.org/boc/2026/168/3035.html) names it on that date.
- **Wednesday 10 February:** Ash Wednesday, on a [2027 church calendar](https://sdcatholic.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/DiocesanLitCal27_ENES_Esp.pdf). The Entierro de la Sardina in Madrid is listed that day.
- **Sunday 14 February:** the Sunday after, which closes Cádiz and Badajoz.

Not every town follows the church calendar. Santa Cruz de Tenerife holds its street carnival after Ash Wednesday, from 19 to 28 February, and Murcia's sardine burial waits until after Easter.

## Is carnival a public holiday in Spain in 2027?

Not nationally or regionally. The BOE list of 2027 national and regional holidays has no carnival day. Carnival holidays are local: every town has two paid local holidays a year, proposed by its council and published in the regional bulletin.

- **Canary Islands:** 40 of the 88 municipalities list Martes de Carnaval in the [BOC](https://www.gobiernodecanarias.org/boc/2026/168/3035.html) of 21 August 2026, our count of the list. Most take 9 February, including Las Palmas de Gran Canaria, Arrecife and Santa Cruz de La Palma. Santa Cruz de Tenerife takes Tuesday 23 February, its Coso day, as do Arafo, Candelaria, El Sauzal, Icod de los Vinos, La Victoria de Acentejo and Santa Úrsula, and El Rosario takes the same day as Santa Cruz's holiday. Puerto del Rosario, on Fuerteventura, takes 16 February.
- **La Laguna:** the BOC lists 9 February, but in October the council moved its holiday to 23 February to match Santa Cruz (reported by [RTVC](https://rtvc.es/ayuntamiento-la-laguna-cambia-fecha-martes-carnaval-2027/)). We found no corrected BOC entry yet.
- **Cádiz:** Monday 8 February and 7 October, in the [BOJA](https://www.juntadeandalucia.es/boja/2026/210/25) of 29 October 2026.
- **Águilas:** Tuesday 9 February and Friday 30 April, in the [BORM](https://www.borm.es/services/anuncio/844400/pdf) of 27 July 2026.
- **Vilanova i la Geltrú:** the [council](https://www.vilanova.cat/calendaris_municipals) has set Monday 8 February. Catalonia's official list of 2027 local holidays wasn't out when we checked, so Sitges and Solsona are unknown.
- **Badajoz:** Tuesday 9 February and 24 June, in the [DOE](https://doe.juntaex.es/pdfs/doe/2026/2070o/26062650.pdf) of 27 October 2026.

## When is the Entierro de la Sardina in 2027?

The burial of the sardine is a mock funeral that usually closes carnival. Each town picks its own day, and Murcia's isn't in carnival at all.

- **Madrid:** listed for Ash Wednesday, 10 February, by the city's [tourist office](https://www.esmadrid.com/node/82635), which will confirm the programme at the end of January. In the morning a parade runs from 11:00 to 15:00 through the old Habsburg quarter to Plaza Mayor. In the evening, from 18:00 to 21:00, the procession with giants and big-heads goes from the Ermita de San Antonio de la Florida to the Fuente del Pajarito in Casa de Campo, where the sardine is buried, with a bonfire at about 21:00. It's free. Madrid's carnival parade is listed for Saturday 6 February on [Madrid Río](https://www.esmadrid.com/node/82625), also to be confirmed.
- **Santa Cruz de Tenerife:** Wednesday 24 February at 21:30, from calle Juan Pablo II to Plaza de España, with the sardine burned on Avenida Marítima ([programme](https://carnavaldetenerife.com/programa-carnaval/)).
- **Las Palmas de Gran Canaria:** Sunday 28 February, on the organiser's provisional calendar.
- **Badajoz:** in 2026 the sardine parade ran on carnival Tuesday in the San Roque district ([carnival site](https://carnavaldebadajoz.org/)). The 2027 date isn't set.
- **Murcia:** Saturday 3 April 2027, after Easter, as the close of the Fiestas de Primavera ([Murcia tourist board](https://itrem.carm.es/en/event/entierro-de-la-sardina-M424175/)). The will of Doña Sardina is read from the town hall balcony the night before. If you're planning that week, see [where to go at Easter](/blog/where-to-go-at-easter).

## When is the Cádiz carnival in 2027?

From Thursday 4 to Sunday 14 February 2027, according to the [Ayuntamiento de Cádiz](https://institucional.cadiz.es/area/Carnaval/103).

**What it is:** Cádiz's carnival is sung. The official contest, the COAC (Concurso Oficial de Agrupaciones Carnavalescas), fills the Gran Teatro Falla with four kinds of group: coros, comparsas, chirigotas and cuartetos, each with a new repertoire of tangos, pasodobles, cuplés or parodies every year ([contest rules](https://institucional.cadiz.es/sites/default/files/areamunicipal/documentos/BASES%20COAC%20PERSONAS%20ADULTAS%202027.pdf)). Then the city's streets and squares take over. The "ilegales", groups of friends who sing outside the contest, gather small crowds mainly in the Barrio de la Viña ([Ayuntamiento](https://institucional.cadiz.es/area/El-Carnaval-en-la-Calle/192)).

**The contest in 2027:** preliminaries from 8 to 21 January, quarter-finals from 24 to 29 January, semi-finals from 1 to 3 February and the Gran Final on Friday 5 February ([Ayuntamiento de Cádiz](https://transparencia.cadiz.es/el-coro-un-coro-increible-abrira-el-coac-2027-el-proximo-8-de-enero/)). At most 16 groups reach the final, four per category.

**The days that matter:** the final on Friday 5 February, the first weekend (6 and 7 February), Monday 8 February (the local holiday) and the closing Sunday, 14 February.

**Tickets:** 2027 prices and sale dates aren't out. For the [2026 final](https://transparencia.cadiz.es/las-entradas-para-la-gran-final-del-coac-2026-salen-hoy-jueves-a-la-venta/) the council charged €45 in the top gallery (paraíso), €70 in the amphitheatre, €80 in the second-tier boxes and front amphitheatre, and €95 in the stalls and lower boxes. Seats went on sale on 15 January at the town hall box office first, then online on Bacantix. Each ticket is in the name of the person attending and is checked against their DNI (Spanish ID card), two per person at the box office, with no changes or refunds. The 2025 final sold out in about three hours (reported by [Onda Cádiz](https://ondacadiz.es/noticias/2025-02/agotadas-en-tres-horas-las-entradas-para-la-gran-final-del-coac-2025), the council's broadcaster).

**Getting there:** from Jerez airport, Renfe's C-1 local train reaches Cádiz in 57 minutes, and Seville–Cádiz trains stop there too ([Aena](https://www.aena.es/en/jerez/getting-there/trains.html)). The C-1 also serves El Puerto de Santa María, Puerto Real and San Fernando.

**Where to stay:** we found no published hotel figures for carnival in Cádiz. Our advice: book the first weekend now, and look at the towns on the C-1 line if the old town is full.

**Crowds and safety:** in 2026 more than 700 officers from the national, civil and local police covered the two weekends, about 900 coaches were expected, and night buses ran on both Saturdays (reported by [Vivir Ediciones](https://vivirediciones.es/?p=972044), from the local security board).

**Weather:** February averages 16.8°C (62°F) by day and 10.7°C (51°F) at night, with rain on about six days ([AEMET](https://www.aemet.es/es/serviciosclimaticos/datosclimatologicos/valoresclimatologicos?l=5973&k=and), 1981–2010 averages).

**The trade-off:** the jokes are in fast Andalusian Spanish full of local references, and the final sells out within hours, in person first, with every ticket in the attendee's name and checked against their ID.

## When is the Tenerife carnival in 2027?

The programme opens on 22 January and the street carnival in Santa Cruz de Tenerife runs from Friday 19 to Sunday 28 February 2027 ([official programme](https://carnavaldetenerife.com/programa-carnaval/)).

**Theme:** "Roma: El imperio del Carnaval", according to the [official site](https://www.carnavaldetenerife.com/). The press reports it as "Roma Eterna: El Imperio del Carnaval" ([RTVC](https://rtvc.es/tema-carnaval-santa-cruz-de-tenerife-2027-roma-eterna/)).

**The 2027 programme, in Canary time:**

| Date | Event |
|---|---|
| Fri 22 Jan, 22:00 | Opening, Plaza de España |
| 1–4 Feb, 20:30 | Adult murga contest heats, Recinto Ferial |
| Sat 6 Feb, 20:00 | Murga final, Recinto Ferial |
| Sat 13 Feb, 20:30 | Comparsas contest, Recinto Ferial |
| Wed 17 Feb, 21:30 | Queen election gala, Recinto Ferial |
| Fri 19 Feb, 19:30 | Cabalgata anunciadora (opening parade) |
| Sat 20 Feb, 21:00 | Ritmo y Armonía, all the comparsas, Avenida Francisco La Roche |
| Sun 21 Feb | First Carnaval de Día (daytime street party) |
| Tue 23 Feb, 16:00 | Coso Apoteósico parade, from Avenida de la Constitución along Francisco La Roche and Marítima |
| Wed 24 Feb, 21:30 | Entierro de la Sardina |
| Sat 27 Feb | Second Carnaval de Día |
| Sun 28 Feb, 20:00 | Closing fireworks, Plaza de La Candelaria |

**Holiday:** Tuesday 23 February is Santa Cruz's local holiday ([BOC](https://www.gobiernodecanarias.org/boc/2026/168/3035.html)).

**Tickets:** parades, concerts and street parties are free, but the Queen gala needs a ticket bought in advance, and they usually sell out fast ([official FAQ](https://www.carnavaldetenerife.com/)). In 2026 a Queen gala seat cost €15, a murga heat €8, the murga final €12 and the comparsas contest €10, sold at entradascarnavaltenerife.com from 21 January (the Queen gala from 23 January), four per bank card per event (reported by [RTVC](https://rtvc.es/fechas-venta-precio-entradas-carnaval-santa-cruz-tenerife-14-enero-2026/)). The 4,191 Queen gala seats sold out in about half an hour ([RTVC/EFE](https://rtvc.es/venta-entradas-de-la-gala-de-la-reina-de-santa-cruz-23-enero-2026/)).

**Getting there:** from Tenerife North airport, TITSA line 20 runs to Santa Cruz for €2.65 ([Aena](https://www.aena.es/en/tenerife-norte-ciudad-de-la-laguna/how-to-get-here/bus.html)). From Tenerife South, take line 10 by day or 711 at night ([Aena](https://www.aena.es/en/tenerife-sur/getting-there/bus.html)). In 2026 the tram ran double units and carried 458,129 passengers over carnival; its busiest day was carnival Friday, with 89,500 ([Metrotenerife](https://metrotenerife.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/02/nota-metrotenerife-carnaval-balance-demanda.pdf)).

**Where to stay:** in 2026 the association's hotels in Santa Cruz averaged 83% occupancy over carnival and 86% on the first long weekend. The final figure was 16 points higher than bookings a little over a week before the start, thanks to last-minute guests (survey by [Ashotel](https://ashotel.es/wp-content/uploads/2026/02/25022026-balance-ocupacion-Carnaval-2026.pdf), the hotel association). So rooms do turn up late, but we wouldn't count on it for the Coso weekend.

**Crowds:** the council counted more than a million participants across the 2026 programme, and its carnival hospital treated 676 people (reported by [RTVC/EFE](https://rtvc.es/mas-de-un-millon-de-personas-participaron-en-el-carnaval-de-santa-cruz-2-marzo-2026/)).

**Weather:** 21.2°C (70°F) by day and 15.3°C (60°F) at night in February, with rain on about four days ([AEMET](https://www.aemet.es/es/serviciosclimaticos/datosclimatologicos/valoresclimatologicos?l=C449C&k=coo), 1981–2010 averages).

**The trade-off:** the 2027 street carnival comes after Ash Wednesday, so it doesn't line up with carnival weekend anywhere else in Spain, and the Queen gala sold out in about half an hour in 2026.

## When is the Las Palmas carnival in 2027?

From 22 January to 28 February 2027, on the provisional calendar the organiser published in June ([preview PDF](https://lpacarnaval.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/avance-carnaval-2027.pdf)). The theme is music, chosen in a public vote ([LPA Carnaval](https://lpacarnaval.com/en/category/news)). The carnival [turned 50 in 2026](https://lpacarnaval.com/doce-finalistas-se-disputaran-la-banda-del-drag-queen-del-50-o-aniversario-del-carnaval-de-las-palmas-de-gran-canaria/).

**The provisional calendar:**

- **22 January:** opening parade and pregón.
- **25–28 January:** murga heats; final on Saturday 30 January.
- **Friday 5 February:** Gran Dama gala.
- **Monday 8 February:** Queen gala, on the eve of the holiday.
- **Tuesday 9 February:** Carnaval de Día and the children's parade.
- **Friday 12 February:** Drag preselection.
- **Saturday 13 February:** Carnaval de Día, with a street parade from the Estadio Insular.
- **Friday 19 February:** Drag Queen gala.
- **Saturday 20 February:** Gran Cabalgata, the main parade.
- **Saturday 27 February:** a night of street carnival on the organiser's [current calendar](https://lpacarnaval.com/); the June preview also lists a Carnaval de Día.
- **Sunday 28 February:** Carnaval de Día and the Entierro de la Sardina.

**Holiday:** Tuesday 9 February ([BOC](https://www.gobiernodecanarias.org/boc/2026/168/3035.html)).

**Tickets:** in 2026 only three events charged: the Drag preselection (€10), the Queen gala (€10) and the Drag Queen gala (€15), four per person, online or at the box office. Everything else was free until full ([organiser](https://lpacarnaval.com/en/tickets/)). Sales opened on 26, 27 and 29 January. The 2026 Drag Queen gala, in Parque Santa Catalina, sold out online four minutes after sales opened ([organiser](https://lpacarnaval.com/roberto-herrera-y-yanely-hernandez-se-ponen-al-frente-de-la-gala-mas-iconica-del-carnaval-de-las-palmas-de-gran-canaria/)).

**Getting there:** Global's line 60 links Gran Canaria airport with San Telmo (€2.30) and Santa Catalina (€2.95), with two buses an hour in the daytime ([Aena](https://www.aena.es/en/gran-canaria/getting-there/bus.html)).

**Weather:** 21.2°C (70°F) by day and 15.0°C (59°F) at night, with rain on about three days in February ([AEMET](https://www.aemet.es/es/serviciosclimaticos/datosclimatologicos/valoresclimatologicos?l=C649I&k=coo), 1981–2010 averages).

**The trade-off:** the calendar is a June preview that can still change, and the ticket everyone wants, the Drag Queen gala, goes in minutes.

## When is the Badajoz carnival in 2027?

From Friday 5 to Sunday 14 February 2027, set by the [Ayuntamiento de Badajoz](https://carnavaldebadajoz.org/2026/11/16/convocatoria-y-bases-de-los-concursos-del-carnaval-de-badajoz-2027/).

**What it is:** a carnival declared of International Tourist Interest, with more than 40 years of history and many visitors from neighbouring Portugal ([carnival site](https://carnavaldebadajoz.org/)). Its heart is the official murga contest (COMBA) at the Teatro López de Ayala, followed by the Gran Desfile of comparsas.

**Contest dates:** not announced, and the 2027 contest rules don't give them. In 2026 the heats ran from 3 to 6 February, the semi-finals on 9 and 10 February, and the final on the opening Friday, 13 February, after the pregón ([carnival site](https://carnavaldebadajoz.org/2026/02/08/las-semifinales-del-comba-el-lunes-y-el-martes-la-gran-final-el-viernes/)).

**New in 2027:** the Gran Desfile gets a new route, from the Colegio FEC Sagrada Familia on Avenida de Santa Marina to the junction of Avenida de Pardaleras and calle Cordero, about 1,030 m (0.6 mi), because of car park works ([council](https://carnavaldebadajoz.org/2026/11/13/el-gran-desfile-del-carnaval-de-badajoz-estrenara-recorrido-en-2027/)). Its date isn't announced.

**Holiday:** Tuesday 9 February ([DOE](https://doe.juntaex.es/pdfs/doe/2026/2070o/26062650.pdf)).

**Getting there:** Badajoz airport has flights to Madrid and Barcelona with Air Nostrum and to Gran Canaria with Binter ([Aena](https://www.aena.es/en/badajoz/airlines-and-destinations/airport-destinations.html)).

**Weather:** 16.1°C (61°F) by day but 4.5°C (40°F) at night, with rain on about six days in February ([AEMET](https://www.aemet.es/es/serviciosclimaticos/datosclimatologicos/valoresclimatologicos?l=4452&k=ext), 1981–2010 averages).

**The trade-off:** the contest and parade dates come late, the murga lyrics are full of local references, and parade nights are cold.

## Águilas: 30 January–20 February 2027

**What it is:** a Fiesta de Interés Turístico Internacional on the Murcia coast ([Murcia tourist board](https://itrem.carm.es/en/event/carnaval-de-aguilas-M424133/)). Its characters are Don Carnal, Doña Cuaresma, the Musa and the Mussona, a wild beast released from the dungeons of the Castillo de San Juan de las Águilas and led to Plaza de España to open the carnival ([organiser](https://www.carnavaldeaguilas.org/suelta-de-la-mussona.php)). The drink is cuerva, a punch of wine and fruit.

**2027 dates:** 30 January to 20 February ([organiser](https://www.carnavaldeaguilas.org/)). On 20 November the programme and the sale of chairs and boxes for the parades were both marked "coming soon". Spain's tourist board describes four big parades, on carnival Sunday, Tuesday and Friday and the Sábado de Piñata, and a night carnival around Plaza de España, above all on carnival Monday ([Turespaña](https://www.spain.info/es/agenda/fiestas-carnaval-aguilas/)).

**Holiday:** Tuesday 9 February ([BORM](https://www.borm.es/services/anuncio/844400/pdf)).

**Getting there:** Renfe's C-2 line from Murcia ends in Águilas, but Renfe has run it by road between Murcia, Lorca and Águilas since July 2022 because of track works, with no end date given ([Renfe](https://grupo.renfe.com/es/es/sala-de-prensa/noticias/2025/09/plan-transporte-c2-murcia)). Check before you travel.

**The trade-off:** the parade days and seat sales aren't out yet, and public transport means a replacement bus.

## Sitges: 4–10 February 2027

**What it is:** a town carnival on a city scale. For 2026 the council expected 250,000 visitors and about 3,000 participants, and deployed about 1,000 police, emergency and private security staff, the second-largest operation in Catalonia after the Mobile World Congress ([Ajuntament de Sitges](https://www.sitges.cat/actualitat/noticies/sitges-presenta-el-dispositiu-extraordinari-de-seguretat-pel-carnaval-2026)).

**2027 dates:** 4 to 10 February on the town's [tourism site](https://www.sitgesanytime.com/en/calendar/pln330/sitges-carnival). Parade days and times aren't out. In 2026 the Rua de la Disbauxa ran on carnival Sunday with about 2,200 participants, and the Rua de l'Extermini on carnival Tuesday at 21:00 ([council](https://www.sitges.cat/serveis/festes/actualitat/noticies/mes-de-2-000-persones-participen-a-la-rua-de-la-disbauxa)). The Extermini had 36 groups on 38 floats, and the burial of the carnival king followed on Ash Wednesday, from 18:30 at the town hall ([council](https://www.sitges.cat/serveis/festes/actualitat/noticies/la-rua-de-l2019extermini-remata-un-carnaval-de-sitges-2026-amb-gran-participacio)).

**Getting there:** Rodalies line R2 Sud runs from Barcelona (Estació de França, Passeig de Gràcia and Sants) to Sitges and Vilanova ([Rodalies](https://rodalies.gencat.cat/en/sobre-rodalies/linies-i-estacions/servei_rodalia_barcelona/r2s/index.html)). In 2026 Renfe added special trains with room for 45,000 travellers.

**Weather:** at Barcelona airport, the nearest long-term station, February averages 14.3°C (58°F) by day and 5.4°C (42°F) at night ([AEMET](https://www.aemet.es/es/serviciosclimaticos/datosclimatologicos/valoresclimatologicos?l=0076&k=cat), 1981–2010 averages).

**The trade-off:** crowds. Over the 2026 carnival weekend, emergency services handled 21 callouts and one robbery with intimidation was reported.

## Vilanova i la Geltrú: dates not announced

**What it is:** a week of carnival that keeps traditions going back 300 years, according to the province's [tourist board](https://www.barcelonaesmoltmes.cat/en/-/carnaval-de-vilanova-i-la-geltru). King Carnestoltes arrives on the Friday and gives his sermon in Plaça de la Vila.

**2027 dates:** not announced, but the council has made Monday 8 February a local holiday. In 2026 the week ran from Thursday 12 to Wednesday 18 February: the Xatonada on Thursday night, the Arrivo on Friday, the Caramel and the Moixó Foguer on Saturday, Les Comparses on Sunday, the Ball del Paperet on Monday, the Vidalot on Tuesday and the Enterro on Wednesday ([council](https://www.vilanova.cat/noticies/detall?id=99998265)).

**Les Comparses:** in 2026 the adult "wars" of dancing couples started at 12:00 in Plaça de la Vila, with 9,119 adult couples registered and about 11,170 in all. Places in the adult wars were given by coloured wristbands allocated by draw ([council](https://www.vilanova.cat/noticies/detall?id=99998251)).

**Getting there:** the R2 Sud, as for Sitges. On weekdays, trains between Vilanova and Estació de França run every 10 minutes at peak and every 15 off-peak ([Rodalies](https://rodalies.gencat.cat/en/sobre-rodalies/linies-i-estacions/servei_rodalia_barcelona/r2s/index.html)).

**The trade-off:** the 2027 programme isn't out, and dancing in Les Comparses isn't a walk-up: in 2026, places in the adult wars came by wristband, allocated by draw.

## Solsona: 4–10 February 2027

**What it is:** a carnival built on mockery, run by the town's carnival association, which says Solsona was the first town in Spain to revive carnival, in 1971 ([organiser](https://carnavalsolsona.com/la-festa/historia/)). Its best-known act is the hanging of a cardboard donkey from the bell tower on carnival Saturday, which turns the old nickname for the townspeople, "matarrucs" (donkey-killers), into a joke ([organiser](https://carnavalsolsona.com/la-festa/el-ruc/)).

**2027 dates:** 4 to 10 February on the [organiser's site](http://carnavalsolsona.com/). The programme page was still empty on 20 November.

**Getting there:** Alsa buses run Barcelona–Manresa–Solsona and stop on Passeig Guitard ([Alsa](https://www.alsa.com/en/coach/manresa-solsona)).

**Where to stay:** the organiser's own list has two places to sleep: Càmping El Solsonès and Hotel Sant Roc ([organiser](https://carnavalsolsona.com/menjar-i-dormir/)).

**The trade-off:** getting there means a bus via Manresa, the organiser lists just two places to sleep, and the humour is in Catalan.

## Ciudad Rodrigo: Carnaval del Toro, 5–9 February 2027

**What it is:** a carnival built around bulls: encierros (bull runs), some on horseback, capeas (amateur bullfighting), desencierros and novilladas (bullfights with young bulls). Spain's tourist board lists it as a Fiesta de Interés Turístico Nacional ([Turespaña](https://www.spain.info/es/agenda/fiestas-tradicionales-carnaval-toro/)).

**2027 dates:** Friday 5 to Tuesday 9 February, set by the town hall in its [poster contest rules](https://www.ciudadrodrigo.es/ayuntamiento/wp-content/uploads/2026/07/2026_8_BASES-XLV-CONCURSO-DE-CARTELES-CARNAVAL-DEL-TORO-2027.pdf). The programme isn't out. In 2026 it ran from 13 to 17 February ([town hall](https://www.ciudadrodrigo.es/ayuntamiento/wp-content/uploads/2026/01/CALENDARIO-Ferias-y-Fiestas-locales-2026.pdf)).

**Getting there:** buses link Salamanca and Ciudad Rodrigo, about 85 km (53 mi) apart, in a little over an hour (reported by [Omio](https://www.omio.es/viajes/salamanca/ciudad-rodrigo), a booking site).

**Weather:** Salamanca airport averages 11.2°C (52°F) by day and −0.2°C (32°F) at night in February, with about 16 frost days ([AEMET](https://www.aemet.es/es/serviciosclimaticos/datosclimatologicos/valoresclimatologicos?l=2867&k=cle), 1981–2010 averages).

**The trade-off:** the bulls are the point, so skip it if bull festivities bother you; running with them is dangerous; and the nights are freezing.

## Can you see two carnivals in one trip?

Yes, because the dates spread out in 2027:

- **Cádiz, then Tenerife:** Cádiz ends on 14 February and Santa Cruz's street carnival starts on 19 February.
- **Sitges and Solsona:** both run 4–10 February in Catalonia. Vilanova's dates aren't out, but its holiday falls in the same week.
- **Badajoz and Ciudad Rodrigo:** both start on Friday 5 February.
- **Las Palmas and Santa Cruz:** on neighbouring islands, Las Palmas's Gran Cabalgata (Saturday 20 February) falls the day before Santa Cruz's first Carnaval de Día (Sunday 21 February).

For carnivals outside Spain, see our [carnival guide for 2027](/blog/carnival-around-the-world), and for other ideas that month, [where to go in February](/blog/where-to-go-in-february).

## How to plan a long weekend around a carnival

**Cádiz, Friday 5 to Tuesday 9 February.** Fly into Jerez and take the C-1. Friday is the COAC final at the Falla, if you have a ticket. Spend Saturday and Sunday on the street carnival and the ilegales in La Viña. Monday is the local holiday, and Tuesday is carnival Tuesday itself.

**Santa Cruz de Tenerife, Friday 19 to Wednesday 24 February.** Friday's opening parade starts at 19:30. On Saturday, Ritmo y Armonía brings every comparsa to Avenida Francisco La Roche at 21:00. Sunday is the first Carnaval de Día, and Monday is another night of street carnival. Tuesday is the Coso from 16:00, and the Entierro de la Sardina follows on Wednesday at 21:30.

You can lay out the days [in the planner](/trips/new), move them around by chatting and share the plan with your group. It plans the days; it doesn't sell tickets or rooms.

## What to book first, and by when

No authority sets booking windows, so this is our advice.

1. **Now:** rooms for the first weekend in Cádiz (5–8 February) and in Sitges, and for the Coso weekend in Santa Cruz (19–23 February).
2. **January:** watch the official ticket channels. In 2026 the Cádiz final went on sale on 15 January, Tenerife's tickets from 21 January and the Las Palmas galas from 26 January.
3. **As soon as dates are fixed:** flights and trains. For Cádiz, consider a bed along the C-1 line.
4. **Before you go:** warm layers for night parades in Badajoz and Ciudad Rodrigo, and a costume for anywhere.

## Frequently asked questions

### When is carnival 2027 in Spain?

Carnival Tuesday is 9 February 2027 and Ash Wednesday is 10 February, so carnival week runs from Thursday 4 to Wednesday 10 February. Cádiz runs to 14 February, Santa Cruz de Tenerife holds its street carnival from 19 to 28 February, and Las Palmas's provisional programme runs to 28 February.

### Is Carnival Tuesday a holiday in Spain in 2027?

Only locally. The 2027 national and regional list has no carnival day. In the Canary Islands, 40 municipalities list Martes de Carnaval, most on 9 February and Santa Cruz de Tenerife on 23 February. Cádiz and Vilanova i la Geltrú take Monday 8 February, and Águilas and Badajoz take Tuesday 9.

### When is the COAC 2027 final in Cádiz?

On Friday 5 February 2027 at the Gran Teatro Falla, according to the council's calendar. The carnival itself runs from 4 to 14 February.

### What is the theme of the 2027 Tenerife carnival?

"Roma: El imperio del Carnaval" on the official site, reported in the press as "Roma Eterna: El Imperio del Carnaval".

### When is the Las Palmas carnival 2027?

From 22 January to 28 February, on the organiser's provisional calendar. The Drag Queen gala is planned for Friday 19 February and the Gran Cabalgata for Saturday 20 February.

### When is the Badajoz carnival 2027?

From 5 to 14 February 2027. The murga contest and parade dates aren't announced yet.

### When is the Entierro de la Sardina in 2027?

In Madrid it's listed for 10 February, in Santa Cruz de Tenerife it's on 24 February, in Las Palmas on 28 February, and in Murcia on 3 April, after Easter.
