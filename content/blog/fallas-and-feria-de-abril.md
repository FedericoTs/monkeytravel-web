# Fallas and Seville's April Fair 2027: Dates, What to See and How to Go

Fallas 2027 ends with the cremà on the night of Friday 19 March, Saint Joseph's day and a public holiday in the Valencian Community. The big days start on 15 March: the fallas go up on 15 and 16 March, the flower offering takes 17 and 18 March and the Nit del Foc fireworks fill the night of Thursday 18 March, while the daily mascletà shakes the Plaza del Ayuntamiento from 1 March (at 14:00 in 2026). Seville hasn't announced its April Fair yet, but it has made Wednesday 14 April a local holiday as "the Wednesday of the Feria", which, with the Monday-to-Sunday format used in 2025 and 2026, points to the alumbrado at midnight on Monday 12 April and the Feria from Tuesday 13 to Sunday 18 April.

Holy Week sits between the two, from Palm Sunday, 21 March, to Easter Sunday, 28 March, and fills Seville too.

Every date, time and price below was read on the organiser's, council's or operator's own page on 21 November 2026. The 2027 Fallas programme isn't published yet, so where a 2027 time isn't announced we give 2026's and say so. Press and independent figures are marked. Times are Spanish local time: CET (UTC+1) during Fallas, then CEST (UTC+2) from Sunday 28 March, when the clocks go forward ([EU summer-time directive](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/TXT/?uri=CELEX:32000L0084)).

## When is Fallas 2027?

The cremà is fixed: 19 March, Saint Joseph's day, a Friday in 2027. Around it, the city council and the Junta Central Fallera (JCF), the body that runs the festival, have settled some of the 2027 days. The full programme with times comes later: the 2026 one was only approved by the falla presidents on [8 January 2026](https://fallas.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/01/C6480_2026-01-08-Acta-asamblea-extraordinaria-presidentes.pdf).

| 2027 | What happens | 2027 status | 2026 time and place |
|---|---|---|---|
| Late Feb | La Crida, the call that opens the festival | Not announced | Sun 22 Feb, the last Sunday of the month, 19:30, Torres de Serranos |
| From Mon 1 Mar | Mascletà every day | Times not out | 14:00, Plaza del Ayuntamiento, 1–19 Mar |
| Mon 15 Mar | Plantà of the children's fallas | Set out by the council (reported) | 09:00 |
| Tue 16 Mar | Plantà of all the fallas | Set out by the council (reported) | 08:00 |
| Wed 17 and Thu 18 Mar | Ofrenda, the flower offering to the Virgin | Confirmed after a July vote | From 15:30 |
| Night of Thu 18 Mar | Nit del Foc, the biggest firework display | Set out by the council (reported) | 23:59, Puente de Monteolivete |
| Fri 19 Mar | Cabalgata del Fuego and cremà | Fixed | 19:00 parade; fallas burn from 20:00 to 23:00 |

The 2026 times are from the [official programme as published by Visit València](https://www.visitvalencia.com/agenda-valencia/programa-fallas-2026), the city's tourism foundation. The 2027 plantà and Nit del Foc dates come from the council's outline of its 2027 Fallas rules, the Bando, which is still being drafted; the same report's calendar shows the usual daily mascletàs from 1 to 19 March (reported by [OfficialPress](https://officialpress.es/fallas-2027/)). The council has also said marquees can go up in the streets from 11 March, that street parties can have live orchestras on 13 and 15 to 18 March, and that mojito stalls are banned ([Ajuntament de València](https://www.valencia.es/val/actualitat/-/content/fallas-puestos-venta-mojitos-2027-asamblea-presidentes)).

The Ofrenda stays on two afternoons. The JCF proposed opening it on the afternoon of 16 March so that the last days wouldn't finish so late; on 21 July 2026 the proposal won 203 of the 327 votes at the falla presidents' assembly, short of the two thirds (218) the rules require (reported by [El Debate](https://www.eldebate.com/espana/comunidad-valenciana/20260721/como-queda-ofrenda-fallas-valencia-2027-sorprendente-votacion_442187.html)). The Fallera Mayor Infantil closes the first day and the Fallera Mayor de València the second.

Once you know which days you want, [lay out your Fallas days](/trips/new) in the planner and share them with whoever's coming.

## Is 19 March 2027 a holiday in Valencia?

Yes. San José, Friday 19 March 2027, is on the official list of 2027 holidays for the Valencian Community, and also for Galicia, Madrid, Murcia and Navarre ([BOE](https://www.boe.es/diario_boe/txt.php?id=BOE-A-2026-21254)). That makes 19 to 21 March a long weekend in all five regions, running straight into Holy Week, which starts on Palm Sunday, the 21st.

**What it does to crowds.** In 2026, when 19 March was a Thursday, the busiest hotel night in Valencia was the eve of the holiday, 18 to 19 March, 76.7% booked a week before ([Visit València survey, via the Ajuntament](https://www.valencia.es/cas/actualidad/-/content/ocupaci%C3%B3n-hotelera-val%C3%A8ncia-fallas)). In 2027 that eve is a Thursday night before a three-day weekend, so we'd expect more Spanish visitors on 18 and 19 March, and we'd book those nights first.

**Holy Week is already close.** In 2027, 19 March is also Viernes de Dolor, the Friday before Palm Sunday. The 31 brotherhoods of Valencia's seaside Holy Week, the Semana Santa Marinera, have decided not to hold processions that day, leaving the city to the cremà, and plan more than 80 processions from 20 to 28 March (reported by [esdiario](https://www.esdiario.com/comunidad-valenciana/valencia/261015/200342/semana-santa-marinera-valencia-2027-sin-procesiones-19-marzo-crema-fallas.html)).

## What to see at Fallas, day by day

### The mascletà, every day at 14:00

A mascletà is a daytime firework show built for rhythm and noise rather than colour. In 2026 one was scheduled in the Plaza del Ayuntamiento at 14:00 every day from 1 to 19 March ([Visit València](https://www.visitvalencia.com/en/fallas)): 19 in all, together 3,465 kg of powder ([Ajuntament de València](https://www.valencia.es/cas/actualidad/-/content/toneladas-de-p%C3%B3lvora-fallas-val%C3%A8ncia)). One was called off for the weather and moved to a night mascletà on 2 May ([Ajuntament](https://www.valencia.es/val/actualitat/-/content/fallas-puestos-venta-mojitos-2027-asamblea-presidentes)).

In 2026 the two metro stations next to the square, Xàtiva and Colón, closed from 12:30 to 14:30 from 14 to 19 March, and Metrovalencia sent people to Alameda, Plaça Espanya or Àngel Guimerà instead (reported by [Europa Press](https://www.ultimahora.es/noticias/comunidades/2026/03/10/2586109/metrovalencia-ofrecera-dias-servicio-ininterrumpido-fallas-prevision-alcanzar-millones-viajeros.html)). On the same days, C1 and C2 commuter trains from the south stopped at Albal, about 15 km (9 mi) out, instead of reaching Estació del Nord between 13:00 and 15:00. The council and the government delegation promised to study alternatives for 2027 (reported by [Ara](https://en.ara.cat/society/commuter-trains-will-not-arrive-in-valencia-during-the-fallas-mascletas_1_5677859.html)).

**The trade-off:** you only get the full force in the square itself, and with the nearest stations shut from 12:30, a mascletà takes the middle of your day.

### The Exposició del Ninot

Before the plantà, each falla sends a figure, a ninot, to an exhibition, and visitors vote for the one to be spared from the fire, the ninot indultat. In 2026 it ran from 7 February to 15 March at the Museu de les Ciències in the City of Arts and Sciences, Monday to Thursday 10:00–20:00 and Friday to Sunday 10:00–21:00, closing at 15:00 on 14 and 15 March. Entry was €4, or €2 for children aged 4 to 12, over-65s and disabled visitors ([Visit València](https://www.visitvalencia.com/en/events-valencia/exhibition-ninot-fallas)). The 2027 dates and venue aren't announced.

**The trade-off:** it's the calmest way to see the craftsmanship up close, but in 2026 it closed on 15 March, before the big days, so you need to arrive early.

### The Despertà

The Despertà is a wake-up march with firecrackers. In 2026 the big one, the Macrodespertà, set off at 07:30 on Sunday 22 February, the day of the Crida, after a children's Despertà at 07:00, and finished in the Plaza del Ayuntamiento ([official programme](https://www.visitvalencia.com/agenda-valencia/programa-fallas-2026)). The 2027 date isn't announced.

**The trade-off:** it's in late February, weeks before the big days, and it starts at 07:00.

### The plantà and the monuments

On the night of 15 to 16 March the commissions put up their fallas, and at dawn the jury picks the winners in each category ([Visit València](https://www.visitvalencia.com/en/fallas)). In 2026 the children's fallas had to be up by 09:00 on 15 March and the main ones by 08:00 on 16 March ([official programme](https://www.visitvalencia.com/agenda-valencia/programa-fallas-2026)). Walking between the monuments from 16 to 18 March costs nothing and is the heart of the festival.

**The trade-off:** the streets around the big fallas are shared with marquees, food stalls and street parties, so getting close to the famous ones takes patience.

### The Ofrenda

On 17 and 18 March, from 15:30 until after midnight, falleros and falleras parade along Calle de la Paz and Calle San Vicente to the Plaza de la Virgen to bring flowers to the Virgin of the Forsaken. In 2026 the last groups arrived between 00:40 and 01:00 ([official programme](https://www.visitvalencia.com/agenda-valencia/programa-fallas-2026)).

**The trade-off:** the parade fills both streets from mid-afternoon to after midnight on both days, so allow extra time to cross the old town.

### The Nit del Foc and the night fireworks

In 2026 there were firework castles (castillos) at 23:59 on 16 and 17 March at the Puente de Monteolivete, by the City of Arts and Sciences, and the biggest, the Nit del Foc, at 23:59 on 18 March ([Visit València](https://www.visitvalencia.com/en/events-valencia/fallas-festival-events-calendar-2022)). There were night shows in the Plaza del Ayuntamiento on several earlier nights too. In 2027 the Nit del Foc falls on the night of Thursday 18 March; time and place aren't announced. On the 2026 firework nights the council closed the roads around the City of Arts and Sciences and the Montolivet bridge ([Ajuntament de València](https://www.valencia.es/es/-/tr%C3%A0fico-fallas-val%C3%A8ncia)).

**The trade-off:** it starts at midnight, by the City of Arts and Sciences rather than in the old town, on the night before the cremà.

### The cremà

On 19 March 2026 the Falleras Mayores offered flowers to Saint Joseph on the San José bridge at 11:00, the Cabalgata del Fuego left Calle de la Paz at 19:00, the children's fallas burned at 20:00, all the others at 22:00, the Special Section winner at 22:30 and the Plaza del Ayuntamiento falla last, at 23:00 ([official programme](https://www.visitvalencia.com/agenda-valencia/programa-fallas-2026)).

**The trade-off:** most fallas burn at the same hour all over the city, so you'll see only a few and have to choose between a famous prize-winner and a smaller one in a quieter street.

## How loud is the mascletà, and is it safe for children?

Very loud. Hospital ear specialists say a mascletà can pass 120 decibels (Quirónsalud doctors, reported by [Alicante Plaza](https://alicanteplaza.es/alicanteplaza/aconsejan-respetar-la-distancia-de-seguridad-para-evitar-danos-en-mascletas)). The council's advice for firework shows ([Ajuntament de València](https://www.valencia.es/es/-/fallas-recomendaciones-para-acudir-a-los-espectaculos-pirotecnicos-bomberos-y-proteccion-civil)):

- Hold children's hands when you move around, avoid the crush and look for a spot with room.
- Stay behind the barriers.
- Keep your mouth slightly open during the show to protect your ears.
- If you can, stand with the wind at your back so debris blows away from you.
- On sunny days, wear light clothes and cover your head.

For small children, the American Academy of Pediatrics recommends well-fitting earmuffs rather than earplugs, which can be a choking hazard ([HealthyChildren.org](https://www.healthychildren.org/Spanish/health-issues/conditions/ear-nose-throat/Paginas/Tips-Preserve-Childs-Hearing-Holidays.aspx)). Spain's paediatric association goes further for concerts and festivals: under-sixes shouldn't go even with ear protection (reported by [Consumer](https://www.consumer.es/bebe/es-seguro-llevar-ninos-conciertos-festivales-musica)). A mascletà isn't a concert, but we'd go by the same advice: earmuffs for any child, and with the very young, keep out of the square and listen from a few streets away.

Firecrackers go off in the streets all week. Visit València asks people to respect the quiet hours, 09:00–10:00 and 15:00–17:00, and to call 112 in an emergency ([Visit València](https://www.visitvalencia.com/en/fallas/responible-tourism)).

## Getting to Valencia and around during Fallas

**By train.** High-speed trains arrive at València Joaquín Sorolla (metro lines 1 and 5); commuter trains use Estació del Nord, next to Xàtiva metro (lines 3 and 5). Renfe runs a free bus between the two for its ticket holders ([Visit València](https://www.visitvalencia.com/en/plan-your-trip-to-valencia/to-get-to-valencia/train)).

- **From Madrid:** OUIGO's non-stop trains from Madrid-Chamartín take 1 hour 54 minutes on its timetable and start at €9 ([OUIGO](https://www.ouigo.com/es/tren-madrid-valencia), read 21 November 2026). Renfe's AVE and Avlo leave from Madrid Puerta de Atocha, the first at 06:30 ([Renfe](https://www.renfe.com/es/es/inspirate/rutas/madrid-valencia)).
- **From Barcelona:** Renfe's Euromed runs from Barcelona Sants six times a day each way ([Renfe](https://grupo.renfe.com/es/es/sala-de-prensa/noticias/2026/05/renfe-incrementa-oferta-servicios-euromed-valencia-castello-barcelona)) and takes about 2 hours 45 minutes to 3 hours 20 minutes on the current timetable (reported by [trenes.com](https://www.trenes.com/barcelona-valencia/), a ticket site).

**By air.** Valencia airport is 8 km (5 mi) from the city ([Visit València](https://www.visitvalencia.com/en/plan-your-trip-to-valencia/to-get-to-valencia)), and metro lines 3 and 5 run into the centre, every 15 minutes on weekdays ([Aena](https://www.aena.es/en/valencia/como-llegar/underground.html)).

**Getting around.** In 2026 the metro and tram ran non-stop from Friday 13 March to the early hours of 20 March, 168 hours in a row (reported by [Europa Press](https://www.ultimahora.es/noticias/comunidades/2026/03/10/2586109/metrovalencia-ofrecera-dias-servicio-ininterrumpido-fallas-prevision-alcanzar-millones-viajeros.html)), and EMT city buses also ran round the clock. The council closed the centre to private cars from 15:00 on Monday 16 March to 04:00 on 20 March: the area inside the right bank of the old Turia riverbed, from the Glorias Valencianas bridge to the Plaza de América, and its main roads, including the Plaza de España. Taxis and buses could still enter ([Ajuntament de València](https://www.valencia.es/es/-/tr%C3%A0fico-fallas-val%C3%A8ncia)). Don't plan to drive in.

**The trade-off:** the centre is easy on foot and hard on wheels from 16 March, and the stations nearest the mascletà close at lunchtime.

## Where to stay for Fallas, and what it costs

A week before Fallas 2026, Valencia's hotels were 67% booked on average for the six nights from 13 to 19 March, with the eve of the holiday highest at 76.7%. The average rate for those nights was €166, the lowest average at that point in four years, and Saturday 14 March was the dearest night at €179. Tourist flats were 60.7% booked. In 2025 hotels reached 75% during the festival ([Visit València survey, via the Ajuntament](https://www.valencia.es/cas/actualidad/-/content/ocupaci%C3%B3n-hotelera-val%C3%A8ncia-fallas)).

So in 2026 Valencia still had rooms a week out, but the 2027 holiday falls on a Friday. Our advice: book the nights of 18 and 19 March as soon as your dates are fixed, and the rest within a month or two.

**Where:** inside the closed centre you're next to everything, including firecrackers and street parties late into the night. A room near a metro station outside the closure should be quieter, and the metro ran all night in 2026.

**Weather:** March in Valencia averages 19.3°C (67°F) by day and 10.0°C (50°F) at night, with rain on about four days ([WMO via NOAA](https://www.ncei.noaa.gov/products/wmo-climate-normals), 1991–2020 normals, Valencia station).

**The trade-off:** for its last five days Fallas takes over the whole city: loud from the morning firecrackers to the midnight fireworks, and hard to cross. If you want sleep, stay outside the centre and come in for the events.

## Seville's Holy Week, between the two

Holy Week runs from Palm Sunday, 21 March, to Easter Sunday, 28 March 2027. Seville's brotherhoods process through the week, and the Consejo de Cofradías has already approved the order on the official route for the biggest days: nine brotherhoods on Holy Wednesday, seven on Holy Thursday, six through the night of the Madrugá (25 to 26 March) and seven on Good Friday ([Consejo de Cofradías](https://www.hermandades-de-sevilla.org/consejo/aprobado-el-orden-de-paso-por-la-carrera-oficial-del-miercoles-santo-jueves-santo-madrugada-y-viernes-santo-de-la-semana-santa-de-2027/)).

Fallas ends two days before Palm Sunday, so one trip can take in both. Our [Easter 2027 guide](/blog/where-to-go-at-easter) covers chairs, hotels, weather and what closes, and [where to go in March](/blog/where-to-go-in-march) has other ideas for the month.

**The trade-off:** Holy Week fills Seville's hotels too, and the processions take over the centre's streets for hours at a time.

## When is the Feria de Abril 2027?

Not announced yet, but the signs point to Tuesday 13 to Sunday 18 April, after the pescaíto dinner and the alumbrado on the night of Monday 12 April:

- **The holiday:** Seville's 2027 local holidays are 14 April and 27 May, published by the regional government in the [BOJA](https://www.juntadeandalucia.es/boja/2026/210/25) of 29 October 2026. The council's festivals department proposed 14 April as "the Wednesday of the Feria" (reported by [Andalucía Informa](https://andaluciainforma.es/?p=31926)).
- **The format:** since 2025 the Feria has opened with the pescaíto and the alumbrado on a Monday night and run from Tuesday to Sunday. The city went back to that traditional format after a consultation in which 52% of 106,384 voters chose it (reported by [Vozpópuli](https://www.vozpopuli.com/espana/andalucia/feria-abril-sevilla-2025-pescaito.html)). In 2026 the Feria ran from Tuesday 21 to Sunday 26 April ([Visit Sevilla](https://tic.visitasevilla.es/vsev/Web_php/imatges/guias/185069aede903d804058444198.pdf)), after the pescaíto on Monday 20 April ([Renfe](https://www.renfe.com/es/es/inspirate/experiencias/fiestas-populares/feria-abril)).
- **Another council's reading:** when Jerez chose its own 2027 Horse Fair dates, its council's tourism board worked around "the Feria de Abril de Sevilla, 13 to 18 April" ([Ayuntamiento de Jerez](https://www.jerez.es/fileadmin/Documentos/Prensa/ANO_2026/06-JUNIO-2026/19-06-2026/NP_Mesa_del_Turismo_.docx)).

So the dates are expected, not confirmed: the [council's Feria pages](https://www.sevilla.org/fiestas-de-la-ciudad/feria-de-sevilla) didn't give them on 21 November. The 2027 gateway, the portada, has been chosen: "Puerta a los sueños 1927" by Hugo Montalbán Ramos (reported by [El Español](https://www.elespanol.com/sevilla/20260624/sevilla-portada-feria-homenaje-exposicion-generacion/1003744298218_0.html)). It will also be a transition year for the fairground: the mayor said the planned expansion, with up to 220 more casetas, can't be finished for 2027 and that 2028 is the realistic target, so part of the works will be under way during the Feria (reported by [Vozpópuli](https://www.vozpopuli.com/espana/andalucia/la-ampliacion-definitiva-de-la-feria-de-sevilla-se-traslada-al-2028-habra-un-numero-record-de-casetas.html)).

## The alumbrado and the Noche del Pescaíto

The Feria opens on Monday night with the pescaíto, a fried-fish dinner in the casetas for their members, followed at midnight by the alumbrado, when the portada and the streets of the fairground, the Real, light up ([Visit Sevilla](https://visitasevilla.es/wp-content/uploads/2025/04/Feria-2025_ESP.pptx.pdf)). In 2026 the portada carried more than 28,000 LED bulbs and stood 40 m (131 ft) high (reported by [Europa Press](https://www.ultimahora.es/noticias/comunidades/2026/04/21/2613521/ciudad-efimera-feria-sevilla-prepara-tras-alumbrao-para-recibir-miles-visitantes.html)); Visit Sevilla counts more than 200,000 bulbs across the Real. The Feria ends at midnight on Sunday with fireworks by the river, best seen from around the Triana bridge ([Visit Sevilla](https://tic.visitasevilla.es/vsev/Web_php/imatges/guias/185069aede903d804058444198.pdf)).

The alumbrado happens at the portada on Calle Antonio Bienvenida, so you can watch the lights come on without an invitation.

**The trade-off:** the pescaíto itself is for caseta members, so without a Sevillian host your first night is the lighting and the street.

## Which casetas can you get into?

There were 1,040 casetas in 2026 (reported by [Europa Press](https://www.ultimahora.es/noticias/comunidades/2026/04/21/2613521/ciudad-efimera-feria-sevilla-prepara-tras-alumbrao-para-recibir-miles-visitantes.html)). Most are private: they belong to families, groups of friends, clubs, firms and associations, and you get in when a member invites you. The district casetas are free to enter ([Visit Sevilla](https://tic.visitasevilla.es/vsev/Web_php/imatges/guias/185069aede903d804058444198.pdf)).

Visit Sevilla's 2026 guide lists 18 public casetas:

- **The six district casetas and the Caseta Municipal,** run by the council: Casco Antiguo (Antonio Bienvenida 97–101), Este–Cerro–Amate (Pascual Márquez 215–219), Macarena (Pascual Márquez 85–89), Nervión–San Pablo–Santa Justa (Costillares 22–26), Sur–Bellavista–La Palmera (Ignacio Sánchez Mejías 61–65), Triana–Los Remedios (Pascual Márquez 153–157) and the Caseta Municipal (Pepe Luis Vázquez 53). They close at 03:00 and play only sevillanas and rumbas.
- **The Caseta "Turismo de Sevilla",** Pascual Márquez 225–229, with a terrace and an indoor area. Tables need a reservation, in 2026 through the tourist office, and there are music sets from 16:00 to 03:00.
- **Ten more:** the trade unions CCOO, UGT and USO, the Partido Popular, C.F. San Fernando, the Hermandad de la Estrella, La Pecera, Marimorena, Niños Perdidos and El Garbanzo Negro.

The 2027 list will come with the 2027 guide; the 2025 and 2026 editions came out in March.

**The trade-off:** without an invitation you'll see the Feria from the street and the public casetas, while most of the eating and dancing happens behind private railings.

## Horses, carriages, dress and hours

The Feria has a day and a night. From about 14:00, riders in short jackets and wide-brimmed hats and horse-drawn carriages parade through the streets until about 19:00, when the horses leave. The night Feria starts around 20:00, and fills up again once the bullfights end, around 21:30. Public casetas close at 03:00, and most private ones go on until dawn ([Visit Sevilla](https://tic.visitasevilla.es/vsev/Web_php/imatges/guias/185069aede903d804058444198.pdf)). In 2026 Wednesday was the busiest day, with the peak at 19:00 (reported by [El Español](https://www.elespanol.com/sevilla/20260427/feria-sevilla-reune-millones-personas-genera-euros/1003744223696_0.html)).

**Carriages:** you can hire a horse-drawn carriage at the official stand next to the portada. The special Feria rate is €95 an hour, inside and outside the fairground (Visit Sevilla's 2026 guide, repeating the 2025 rate, read 21 November 2026).

**Dress:** nothing is compulsory. Women usually wear the flamenca dress and men a jacket and tie; otherwise Visit Sevilla suggests smart, comfortable clothes for dancing and for walking on albero, the golden sand that covers the streets.

**Money:** bring cash, because some casetas sell drinks with vouchers paid in cash.

**Weather:** April in Seville averages 23.7°C (75°F) by day and 11.5°C (53°F) at night, with rain on about six days ([WMO via NOAA](https://www.ncei.noaa.gov/products/wmo-climate-normals), 1991–2020 normals, Seville San Pablo station).

## How to get to the Real de la Feria and to Seville

**The fairground.** The Real is in Los Remedios, across the river from the old town, where it moved in the 1970s from the Prado de San Sebastián ([Visit Sevilla](https://visitasevilla.es/wp-content/uploads/2025/04/Feria-2025_ESP.pptx.pdf)). In 2026, metro line 1 ran non-stop during the Feria, stopping at Plaza de Cuba, Parque de los Príncipes and Blas Infante. Tussam ran special Feria shuttles from the Prado de San Sebastián for a single fare of €1.60, and city lines 5, 6, 41, C1 and C2 also reached the Real. Taxis had special access points and fares. Visit Sevilla's advice is not to drive ([Visit Sevilla](https://tic.visitasevilla.es/vsev/Web_php/imatges/guias/185069aede903d804058444198.pdf)). Tussam carried more than 1.3 million passengers in Feria week 2026 (reported by [El Español](https://www.elespanol.com/sevilla/20260427/feria-sevilla-reune-millones-personas-genera-euros/1003744223696_0.html)).

**From Madrid by train.** OUIGO's trains from Madrid Puerta de Atocha to Sevilla Santa Justa average 2 hours 47 minutes and start at €9 ([OUIGO](https://www.ouigo.com/es/tren-tren-madrid-sevilla-barato)). But the Madrid–Andalusia high-speed line closed after a fatal collision near Urda (Toledo) in late October 2026. On 21 November, OUIGO's page said trains between Madrid and Andalusia were suspended until at least 29 November; at the end of October Renfe had stopped selling new tickets on the line until 13 December (reported by [Demócrata](https://www.democrata.es/en/society/when-does-the-ave-madrid-andalusia-reopen-after-the-urda-accident-november-14-the-scheduled-date/)). The transport minister said that once the line reopens, trains will run every 10 to 15 minutes instead of every 5 until new signalling is in place (reported by [El Español](https://www.elespanol.com/invertia/observatorios/movilidad/20261103/puente-anuncia-reduciran-frecuencias-linea-madrid-sevilla-reanude-servicio/1003744444038_0.html)). Check before you book April trains.

**From the airport.** The EA bus costs €6 single, or €8 return on the same day, and runs into the city from 04:30 to 00:05 ([Aena](https://www.aena.es/en/sevilla/getting-there/bus.html)).

## Hotels and prices in Feria week

In April 2026 Seville had the highest average hotel rate of any Spanish destination the statistics office tracks, €204.60 a night, and the highest revenue per available room ([INE](https://ine.es/dyngs/Prensa/es/CTH0426.htm)). That month also held the end of Holy Week; in 2027 Holy Week falls in March. Hotel occupancy over Feria 2026 was 89%, five points up on 2025, and the council counted 2,414,625 people at the fairground with cameras (both from the mayor's Feria balance, reported by [El Español](https://www.elespanol.com/sevilla/20260427/feria-sevilla-reune-millones-personas-genera-euros/1003744223696_0.html)).

No authority sets booking windows, so this is our advice: book Feria-week rooms now, before the 2027 dates are formally announced, and choose a cancellable rate in case they move.

**The bullfights.** The Real Maestranza bullring is part of the Feria programme: its season opens on Easter Sunday, and its "farolillos" cycle runs to the last day of the Feria. Tickets are sold at the bullring's box office and the city's tourist offices ([Visit Sevilla](https://visitasevilla.es/wp-content/uploads/2025/04/Feria-2025_ESP.pptx.pdf)). The 2027 bills weren't announced on 21 November.

**The trade-off:** the Feria is a private party with a public street. Most casetas need an invitation, rooms cost more than in any other destination the statistics office tracked in April 2026, and this time the Madrid trains are less certain than usual.

## How to plan four days around Fallas or the Feria

**Fallas, Tuesday 16 to Friday 19 March.** Arrive on Monday evening, 15 March, when the fallas go up overnight. On Tuesday, walk the monuments in the morning, see the mascletà from the edge of the square and a castillo at night. Wednesday is the first Ofrenda afternoon, from 15:30, and Thursday the second, followed by the Nit del Foc. On Friday, the holiday, catch the last mascletà, the Cabalgata del Fuego at 19:00 and the burnings from 20:00. The clock times are 2026's; check the 2027 programme when it comes out. If you want the Ninot exhibition, come a few days earlier: in 2026 it closed on 15 March.

**Feria, Monday 12 to Thursday 15 April (expected dates).** Arrive on Monday, have dinner in town and walk to the portada for the alumbrado at midnight. On Tuesday, go in the early afternoon for the horses and a carriage ride, and eat in a public caseta. On Wednesday, the holiday and in 2026 the busiest day, go early and leave the fairground before the evening peak. On Thursday, see the city in the morning and go back for the night Feria. To finish with the fireworks, stay until Sunday 18 April.

[Lay out your Feria days](/trips/new) in the planner, move them around by chatting and share the plan with your group. It plans the days; it doesn't sell tickets or rooms.

## What to book first, and by when

No authority sets booking windows, so this is our advice.

1. **Now:** Seville rooms for 12–18 April, with free cancellation, and Valencia rooms for the nights of 18 and 19 March.
2. **As soon as you know your dates:** trains to Valencia. For Seville, check the state of the Madrid–Andalusia line with Renfe, Iryo or OUIGO before you commit, and again nearer the time.

5. **Before you go:** earmuffs for children at Fallas, cash and comfortable shoes for the Feria.

## Frequently asked questions

### When are the Fallas 2027?

The big days are 15 to 19 March: the plantà on 15 and 16 March, the Ofrenda on 17 and 18 March, the Nit del Foc on the night of Thursday 18 March and the cremà on Friday 19 March. The daily mascletàs start on 1 March; the programme with times isn't out yet.

### What time is the mascletà?

In 2026 it was at 14:00 every day from 1 to 19 March, in the Plaza del Ayuntamiento. The 2027 times aren't published yet. In 2026 the nearest metro stations, Xàtiva and Colón, closed from 12:30 to 14:30 on 14–19 March.

### When is the cremà in 2027?

On the night of Friday 19 March. The 2027 times aren't announced; in 2026 the children's fallas burned at 20:00, the rest at 22:00 and the Plaza del Ayuntamiento falla at 23:00.

### When is the Nit del Foc 2027?

On the night of Thursday 18 March, according to the council's outline of the 2027 rules. In 2026 it started at 23:59 at the Puente de Monteolivete.

### Is 19 March 2027 a holiday in Valencia?

Yes. San José is a holiday in the Valencian Community in 2027, and in Galicia, Madrid, Murcia and Navarre, according to the BOE.

### When is the Feria de Abril 2027?

Expected from Tuesday 13 to Sunday 18 April, with the pescaíto and the alumbrado on the night of Monday 12 April. The council hasn't announced the dates, but Wednesday 14 April, "the Wednesday of the Feria", is already one of Seville's two 2027 local holidays.

### When is the alumbrado of the Feria 2027?

Expected at midnight on Monday 12 April, after the pescaíto dinner, at the portada on Calle Antonio Bienvenida.

### Can I get into a caseta without an invitation?

Yes, into the public ones: Visit Sevilla listed 18 in 2026, including the six district casetas, the Caseta Municipal and the Caseta "Turismo de Sevilla". Most of the others, more than a thousand in all, are private.
