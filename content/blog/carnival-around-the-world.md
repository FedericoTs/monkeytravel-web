# Carnival 2027: Dates and the Best Celebrations Around the World

Carnival 2027 peaks on Tuesday 9 February: Mardi Gras, Shrove Tuesday and Carnival Tuesday at once. Ash Wednesday follows on 10 February, and Easter Sunday, the date pre-Lenten carnivals count back from, falls on 28 March. So the big weekend runs from Saturday 6 to Tuesday 9 February in Rio, Trinidad, Barranquilla, Binche and Ivrea, and Cologne's street carnival starts on Thursday 4 February.

A few cities keep their own clock: Basel begins on the Monday after Ash Wednesday, Santa Cruz de Tenerife has moved its 2027 street carnival to 19–28 February, and Nice runs to 28 February.

Every date below was checked on 4 October 2026 on the organiser's, city's or tourist board's own page wherever one exists; where we relied on local press, we name it. Where a 2027 date was not yet published, we say so and give the 2026 pattern. Weather figures are long-term normals from national weather services; safety notes come from city rules and UK travel advice.

## 1. Rio de Janeiro — Sambadrome nights and free blocos

Rio runs two carnivals: the ticketed samba-school contest at the Marquês de Sapucaí Sambadrome, and the free street *blocos*. Riotur authorised more than 400 bloco parades in 2026 and expects the 2027 street carnival to start on 16 January.

At the Sambadrome, the 17 Série Ouro schools parade on Friday 5 and Saturday 6 February. The 12 Special Group schools follow, four a night, on Sunday 7, Monday 8 and Tuesday 9 February, and the top six return for the Champions' Parade on Saturday 13 February.

LIESA, which runs the contest, sells through Ticketmaster. Grandstands in sectors 2 to 11 (except 9) and numbered chairs in sector 12 cost R$250 a night (about €43), or R$125 half price; *frisas*, open boxes for six beside the track, cost R$1,835–10,200 a night (about €313–1,740). Purchase limits are tied to a Brazilian CPF taxpayer number, and the "popular" sectors 12 and 13 were still to be released in early October. Série Ouro seats cost R$45 (about €8).

February averages 30°C (86°F) by day and 24°C (74°F) at night (INMET 1961–1990 via the WMO). The UK Foreign Office says criminals target Carnival and pickpocketing is common, so carry little cash and keep your phone hidden. From Galeão airport, the TransCarioca BRT connects with metro Line 2 at Vicente de Carvalho, and a Mobi-Rio bus runs from Terminal 2 to the Gentileza terminal for R$15 (about €3). See our [Rio de Janeiro guide](/destinations/rio-de-janeiro).

**The trade-off:** frisas start at R$1,835 a night, and the free blocos come with heat, crowds and pickpockets.

## 2. Salvador and Recife/Olinda — Brazil's street carnivals

Salvador's carnival happens in the street, behind *trios elétricos* (sound trucks). A Tarde reports Mayor Bruno Reis's 2027 plan: pre-carnival parties from Friday 29 January, the Festa de Iemanjá on 2 February, carnival from Thursday 4 to Tuesday 9 February, and the closing Arrastão on Ash Wednesday. The city's 2026 programme ran three circuits, Dodô (Barra–Ondina), Osmar (Campo Grande) and Batatinha (Pelourinho), and the Salvador da Bahia tourism portal lists access as free.

Recife opens with the Galo da Madrugada, which in 2026 paraded on carnival Saturday, 14 February, along a 6.5 km route, as Portal de Prefeitura reported. That pattern points to Saturday 6 February 2027, but we found no 2027 confirmation by 4 October. In Olinda, the city hall describes frevo, maracatu, afoxé, blocos and giant puppets climbing the hills of the historic centre.

Both cities average 30°C (86°F) highs and 23–24°C (73–75°F) nights in February, with showers on 16–17 days (INMET 1961–1990 via the WMO). The Foreign Office crime advice for Brazil applies here too. Salvador's Aeroporto metro station is a short walk or shuttle ride from arrivals, and Recife airport's nearest station, Aeroporto on the Linha Sul, has trains from 05:00 to 23:00.

**The trade-off:** the peak days overlap, so you pick Salvador or Recife and Olinda for the main weekend, and the humidity and showers come with both.

## 3. Trinidad and Tobago — J'Ouvert and two days of mas

Trinidad's carnival is made for joining: you can "play mas" in a band's costume or watch from the roadside. The National Carnival Commission's ten-year calendar fixes Carnival Monday and Tuesday on 8 and 9 February 2027, and the tourism board says the season starts just after Christmas.

The 2027 events calendar was not out on 4 October. In 2026, Dimanche Gras (the Kings and Queens final) ran on Sunday at 19:00 at the Queen's Park Savannah, J'Ouvert started across the country at 04:00 on Monday, and the Parade of the Bands ran from 11:00 on Monday and 08:00 on Tuesday, from central Port of Spain to the Savannah. J'Ouvert is the pre-dawn opening, when people cover themselves in paint, mud and powder.

Port of Spain averages 32°C (89°F) highs and 20°C (68°F) lows in February (Trinidad and Tobago Meteorological Service 1961–1990 via the WMO). The Foreign Office warns of robberies and opportunistic crime during carnival: hide jewellery, plan your ride home and never leave a drink unattended. At Piarco International Airport, the airport taxi desk is just outside the customs hall exit, and public buses leave from the kerb outside Arrivals between 06:00 and 20:00.

**The trade-off:** the big days start at 04:00 and end in paint and mud, and the commission's own planning slogan is "book earlier".

## 4. Barranquilla — Colombia's Caribbean carnival

UNESCO proclaimed Barranquilla's carnival in 2003 and inscribed it in 2008: four days before Lent of congo, mico y micas and paloteo dances, with cumbia, puya and porro. The Fundación Carnaval de Barranquilla has set 2027 for Saturday 6 to Tuesday 9 February.

The carnival queen receives the city's symbolic keys at the Lectura del Bando on Saturday 16 January, and the Guacherna lantern parade lights the streets on Friday 29 January. Baila la Calle turns Carrera 50 into an open-air dance floor from Thursday 4 to Sunday 7 February. The Batalla de Flores opens carnival on Saturday 6, the Gran Parada de Tradición follows on Sunday 7 and the Gran Parada de Comparsas on Monday 8; Tuesday's mock funeral of Joselito Carnaval is marked "to be confirmed".

Expect 32°C (89°F) highs, 24°C (75°F) nights and, on average, no February rain (IDEAM 1971–2000 via the WMO). The Foreign Office lists pickpocketing, mugging and armed robbery in Colombia's major cities, including the Caribbean coast, and reports criminals drugging victims with scopolamine.

**The trade-off:** the sun is relentless and the safety advice is serious, so buy parade entries through official channels.

## 5. Oruro — the Diablada at 3,700 metres

Oruro sits at 3,700 metres in western Bolivia. UNESCO, which proclaimed its carnival in 2001 and inscribed it in 2008, describes Andean ritual that survived behind Christian liturgy. The main event is the *entrada*: more than 28,000 dancers and 10,000 musicians in about 50 groups cover a 4 km route over 20 hours. The *diablada*, the devil dance, is the signature.

We could not find a published 2027 programme by 4 October 2026. UNESCO lists the 2025 carnival days as 1–2 March, the Saturday and Sunday before Ash Wednesday; the same pattern points to 6–7 February 2027. Confirm before you book.

SENAMHI's 1991–2020 normals for Oruro city give February highs of 18.9°C (66°F) and lows of 4.4°C (40°F), so pack for a mild afternoon and a cold night. The Foreign Office flags altitude sickness as a risk in parts of Bolivia including La Paz, petty crime in crowded areas, and road blockades that can close routes, including roads to airports, at short notice.

**The trade-off:** you need days to acclimatise and slack in the schedule for blockades, and the dates are not yet official.

## 6. New Orleans — Mardi Gras from Twelfth Night

The season opens on Twelfth Night, 6 January, and ends on Mardi Gras, Tuesday 9 February 2027. New Orleans & Company lists Joan of Arc on 6 January, Krewe du Vieux on 23 January, Muses on Thursday 4 February, Endymion on Saturday 6, Bacchus on Sunday 7, Orpheus on Monday 8, and Zulu and Rex on Mardi Gras day, with final dates and routes still to come.

All parades are free; tickets are sold for some balls and viewing-stand seats. City code rules the route: nothing on public ground more than four hours before a parade, ladders no taller than 6 feet (1.8 m) and at least 6 feet back from the curb, and no tents, ropes or other enclosures. Throwing beads back at floats costs a $250 fine (about €223). Stay behind barricades and never cross between floats.

February averages 19°C (66°F) by day and 10°C (50°F) at night (NOAA 1991–2020, New Orleans airport); bring layers for night parades. Flights and rooms get dearer closer to the date, the tourism board warns.

**The trade-off:** hotels on the St. Charles Avenue and Canal Street routes book out close to a year ahead for the final weekend, and road closures mean you walk.

## 7. Venice — masks, costumes and the city as a stage

Venice has confirmed Carnevale 2027 for 23 January to 9 February. The programme, theme and any Volo dell'Angelo date had not been published by 4 October 2026. The 2026 edition (31 January to 17 February) staged the Festa delle Marie, a mask contest in Piazza San Marco, commedia dell'arte stages, street shows across Venice and Mestre, and float parades on the islands and mainland. Tickets for the Official Dinner Show have been on sale since 27 July 2026.

Venice's day-tripper access fee (€5 or €10) ran on selected days from 3 April to 26 July in 2026, so it missed Carnival; the 2027 calendar had not been published by 4 October 2026.

February averages 9°C (47°F) highs and 1°C (33°F) lows (Italian Air Force 1971–2000 via the WMO). When the tide passes 80 cm, raised walkways go up along the main routes. Venezia Unica sells transport, including the Marco Polo airport transfer, with some online fares cheaper if you buy at least 30 days ahead.

**The trade-off:** it is cold and damp, and the full programme arrives late, so you book a room before you know what is on.

## 8. Viareggio — giant satirical floats on the seafront

Viareggio's floats are papier-mâché caricatures of politicians and celebrities, and Tuscany's tourist board calls them the world's largest. They parade along a seafront promenade of a little over 3 km on six days in 2027: Saturday 30 January, Thursday 4 February (Giovedì Grasso), Sunday 7, Tuesday 9 (Martedì Grasso), Sunday 14 and Saturday 20 February. On weekend nights the *rioni* (neighbourhoods) hold open-air parties in streets closed to traffic.

Parades are ticketed. The carnival foundation's website blocked our checks, so the latest prices we could verify come from FamilyGo's 2026 guide: €16.50 for under-14s, free for children under 120 cm, and discounted passes for all six parades until 6 January 2026, sold online and at the box office in the Cittadella del Carnevale. In 2026 the parades fell on 1, 7, 12, 15, 17 and 21 February.

Using Pisa's normals as a proxy (Italian Air Force via the WMO), February averages 13°C (55°F) highs and 3°C (37°F) lows, with rain on about seven days.

**The trade-off:** you get six fixed days, so the trip has to fit one of them, and we could not verify a 2027 adult ticket price.

## 9. Ivrea — the Battle of the Oranges

Ivrea's carnival re-enacts a legendary revolt: teams of *aranceri* on foot, without protection, fight masked throwers on horse-drawn carts with oranges. The season opens at Epiphany, 6 January. The official 2027 programme has the General taking power at 14:00 on Thursday 4 February, the battle on Sunday 7, Monday 8 and Tuesday 9 February in five squares (Piazza Ottinetti, Piazza di Città, Borghetto, Piazza del Rondolino and Piazza Freguglia), and polenta and cod in Piazza Lamarmora at 11:00 on Ash Wednesday.

Wear the red Phrygian cap. The official guide invites everyone in the squares to put it on from Thursday and calls it "indispensable" from Sunday if you do not want to be the target of a "gentle and moderate" throw of oranges. Entry to the city on Carnival Sunday, 7 February, is paid (free for under-12s, residents, disabled visitors and registered aranceri); in 2026 it cost €15 plus booking fees.

Using Turin's normals as a proxy (Italian Air Force 1971–2000 via the WMO), February averages 9°C (48°F) highs and −1°C (31°F) lows.

**The trade-off:** this is a real fight with horses and flying fruit in small squares, so think twice with small children, and budget for Sunday's fee.

## 10. Nice — illuminated corsos and flower battles

The Côte d'Azur tourism board lists 9 to 28 February 2027, and the official site has announced the theme, *Vive l'Amour*; the programme and prices were not out by 4 October. In 2026, illuminated corsos ran on Tuesdays and Saturdays, with a daytime corso on one Sunday, flower battles (Batailles de Fleurs) on Wednesdays and Saturdays, and the carnival king's burning at 22:45 on the final Saturday.

Adult prices in 2026: illuminated-corso grandstands €28 on Place Masséna or €23 on the Promenade des Anglais, standing zones €14 or €7; flower-battle grandstands €28 or €23. Refundable tickets cost about 10% more. The official site says visitors in full costume can stand free in the illuminated corsos' pedestrian zones, capacity permitting.

Security means gates, bag searches and pat-downs, so arrive two hours early and bring no bag. Alcohol, glass, sprays, folding chairs, drones and religious, military or offensive costumes are banned. Nice-Ville station is a 15-minute walk from Place Masséna, and in 2026 trains ran every 15 minutes between Cannes and Menton, with extras after 23:00.

February averages 13°C (56°F) highs and 6°C (43°F) lows (Météo-France 1971–2000 via the WMO).

**The trade-off:** every big moment is ticketed and security-checked, and the queue is part of the evening.

## 11. Santa Cruz de Tenerife and Cádiz — Spain's two great carnivals

Santa Cruz de Tenerife has pushed its 2027 street carnival two weeks later, to 19–28 February (after Ash Wednesday), because its fairground was booked until 6 January. The programme lists the Queen election gala on Wednesday 17 February at 21:30, the opening cabalgata on Friday 19 at 19:30, Carnaval de Día on Sunday 21 and Saturday 27, the Coso Apoteósico parade on Tuesday 23 at 16:00 (a local holiday), and closing fireworks on Sunday 28 at 20:00. Galas usually sell out fast; parades and street parties are free. Bus 20 links Tenerife North airport with Santa Cruz for €2.65. February averages 21°C (70°F) highs and 15°C (60°F) lows (AEMET 1981–2010 via the WMO).

Cádiz keeps its usual slot, 4–14 February 2027: from the Thursday before Ash Wednesday to the following Sunday. Its heart is the official contest (COAC) at the Gran Teatro Falla, where *coros*, *comparsas*, *chirigotas* and *cuartetos* compete with satirical songs; Planomato reports the adult final for Friday 5 February. Then the streets fill with homemade costumes. Renfe's C-1 line runs from Jerez airport to Cádiz in about 57 minutes. February averages 17°C (62°F) highs and 11°C (51°F) lows (AEMET 1981–2010 via the WMO).

**The trade-off:** Tenerife's late dates rule out pairing it with the 9 February carnivals, and Cádiz's humour lives in fast Spanish lyrics.

## 12. Cologne — from Weiberfastnacht to Rosenmontag

Cologne's season opens every 11 November, but the street carnival starts on Weiberfastnacht, Thursday 4 February 2027, peaks with the Rosenmontag parade on Monday 8 February and stops on Ash Wednesday. The 2027 motto is *Morje es, wat do drus mähs!* ("Tomorrow is what you make of it").

In 2026 the Rosenmontagszug left Chlodwigplatz at 10:00 and covered 8.5 km in about 3.5 hours, with some 12,000 participants and 300 tonnes of sweets for the hundreds of thousands lining the route. Grandstand seats and indoor *Sitzungen* (carnival shows) for 2027 went on sale through the Festkomitee on 16 September 2026.

Plan around the glass bans. In 2026 glass was banned in the Zülpicher Viertel from 08:00 on Weiberfastnacht to 08:00 the next day, from 15:00 on Friday, from 12:00 on Saturday and from 15:00 on Rosenmontag, with shorter bans in the Altstadt and on the Inner Green Belt, and entrances to the Zülpicher Viertel were controlled.

February averages 7°C (44°F) highs and −1°C (30°F) lows, with rain on about nine days (DWD 1971–2000 via the WMO). Cologne Bonn Airport is served around the clock by S-Bahn and regional trains.

**The trade-off:** the party districts mean glass checks and controlled entrances, and the weather is cold and often wet.

## 13. Binche — the Gilles of Mardi Gras

Binche's carnival, proclaimed by UNESCO in 2003 and inscribed in 2008, peaks on Mardi Gras, when several hundred Gilles in red, yellow and black costumes, wax masks, wooden clogs and ostrich-feather hats parade through town to the drum. The 2027 dates are Sunday 7, Monday 8 and Tuesday 9 February.

Mardi Gras runs to a script. The Gilles dress at home before dawn and are collected house by house; from 08:30 the societies gather on the Grand-Place in wax masks. At 15:00 the parade leaves the Battignies crossroads, and this is when the oranges are handed out and the feather hats go on. Around 20:00 an evening parade follows by Bengal light, and fireworks close the night at about 21:30.

Night shops may not sell glass bottles or alcohol from Sunday 05:00 to Wednesday 06:00, and a lost-children desk at the town hall opens all three days. In 2025 the Belgian railways added capacity and a special 22:34 return to Brussels-Midi on Mardi Gras.

February averages 6°C (44°F) highs and 1°C (33°F) lows, with rain on about ten days (Royal Meteorological Institute, Brussels, 1971–2000 via the WMO).

**The trade-off:** it is one long, cold day in a small town, and the last trains set your bedtime.

## 14. Basel — Morgestraich, the Monday after Ash Wednesday

Basel's Fasnacht begins after Ash Wednesday, at 04:00 on Monday 15 February 2027, and lasts exactly 72 hours, to 04:00 on Thursday 18 February. UNESCO inscribed it in 2017 and counts about 20,000 active participants and 200,000 visitors.

At the fourth chime of St Martin's church the centre's lights go off and the cliques march in with piccolos, drums and hand-painted lanterns. Barfüsserplatz, Marktplatz and Freie Strasse are the busiest spots; Münsterplatz and Nadelberg are calmer. On the 2026 pattern, about 10,000 costumed participants join the cortège on Monday and Wednesday from 13:30 to 18:00, and Tuesday brings the children's Fasnacht and the Guggen brass concerts from 19:30 to 23:00.

Basel Tourism asks spectators to wear the Fasnacht badge (*Blaggedde*) visibly, skip face paint, clown noses and jester hats, never use a flash, keep curtains closed during the Morgestraich, avoid forming chains and agree on meeting points.

February averages 7°C (45°F) highs and 0°C (32°F) lows, with 13 frost days (MeteoSwiss 1991–2020). The EuroAirport shuttle bus runs every 7 minutes and takes 15 minutes to the centre.

**The trade-off:** you stand in the dark and cold at 04:00, and spectators stay on the pavement while the cliques march.

## Practical tips

- **Book fixed-date events first.** Rio's Sambadrome sales have run in phases since April 2026, Cologne's grandstands went on sale on 16 September, and New Orleans' parade-route hotels book up a year ahead. Then match flights; see [when to book cheap flights](/blog/cheapest-flights-2026-when-and-where-to-book).
- **Read the small print.** Check minimum-stay rules and cancellation terms on carnival-week rooms. In Nice, refundable seats cost about 10% more.
- **Know the costume rules.** Costume freely in Venice and Cádiz, wear the red cap in Ivrea, go fully costumed for free standing entry in Nice, and skip face paint and clown noses in Basel.
- **Carry less.** Nice bans most bags and drink containers. Leave jewellery at home, carry little cash, agree a meeting point and take a group photo at the start of the day, as New Orleans' emergency office suggests.
- **Watch the transport windows.** Nice added late trains after night corsos in 2026, Binche ran a special return train in 2025, and New Orleans closes roads before parades.
- **Respect heat, altitude and cold.** The tropical carnivals average 30–32°C (86–89°F) highs, Oruro sits at 3,700 m, and Basel averages 13 frost days in February. Use our [travel packing checklist](/blog/travel-packing-checklist).
- **Money and groups.** Prices use the European Central Bank rate of 2 October 2026 (€1 = R$5.86 = US$1.12). For trips with friends, read [how to plan a group trip](/blog/how-to-plan-a-group-trip), and see [where to go in February](/blog/where-to-go-in-february) for more ideas.

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### When is Carnival 2027?

Carnival Tuesday (Mardi Gras or Shrove Tuesday) is 9 February 2027, Ash Wednesday is 10 February and Easter Sunday is 28 March. Most carnivals peak from Saturday 6 to Tuesday 9 February; Basel, Santa Cruz de Tenerife and Nice run later.

### What are the Rio Carnival 2027 dates?

The Special Group parades at the Sambadrome are on 7, 8 and 9 February 2027, after the Série Ouro nights on 5 and 6 February, and the Champions' Parade is on 13 February. Grandstand seats cost R$250 a night (about €43). Riotur expects the free street blocos to start on 16 January.

### When is Venice Carnival 2027?

From 23 January to 9 February 2027, according to the official site. The programme, theme and any Volo dell'Angelo date had not been published by 4 October 2026, so keep plans flexible.

### When is Mardi Gras 2027 in New Orleans?

Mardi Gras is Tuesday 9 February 2027, and parades start on Twelfth Night, 6 January. New Orleans & Company's provisional listing for the final week has Muses on 4 February, Endymion on the 6th, Bacchus on the 7th, Orpheus on the 8th, and Zulu and Rex on the 9th; final dates and routes are still to come. All parades are free.

### Can I see two carnivals on one trip?

Cologne's Rosenmontag (8 February) and Binche's Mardi Gras (9 February) fall on consecutive days, and Basel's Morgestraich follows on 15 February. In Italy, Venice overlaps with Ivrea's oranges (7–9 February) and Viareggio's parades.

### Which carnivals need tickets?

Rio's Sambadrome, Nice's corsos and flower battles, Viareggio's parades, Tenerife's galas and Ivrea's Carnival Sunday all charge. New Orleans parades, Salvador's street circuits and Tenerife's street parties are free; Basel expects spectators to wear the badge.
