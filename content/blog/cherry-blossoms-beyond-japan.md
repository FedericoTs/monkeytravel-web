# Cherry Blossom 2027 Beyond Japan: Korea, Washington DC, Spain's Jerte Valley and More

Outside Japan, the surest cherry blossom trips in 2027 are South Korea from the last days of March into early April, Washington DC, where the National Cherry Blossom Festival runs from 20 March to 11 April 2027, and Spain's Valle del Jerte, whose bloom of about ten days usually lands between mid-March and early April. Taiwan opens the season in February, and the later trees of Bonn and Vancouver carry it on through April.

No 2027 bloom forecast exists yet for any of these places; the first arrive in late February and early March. Until then, plan on the official averages below and keep your dates movable. Every date, time and price was read on 21 November 2026, on an official page wherever one exists: the city, organiser, rail operator, tourism board or weather service. ## When is cherry blossom season outside Japan in 2027?

The bloom follows latitude and altitude more than the calendar, so it helps to line the places up by month:

- **February:** Wuling Farm in Taiwan's central mountains.
- **Mid-March:** Alishan's season opens on 10 March with its mountain cherries, and the lowest orchards of the Jerte valley can start.
- **Late March:** Jinhae, Busan and Gyeongju in an average year, Washington's peak, the main Jerte bloom and Vancouver's Akebono trees.
- **Early April:** Seoul, Washington in a late year and the highest Jerte villages.
- **Mid-April:** Bonn's Kanzan cherries and Vancouver's.

2026 ran early in Korea: Seoul's official tree flowered on 29 March, ten days before its average. Washington peaked on 26 March 2026. Japan blooms in the same weeks; our [Japan cherry blossom guide](/blog/japan-cherry-blossom-season-guide) has its averages and forecasters.

Once you've picked a window, [lay out your blossom trip](/trips/new) in the planner: add the cities and dates, move days around by chatting and share the plan with whoever is coming. It doesn't book trains or rooms; that part stays with you.

## Where to see cherry blossoms in South Korea in 2027

Korea's blossom moves from south to north. On the KMA's 1991–2020 averages, cherry trees first flower on 25 March in Jeju, 28 March in Busan, 29 March in Daegu and 8 April in Seoul. The KMA counts a tree as flowering when one branch has three or more open flowers, and in full bloom when 80% are open; Seoul's average full bloom is 10 April.

### Jinhae Gunhangje: Korea's biggest cherry blossom festival

**When:** the 2027 dates are not announced yet. In 2026 the 64th festival ran from Friday 27 March to Sunday 5 April, and in 2025 from 29 March to 6 April; both spanned the last weekend of March and the first weekend of April.

**What you see:** about 360,000 king cherry trees across a naval town in Changwon, where a memorial to Admiral Yi Sun-sin in 1952 became the festival in 1963 (Changwon city). The famous spots are the Yeojwacheon stream and Gyeonghwa Station, and the Naval Academy and naval base, normally closed to the public, open their gates during the festival (Korea Tourism Organization). In 2026 the Black Eagles display team was scheduled over the Naval Academy on 1 April from 15:15 to 15:40 KST, and fireworks off Jinhaeru from 20:00 to 21:00, both weather permitting (Changwon tourism).

**Getting there and cost:** On the timetable in force in November 2026, KTX trains from Seoul station to Changwon Jungang take 2 hours 45 minutes to 3 hours 7 minutes, with 12 trains a day from Monday to Thursday and 15 from Friday to Sunday, for standard fares of ₩47,000 to ₩47,400 (Korail). From there you continue by bus or taxi into Jinhae. For 2026 the city planned three free shuttle lines inside Jinhae at weekends and one on weekdays, plus a 3.2 km (2-mile) bus-only lane on weekends (reported by The Asia Business Daily). The festival is free except for the ticketed Cherry Blossom Music Festival.

**Crowds:** more than two million visitors during the festival, by the city's count.

**The trade-off:** two million visitors in ten days is a lot of people for one district, and the dates are fixed before anyone knows the bloom. The naval base open days aren't guaranteed either: in 2025 all of them, planned for 29 March to 6 April, were cancelled, and the city's notice gave no reason.

### Seoul: Yeouido and Seokchon Lake

**When:** the KMA's average first flowers at Seoul's official tree are on 8 April, with full bloom on 10 April; the trees on Yeouido's Yunjung-ro average 6 April. Both flowered on 29 March in 2026, and the city tree on 4 April in 2025.

**What you see:** the Yeouido Spring Flower Festival lines Yeouiseo-ro, the road behind the National Assembly, with about 1,886 cherry trees and some 80,000 spring flowers. In 2026 it ran from 3 to 7 April and was free (Korea Tourism Organization). It was brought forward because of the early bloom, and traffic around it was restricted from 1 to 8 April (reported by News1). At Seokchon Lake, more than 1,000 king cherries ring the water; Songpa district's festival there ran from 3 to 11 April 2026, with free performances and paid market stalls and food trucks.

**Getting there:** Line 9's National Assembly station is in front of the Assembly building (reported by The Korea Times), and Seokchon Lake is 282 m from exit 2 of Jamsil station on Lines 2 and 8 (Visit Seoul). Our [five-day Seoul itinerary](/blog/seoul-5-day-itinerary) covers the rest of the city, and [Tokyo vs Seoul](/blog/tokyo-vs-seoul) weighs the two.

**The trade-off:** on average Seoul flowers 11 days after Busan, so a single week rarely catches the south and the capital at their best, though an early spring like 2026's can close the gap. April nights in Seoul average 8.0°C (46°F).

### Gyeongju: blossom by the royal tombs

**When:** we found no KMA average for Gyeongju; Daegu, the nearest city with one, averages 29 March. The 2027 festival dates are not announced yet. In 2026 the Daereungwon Stone Wall Road Festival ran from 3 to 5 April, free, with the road closed to traffic and the trees lit from 20:00 to 22:00 (Korea Tourism Organization).

**Getting there and cost:** On the timetable in force in November 2026, KTX trains from Seoul station to Gyeongju on the high-speed line take 1 hour 58 minutes to 2 hours 53 minutes, typically 2 hours 14, with 23 departures a day from Monday to Thursday and 27 from Friday to Sunday; five slower KTX-Eum trains a day go via Andong in 3 hours to 3 hours 17 minutes. Standard fares run from ₩38,000 to ₩44,600 depending on the route the train takes (Korail).

**The trade-off:** the festival is a single weekend fixed weeks ahead, and Gyeongju's spring swings hard: April averages 19.7°C (67°F) by day and 6.0°C (43°F) at night, at a station with only ten years of records.

### Busan: a base for the south

**When:** Busan's average first flowers are on 28 March (KMA). In 2026 Gangseo district's Nakdong River 30-ri Cherry Blossom Festival ran from 3 to 5 April at Daejeo Ecological Park, free apart from some paid activities (Korea Tourism Organization).

**Getting there and cost:** On the timetable in force in November 2026, KTX trains from Seoul station take 2 hours 18 minutes to 3 hours 27 minutes, typically 2 hours 44, with 53 to 65 departures a day, the most on Saturdays. Standard fares run from ₩48,000 to ₩54,400 (Korail).

**The trade-off:** April is wet, with 140.9 mm (5.5 in) of rain on average, almost twice Seoul's 72.9 mm, and Gangseo's festival lasts a single weekend.

## Washington DC: when is peak bloom in 2027?

**When:** the National Park Service defines peak bloom as the day 70% of the Yoshino blossoms are open, and says it's most likely between the last week of March and the first week of April. Records run from 15 March (1990) to 18 April (1958). Over the last ten years (2017–2026) the peak fell between 17 March and 5 April, on 25 or 26 March on average (our calculation from the NPS table). Once peak bloom begins, the blossoms typically stay on the trees for seven to ten days.

**The forecast:** the NPS announced its 2026 window, 29 March to 1 April, on 5 March, so expect the 2027 call in early March. Its Bloom Watch page then tracks the buds through six stages, from green buds to peak.

**What you see:** about 3,800 cherry trees. The Yoshinos, about 70% of them, circle the Tidal Basin and run onto the Washington Monument grounds; the Kwanzans, about 13%, grow mainly in East Potomac Park and bloom about two weeks later, which rescues a trip that misses the main peak (NPS). The restored Tidal Basin seawall reopened in April 2026 with wider walkways, after the planting of 546 new trees, 353 of them cherries (US Department of the Interior). The loop around the basin is two miles (3.2 km), level and wheelchair accessible, and takes one to three hours.

**The festival:** the National Cherry Blossom Festival runs from 20 March to 11 April 2027. The Blossom Kite Festival is on Saturday 27 March (10:00–16:00) on the Washington Monument grounds, Petalpalooza at Capitol Riverfront on Saturday 3 April (13:00–21:00), and the parade on Constitution Avenue on Saturday 10 April (11:00–13:00), with the Sakura Matsuri street festival on 10 and 11 April. Times are Eastern Daylight Time.

**Getting there and cost:** take the Metro to Smithsonian, about half a mile (0.8 km) from the Tidal Basin Welcome Area; the NPS warns there isn't enough parking for everyone who drives. Seeing the trees is free, and so is watching the parade from the route; grandstand seats cost $28 to $45 in 2026 (festival).

**Crowds:** the festival says it welcomes more than 1.6 million people.

**The trade-off:** the festival fills three weeks, the peak lasts days. In 2024 the peak came on 17 March; a peak that early in 2027 would land three days before the festival opens.

## Spain: when is the Valle del Jerte's Cerezo en Flor in 2027?

**When:** Turespaña, Spain's tourist board, puts the bloom at about ten days between mid-March and early April. In 2026 the valley's trees flowered from 20 March to about 3 April, by the tourist office's count. For 2027 it has no indication yet, and says the first signs usually come in late February or early March. The lower orchards open first and the colder, higher ones last, so the white climbs the valley over those days.

**The fiesta:** the Fiesta del Cerezo en Flor is a Fiesta de Interés Turístico Nacional, set inside a longer spring programme. In 2026 that programme ran from 20 March to 3 May (Junta de Extremadura); the fiesta itself opened in Tornavacas on 27–29 March and closed in Casas del Castañar on 11–12 April (tourist office). For a few days, the office says, villages open old cellars, forges and wine presses as they were 50 or more years ago. The 2027 dates and host villages are not announced yet.

**What you see:** more than one and a half million cherry trees on terraces cut into the valley sides. The tourist office suggests two drives: about 30 km (19 miles) up the N-110 through Navaconcejo, Cabezuela del Valle, Jerte and Tornavacas to the Puerto de Tornavacas pass, and a loop of about 50 km (31 miles) through the hill villages of Valdastillas, Piornal, Barrado, Cabrero, Casas del Castañar, El Torno and Rebollar.

**Getting there:** Turespaña puts the valley less than three hours west of Madrid and recommends going by car. The fastest road is the A-5 to Navalmoral de la Mata, the EX-A1 to Plasencia and the N-110 up the valley. The AP-6 and N-110 via Ávila and Barco de Ávila is slower but enters from the top, over the pass. Without a car, CEVESA buses run from Madrid's Estación Sur via Barco de Ávila, Renfe trains run to Plasencia, and buses from Plasencia stop in Navaconcejo, Cabezuela del Valle, Jerte and Tornavacas (tourist office). The tourist office in Cabezuela del Valle opens Tuesday to Thursday 9:00–15:30 and at weekends 10:00–14:00, and is closed on Mondays and Fridays.

**The trade-off:** ten days is a short window, nobody will name it before late February, and weekends bring the crowds, which is why the tourist office suggests a weekday. Book a room you can cancel, and drive.

## More cherry blossoms: Bonn, Vancouver and Taiwan

### Bonn: two streets of Japanese cherries

**When:** the city says the peak usually comes in mid-April, lasts at most 10 to 14 days and is over by early May. 2026 ran early, if not as early as first forecast: on 23 March the city expected the buds to open by the last weekend of March, on 25 March it moved that to the first week of April at the earliest, and on 2 April about 60% were open, with full bloom expected over Easter.

**What you see:** about 60 Kanzan cherries on each of Heerstraße and Breite Straße in the Altstadt, which the city calls its blossom tunnels, among about 300 ornamental cherries in the old town. Beuel, across the Rhine, has smaller avenues: 47 trees on Professor-Neu-Allee and 49 on Rilkestraße.

**Getting there and cost:** it's free. In 2026 the city closed both streets to through traffic from Good Friday, 3 April: on Fridays and holidays from 14:00 and at weekends from 10:00, with tour buses banned throughout. It asks visitors to come on foot, by bike or by bus and train, and large photo set-ups and drones need a permit.

**The trade-off:** it's two streets, not a landscape, and weekend crowds are the reason for the closures. Treat it as half a day on a Rhineland trip.

### Vancouver: a long season across the city

**When:** Vancouver has about 43,000 cherry trees of 54 varieties, according to the festival. Early varieties open in early March in a warm year, the pale pink Akebono in late March and the dark pink Kanzan a couple of weeks later, in April. Each tree blooms for about two weeks.

**The festival:** the Vancouver Cherry Blossom Festival says it will run from March to April 2027, with no dates yet. In 2026 it ran from 27 March to 12 April, with the Big Picnic at David Lam Park on 28–29 March and the Sakura Days Japan Fair at VanDusen Botanical Garden on 11–12 April (Destination Vancouver). Most events are free; the Japan Fair is the only ticketed one, and the festival's blossom maps show what is flowering where.

**The trade-off:** the trees are spread across the city, so you hunt them neighbourhood by neighbourhood rather than stand at one famous spot. Spring is damp, too: 106.4 mm (4.2 in) of precipitation in March and 85.5 mm (3.4 in) in April on average.

### Taiwan: Alishan and Wuling Farm

**Alishan:** the national forest recreation area, 2,200 to 2,700 m up, keeps the same flower season every year, 10 March to 10 April. Mountain cherries open it and Japanese varieties follow, Somei-yoshino among them. The forestry agency's spots are Zhaoping Park, the police station, the plaza in front of the Alishan House hotel and the trail from the workstation to the Sandai Tree. Entry is NT$300, or NT$150 with a bus or coach ticket stub. The Alishan Forest Railway from Chiayi has fully reopened, and by car the climb up Provincial Highway 18 takes under two hours. On ten weekend and holiday days in 2026, cars were barred from the top of that road from 6:00 to 11:00, and drivers switched to shuttle buses at the Leye service area (reported by TVBS).

**Wuling Farm:** the earliest big bloom of the lot. Its 2027 cherry-season control period runs from 13 February to 1 March, and coach bookings for travel agents opened on 10 November 2026 (Wuling Farm). In 2026 the farm capped entry at 6,000 people a day, only guests staying on the farm could drive their own cars in, with a farm-issued pass, and shuttle buses from Taipei cost NT$730 including entry (reported by the Commercial Times and CNA). The farm's lodge charges holiday rates every night from 21 January to 28 February 2027, and entry is NT$160, cash only.

**The trade-off:** Alishan's April is wet, 223.9 mm (8.8 in) on average, with nights around 8.4°C (47°F) (Central Weather Administration). Wuling means February mountain cold and a daily quota, and without a room on the farm you depend on the shuttles.

No authority sets booking windows, so this is our advice, with the official sale dates we found.

## Frequently asked questions

### When is cherry blossom season in Korea in 2027?

In an average year, late March in the south and early April in Seoul: the KMA's 1991–2020 averages for first flowers are 28 March in Busan and 8 April in Seoul. Recent springs have been earlier: Seoul flowered on 4 April 2025 and 29 March 2026.

### When is the Jinhae cherry blossom festival in 2027?

Not announced yet. Changwon city set the 2026 festival for 27 March to 5 April and the 2025 one for 29 March to 6 April.

### When will Washington DC's cherry blossoms peak in 2027?

Nobody knows yet. The NPS says peak bloom is most likely between the last week of March and the first week of April, and it published its 2026 prediction on 5 March. The festival runs from 20 March to 11 April 2027.

### When is the Valle del Jerte cherry blossom in 2027?

The valley's tourist office won't know until late February or early March. The bloom usually lasts about ten days between mid-March and early April; in 2026 it ran from 20 March to about 3 April.

### Where can I see cherry blossoms in Europe?

Spain's Valle del Jerte for a whole valley in flower, usually for about ten days between mid-March and early April, and Bonn's Altstadt for two streets of Japanese cherries, usually in mid-April.

### Is Korea a good alternative to Japan for cherry blossom?

It blooms in the same weeks as Tokyo and Kyoto (our [Japan guide](/blog/japan-cherry-blossom-season-guide) has their averages), and KTX trains link Seoul with Busan, Gyeongju and Changwon in three and a half hours or less. Our [Tokyo vs Seoul](/blog/tokyo-vs-seoul) comparison weighs the rest of the trip.
