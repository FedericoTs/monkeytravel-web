# Songkran 2027 in Thailand: Dates, Where to Celebrate and What to Expect

Songkran 2027 falls on Tuesday 13, Wednesday 14 and Thursday 15 April, the three Songkran holidays on the Bank of Thailand's official 2027 calendar. No bridge day has been announced, so unlike 2026 there is no built-in long weekend, and the big local festivals keep their own dates: Chiang Mai's ran for more than a week in 2026, and Pattaya's Wan Lai comes after the national days (18 and 19 April in 2026).

This guide is for anyone deciding whether to be in Thailand for the water festival, or to stay away from it. We found no city's 2027 programme yet, so for each place we give what happened in 2026 and say so.

Dates and rules were read on official pages on 21 November 2026; figures from the press or independent guides are marked. Times are Thailand time (ICT, UTC+7).

## When is Songkran 2027?

**The national holidays are Tuesday 13 to Thursday 15 April 2027.** The Bank of Thailand's [2027 holiday notice](https://www.bot.or.th/content/dam/bot/fipcs/documents/FPG/2569/EngPDF/25690175.pdf) (No. 37/2569, announced 17 August 2026) lists all three as "Songkran Festival", so banks close on each. Chakri Memorial Day, on Tuesday 6 April, is a holiday the week before.

**No bridge day so far.** Bangkok Biznews' [2027 calendar](https://www.bangkokbiznews.com/news/news-update/1260768) lists only the three days and suggests taking leave on Monday 12 and Friday 16 April to stretch them into nine. The Cabinet has added days before: Friday 12 April in 2024 ([reported by Bangkok Biznews](https://www.bangkokbiznews.com/business/economic/1112945)), and Wednesday 16 April in 2025, a substitute because the 13th fell on a Sunday ([reported by Thansettakij](https://www.thansettakij.com/economy/622849)). As of 21 November 2026 nothing is announced for 2027, and the bank's only extra holiday since its list is [16 December 2026](https://www.bot.or.th/en/news-and-media/news/news-20261117.html), for a royal cremation.

**In 2026** the holidays ran Monday 13 to Wednesday 15 April, and government offices closed on all three ([reported by Thairath](https://en.thairath.co.th/scoop/interview/2921634)). With the weekend, that made a five-day break from 11 April ([reported by The Nation](https://www.nationthailand.com/news/general/40064309)).

**Local festivals run on their own calendars.** In 2026 Hat Yai started on 10 April, Chiang Mai's programme ran for more than a week, and Chon Buri province spread its Wan Lai events from 5 to 20 April. The road-safety campaign known as the "seven dangerous days" has no 2027 dates yet; in 2026 it ran 10–16 April.

**It's UNESCO-listed.** "Songkran in Thailand, traditional Thai New Year festival" joined UNESCO's [Representative List of the Intangible Cultural Heritage of Humanity](https://ich.unesco.org/en/RL/songkran-in-thailand-traditional-thai-new-year-festival-01719) in 2023.

Once you've picked a base, [lay out your Songkran week](/trips/new) in the planner: it drafts the days, you change them by chatting, and you can share the plan with whoever's coming. It doesn't book anything, so trains, flights and rooms are yours to reserve.

## What actually happens at Songkran?

UNESCO describes Songkran as marking the sun's passage into Aries, the traditional start of the year. Pouring water stands for cleansing, reverence and good fortune, and people visit family and pay respects to older relatives, ancestors and Buddha images.

TAT's [guide to the customs](https://www.tatnews.org/2017/03/songkran-top-tips-for-enjoying-thailands-new-year-celebrations/) names the quiet ones first. Song Nam Phra is pouring scented water over Buddha images, on the body and never the head. In Rod Nam Dam Hua, children wash their parents' and elders' feet with scented water and receive a blessing. Temples fill with sand pagodas decorated with flags, and people make merit, sometimes by releasing birds or fish. TAT's guide says the gentle ceremony grew into the street water fight.

The two halves still keep different hours. Rituals happen in the morning: Chiang Mai's sacred Buddha image left its temple hall at 8:30 on 13 April 2026. Official water play mostly started at midday or later in 2026: 12:00 on Khao San Road, 13:00 on Silom Road and 15:00 on Patong Beach.

## Where to celebrate Songkran in 2027

Programmes appear late: in 2026 the government published its list of events on 31 March. So each section below gives what happened in 2026.

### Bangkok: Silom, Khao San and the river temples

**What happens:** Silom Road is the biggest street party. In 2026 the Bang Rak district office closed it from the Sala Daeng to the Nararom junction for water play from 13:00 to 21:00 on 12–14 April ([reported by The Nation](https://www.nationthailand.com/thailand/bangkok/40064979)). City cameras counted about 160,000 people coming and going on 13 April, against a previous high of about 100,000, and the governor ended the last day at 20:00 ([reported by The Nation](https://www.nationthailand.com/thailand/bangkok/40065055)).

Khao San Road is the backpacker version. In 2026 police allowed water play there only on 13–15 April, from 12:00 to 22:00, and screened people for powder and high-pressure water guns ([reported by The Nation](https://www.nationthailand.com/news/general/40064997)). TAT's own [Maha Songkran World Water Festival](https://www.tatnews.org/2026/04/thailand-to-host-maha-songkran-world-water-festival-2026-with-saneh-art-by-songkran-festival/) ran at Benchakitti Park on 11–15 April 2026, after moving from Sanam Luang ([reported by The Nation](https://www.nationthailand.com/news/tourism/40064821)).

**The temples and the quieter side:** in 2026 the Bangkok Water Festival ran free hop-on boats on 13–15 April between Wat Arun, Wat Kalayanamit, Wat Prayurawongsawat and Wat Pho, from 9:00 to 16:30 ([reported by Time Out Bangkok](https://www.timeout.com/bangkok/news/old-town-this-songkran-free-boat-rides-041026)). The city is emptier than usual too: expressway use in Bangkok fell by more than 5% as residents left ([reported by The Nation](https://www.nationthailand.com/news/general/40065198)).

**Ticketed parties:** SIAM Songkran has announced 10–13 April 2027 at Bravo BKK. Early-bird general-admission passes were 4,000 baht for three days and 4,900 baht for four, before fees and VAT ([reported by EDM House Network](https://edmhousenetwork.com/siam-songkran-reveals-dates-and-theme-for-2027-edition/), a music news site, read 20 November 2026). S2O, the other big one, hasn't announced 2027; it ran 11–13 April in 2026.

**The rules in 2026:** the city's Songkran measures prohibited alcohol sales and drinking, foam parties, smearing powder in public and high-pressure guns, and asked for modest dress ([reported by Thairath](https://en.thairath.co.th/news/politic/2926482), which doesn't say exactly where each rule applied).

**Where to stay and how far ahead:** Khao San's hotels were 98–100% full over Songkran 2025, according to its business association. In 2026, a weak year, hotels in the Silom, Surawong and Khao San zones expected 80–90% and the rest of the city about 70% ([reported by The Nation](https://www.nationthailand.com/news/tourism/40064821)). City Hall asked visitors to leave the BTS a stop before Sala Daeng to ease the crush. If you want to sleep, stay on a BTS or MRT line a few stops away from the zones.

**The trade-off:** the zones are packed shoulder to shoulder and you will be soaked within minutes. Bangkok is also where the exodus starts: on Saturday 11 April 2026 the state bus company carried a record 151,593 passengers, 88,598 of them leaving the city ([reported by Thairath](https://en.thairath.co.th/news/governmentpolicy/2926340)).

### Chiang Mai: the moat, the old city and the merit-making mornings

**What happens:** Chiang Mai celebrates the Lanna New Year, Pi Mai Mueang, over more than a week; The Nation listed the 2026 city programme as [6–17 April](https://www.nationthailand.com/news/tourism/40063916), and Thansettakij as [7–19 April](https://www.thansettakij.com/lifestyle/travel-shopping/655736). On 13 April 2026 the Phra Buddha Sihing image left its hall at Wat Phra Singh at 8:30 ([reported by Thai PBS](https://www.thaipbs.or.th/news/content/504562)). In the afternoon the procession of sacred images formed at Nawarat Bridge and moved along Tha Phae Road to Ratchadamnoen Road, at 14:09 by [Chiang Mai City Life's](https://www.chiangmaicitylife.com/citynews/general/monday-13th-songkran-schedule) schedule, an independent local magazine. On the afternoon of 14 April, a procession carried sand to the temples on Tha Phae Road.

The water fight runs along the old city's moat, and on 13 April 2026 Tha Phae Road closed for a street party from 17:00 to midnight. TAT's Chiang Mai Water Festival was at Wat Chedi Luang on 11–13 April.

**Getting there:** the overnight train from Bangkok's Krung Thep Aphiwat station takes about 12½ to 13 hours, and a second-class air-conditioned berth costs 728–1,038 baht ([Seat61](https://www.seat61.com/Thailand), an independent guide, listing 2023 fares). By 27 March 2026, every class on every regular train for the holiday had sold out ([reported by The Nation](https://www.nationthailand.com/news/general/40064309)). Since 24 April 2026 the state railway sells the longest journeys 90 days ahead ([reported by Thaiger](https://thethaiger.com/news/national/srt-shortens-train-ticket-booking-period-from-180-days-to-90-days)), so if that holds, seats for Saturday 10 April 2027 go on sale on 10 January 2027 (our calculation). Flying is the alternative.

**Where to stay and how far ahead:** inside the moat is the centre of it. Nimman isn't dry either: One Nimman ran its own Songkran on 11–15 April 2026, 15:00–22:00 (The Nation). Hotels are usually 80–90% full at Songkran; in 2026, a week before, bookings stood at 50–60%, according to the Chiang Mai Tourism Industry Council ([reported by Chiang Mai City Life](https://www.chiangmaicitylife.com/citynews/general/tourism-numbers-low-ahead-of-songkran/)).

**The trade-off:** heat and smoke. April is Chiang Mai's hottest month, with an average high of 36.7°C (98°F). It is also the end of the burning season: in April 2026, reports ranking Chiang Mai's PM2.5 among the worst in the world led to cancellations (Chiang Mai City Life), and some European visitors cut their stays short (reported by The Nation). Check the air before you commit.

### Phuket: Patong's party and an alcohol-free street in the Old Town

**What happens:** the [Phuket Water Festival](https://thailand.go.th/event-detail/phuket-water-festival-2026?hl=en) ran on Patong Beach on 11–13 April 2026, from 15:00 to 21:00 each day ([reported by The Nation](https://www.nationthailand.com/life/travel/40064915)). On the morning of 13 April, Wat Mai Khao in Thalang district held merit-making, Buddha-image bathing and water-pouring for elders. In Phuket Town, the Songkran No Alcohol Festival filled Dibuk Road on 13 April from 16:00 to 22:00, with a marked safety zone ([reported by The Nation](https://www.nationthailand.com/news/tourism/40063948)).

**Getting there and where to stay:** fly in, and choose Patong for the party or the Old Town for the local version. Phuket books early: in 2026 hotels were 70–75% booked, mostly by foreign guests who had reserved three to six months ahead. The Thai Hotels Association's southern chapter says Thai visitors normally fill the island to 100% at Songkran ([reported by The Nation](https://www.nationthailand.com/news/tourism/40064821)).

**The trade-off:** it's the wettest of the big cities in April, at 135 mm over almost ten rainy days on average, against 93 mm and five days in Bangkok. And the main festival lasted only three afternoons.

### Pattaya and Chon Buri: Wan Lai, the Songkran that comes later

**What happens:** Wan Lai is Chon Buri's own Songkran tradition, originally a merit-making gathering with Buddhist rites, sand pagodas, water play and folk games. In 2026 the province spread it across 5–20 April: Bang Saen on 16–17 April, Naklua and Ko Larn on 18 April, Pattaya itself on 19 April, and three days of water play and concerts on Pattaya Beach on 17–19 April ([reported by The Nation](https://www.nationthailand.com/news/tourism/40065105), citing TAT). The 2027 dates aren't announced. If Chon Buri keeps the same days, Naklua's would fall on Sunday 18 April and Pattaya's on Monday 19 April.

**Getting there:** one cheap way from Bangkok is train 283, third class, daily from Hualamphong at 6:55, reaching Pattaya at 10:34 for 31 baht with no reservation (Seat61).

**Where to stay and how far ahead:** Beach Road and Naklua are where the Wan Lai days happen. In 2026, Pattaya's hotels were 70% booked in the week before Songkran and expected about 80%, according to the Thai Hotels Association's eastern chapter ([reported by The Nation](https://www.nationthailand.com/news/tourism/40064821)).

**The trade-off:** if you arrive for the national days and stay for Wan Lai, you're wet for a week. That's also the strength: Pattaya is the easiest place to do Chiang Mai or Bangkok first and still catch a full-scale water fight after the 15th.

### Hat Yai: Midnight Songkran in the south

**What happens:** in 2026, Hat Yai Midnight Songkran ran 10–13 April in the city centre, with water play from midday to midnight and EDM stages, aimed especially at Malaysian visitors ([reported by Pattaya Mail](https://www.pattayamail.com/news/hat-yai-to-host-midnight-songkran-2026-with-major-edm-and-citywide-water-celebrations-541865), credited to TAT). Afterwards, Pattaya Mail reported attendance above 500,000 over the four days, without saying [who counted](https://www.pattayamail.com/thailandnews/hat-yai-midnight-songkran-draws-over-half-a-million-cements-southern-tourism-powerhouse-544005).

**Getting there:** fly, or take the overnight train from Bangkok, where a second-class air-conditioned berth costs 1,003–1,103 baht (Seat61).

**The trade-off:** it's far from the other hubs, and near areas where foreign offices advise against travel. The UK Foreign Office advises against all but essential travel to four districts of southern Songkhla, Chana, Thepa, Na Thawi and Saba Yoi, and on the Hat Yai–Padang Besar railway ([FCDO](https://www.gov.uk/foreign-travel-advice/thailand), updated 12 October 2026). Hat Yai itself isn't on that list. Check your own government's advice too.

### Khon Kaen: Khao Niao Road

**What happens:** Khon Kaen's festival ran 11–15 April 2026 around the Bueng Kaen Nakhon lake and on Si Chan Road. For the Khao Niao Road party (khao niao is sticky rice), officials closed Si Chan Road along its full length, nearly 3 km (2 miles), from the Pratu Muang to the Tiao Hong junction ([reported by Thairath](https://en.thairath.co.th/news/local/2926501)). Wat Chai Si held a separate water festival on 13–15 April.

**The trade-off:** the heat. Khon Kaen's April average high is 36.2°C (97°F), close to Chiang Mai's, so save the street for the late afternoon and evening.

### A quieter Songkran: temples, old towns and later dates

**Go in the morning.** Temple rituals come first and the water play later, so a morning at a temple is the calmest way in.

**Ayutthaya:** in 2026 the old capital held Songkran at Wat Chaiwatthanaram on 9–13 April, and elephants splashed the crowd on Sri Sanphet Road, in front of the TAT office, on 13–15 April.

**Sukhothai and Nan:** "Maha Songkran at Sukhothai" ran 6–19 April 2026 in Si Satchanalai and Mueang Sukhothai, and Nan held its festival on 12–15 April.

**Phra Pradaeng:** the Mon community's Songkran in Samut Prakan province, on Bangkok's southern edge, came well after the national days, on 24–26 April 2026.

**Islands:** we found no official list of islands that skip the water fights, so don't count on one. Ko Samui had an official festival on 12–15 April 2026 in the Bo Phut, Maret and Ang Thong subdistricts. April is one of its drier months, at 82.9 mm on average against 444.6 mm in November. Ko Chang held its Wan Lai on 19–21 April 2026.

**The trade-off:** nowhere is guaranteed dry, and the small programmes are announced last.

| 2026 | 10–16 April | 1,242 | 1,200 | 242 |

Reported by [InfoQuest](https://www.infoquest.co.th/?p=392197) (2024), [The Nation](https://www.nationthailand.com/news/general/40048949) (2025) and [Thai PBS](https://www.thaipbs.or.th/news/content/504716), the public broadcaster (2026). In 2025, motorcycles were involved in 84% of crashes. In 2026, Bangkok had the most deaths, 21, and speeding was the leading cause on the final day.

Don't rent a scooter that week. TAT's guide says to wear a helmet and not to ride into areas where water is being thrown, and in 2026 riding without one carried a 2,000-baht fine for rider and passenger alike, and drink-driving up to 20,000 baht ([reported by The Nation](https://www.nationthailand.com/news/general/40064997)).

April has the highest average high of the year in Bangkok, Chiang Mai, Khon Kaen, Hat Yai and Pattaya. In Phuket, March is a fraction hotter, and on Ko Samui, May. Bangkok's April nights average 27.2°C (81°F), so there's little relief after dark. Plan the hottest hours indoors or in the shade.

### Alcohol: new hours, new fines

- **Hours:** since 29 May 2026, shops and restaurants may sell alcohol from 11:00 to midnight, and the old afternoon ban from 14:00 to 17:00 is gone. Hotels, licensed entertainment venues and international airport terminals follow their own rules ([TAT](https://www.tatnews.org/2026/05/alcohol-sales-and-consumption-rules-updated-in-thailand-what-tourists-need-to-know/), on the Royal Gazette notice).
- **Fines for drinkers:** since 8 November 2025, customers drinking in restaurants or venues at banned times can be fined up to 10,000 baht ([reported by The Nation](https://www.nationthailand.com/news/general/40058091)). The health ministry said in November 2025 that it would start with warnings while the detailed rules were written.
- **Water zones:** these set their own bans. In 2026 Bangkok's Songkran measures prohibited alcohol sales and drinking, Phuket Town's Dibuk Road party was alcohol-free by design, and the nationwide list of rules left the rest to each area's announcement.

### Closures: banks, offices and embassies

Banks close on 13–15 April 2027. In 2026, government offices closed on the same days, and so did some embassies: Denmark's in Bangkok, for example, [closed 13–15 April](https://um.dk/thailand/en/news/closing-days-april-2026/) and also on 6 April. Do bank errands, visa matters and any immigration paperwork before the 13th.

That matters more now: since 15 September 2026, tourists from the 60 countries and territories on the visa-free list get 30 days, not 60. [TAT lists them](https://www.tatnews.org/2026/09/thailand-introduces-new-30-day-and-15-day-visa-exemption-rules-from-15-september/); Seychelles and Mauritius get 15 days. Brazil, Argentina, Chile, Peru, Mexico and Colombia aren't on that list: TAT says separate bilateral arrangements still apply where relevant, so check the Thai embassy's page for your passport.

### Packed transport

In 2026, public transport carried 20.1 million journeys between 10 and 17 April, 45% of them by rail ([reported by The Nation](https://www.nationthailand.com/news/general/40065198)). The state bus company expected about 120,000 passengers a day coming back on 16–19 April and planned 1,200 extra buses. Rail tickets sell out first, as above. With the 2027 holidays in midweek, expect the rush out on the weekend before and the rush back after the 15th; that's our reading of 2026's pattern, not a forecast.

### Phones, documents and clothes

TAT's advice is to keep phones, wallets and cameras sealed in plastic bags, and for women to be wary of tight, light-coloured or thin clothes. A waterproof pouch on a lanyard keeps a phone usable for maps and payments, and your passport belongs in a sealed bag too. Expect anything in a pocket to get wet.

### Respect: who not to splash

TAT's rules are short: "Never splash people on motorcycles, monks or pregnant women." Leave alone strangers who are clearly trying to stay out of it. Use clean water, skip large pieces of ice, and ask before putting powder on anyone, away from the eyes. With elders, the custom is a gentle pour of scented water, not a soaking.

In 2026, splashing people who didn't want it carried fines of up to 60,000 baht, powder without consent 5,000 baht, high-pressure water guns up to 4,000 baht and sexual harassment up to 200,000 baht ([reported by The Nation](https://www.nationthailand.com/news/general/40064997)).

## How to plan a week around Songkran 2027

This sample week assumes 2027 follows 2026's pattern; check the programmes in March.

- **Thursday 8 April:** arrive in Bangkok. Spend the afternoon at Wat Pho and Wat Arun before the crowds.
- **Friday 9 April:** overnight train to Chiang Mai. If the 90-day rule holds, tickets go on sale on 9 January 2027.
- **Saturday 10 to Thursday 15 April:** Chiang Mai. Temples and the old city first, the processions on the 13th and 14th, the moat in the afternoons, and one day out of the city if the air is bad.
- **Friday 16 April:** fly back to Bangkok and go on to Pattaya.
- **Saturday 17 to Monday 19 April:** Pattaya for Wan Lai on the beach, Naklua and the town, if the dates repeat.
- **Tuesday 20 April:** fly home from Bangkok.

For a shorter trip, stay in Bangkok: Benchakitti Park and the river temples on the weekend of 10–11 April, Silom or Khao San on the 13th, and Ayutthaya's elephants for a day. To miss Songkran instead, avoid roughly 10–19 April, the stretch the Transport Ministry planned for as the holiday rush in 2026 ([reported by The Nation](https://www.nationthailand.com/news/policy/40064245)), and the Chon Buri coast until about the 20th.

[Build this week day by day](/trips/new) and swap Chiang Mai for Phuket or Hat Yai if you'd rather have the beach or the night party; the plan updates as you chat.

## What to book first, and by when

No authority sets booking windows for Songkran, so this is our advice from 2026's numbers.

1. **Now:** flights and rooms, above all Khao San, Silom, Phuket and Chiang Mai's old city. Phuket's foreign guests booked three to six months ahead in 2026. SIAM Songkran's early-bird sale was due to open on 29 October 2026.
2. **9 to 13 January 2027:** long-distance train tickets for 9–13 April, 90 days ahead from 8:30 each morning ([Time Out Bangkok](https://www.timeout.com/bangkok/news/the-state-railway-of-thailand-new-year-train-tickets-092426) explains the windows), if the current rule holds.
3. **March 2027:** event programmes, which came out on 31 March in 2026. Check Chiang Mai's air quality at the same time.
4. **Before 13 April:** money, visa and any office errands.
5. **Within three days before you land:** the free Thailand Digital Arrival Card, which every foreign visitor must file online at tdac.immigration.go.th ([Royal Thai Embassy guidance](https://buenos-aires.thaiembassy.org/en/content/how-to-submit-the-thailand-digital-arrival-card-td)). Ignore sites that charge for it.

## Frequently asked questions

### When is Songkran 2027?

Tuesday 13 to Thursday 15 April 2027, the three Songkran holidays on the Bank of Thailand's 2027 list. Local festivals start earlier or end later.

### Is there a long weekend for Songkran 2027?

Not on the official list. The holidays fall midweek and no bridge day has been announced. Taking Monday 12 and Friday 16 April off gives you nine days.

### When is Pattaya's Wan Lai in 2027?

Not announced yet. In 2026 it was 18 April in Naklua and 19 April in Pattaya, with beach events on 17–19 April.

### How long does Songkran last in Chiang Mai?

More than a week. In 2026 the city programme was listed as 6–17 April (one report said 7–19), with the big processions on 13 and 14 April and water play along the moat.

### Is Songkran a good time to visit Thailand?

Yes, if you want the festival and can take 35°C heat and full trains. If you came for temples, beaches and quiet roads, the week is harder work: book everything early, and give the temple days to the mornings.

### Can I avoid getting wet?

Only by staying out of the zones, and even then not reliably. Mornings, temples and places with no listed event are your best bet; many malls ran their own water events in 2026.

### Is alcohol banned during Songkran?

Not nationwide. Normal sales hours are 11:00 to midnight, but water zones can ban alcohol, as Bangkok's rules and Phuket Town's party did in 2026.
