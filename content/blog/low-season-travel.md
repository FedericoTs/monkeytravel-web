# Low Season 2027: When It Is, Month by Month, in Spain and Around the World

In Spain, low season in 2027 means January and February above all, then March before Holy Week, November and early December. In 2025, the latest full year that INE (Spain's National Statistics Institute) has published, January had both the cheapest hotel rooms, €112.69 a night on average, and the emptiest beds, 47.5% filled, against €155.52 and 75.3% in August. The exceptions are big: the Canaries are cheapest in May and June, Madrid and Seville in August, and the ski resorts in spring and autumn.

There is no single official low season. Each hotel, airline and tourist board draws its own line, so this guide uses what can be measured: INE's monthly hotel figures, Eurostat's tourist nights for the EU, Spain's official 2027 holiday list in the BOE, the regional school calendars, and official tourism and weather services abroad. Everything was read on 20 November 2026.

Room prices are INE's average daily rate: what hotels actually took per occupied double room per night, without taxes or extras ([INE methodology](https://www.ine.es/metodologia/t11/meto_irsh.pdf)). INE still marks October to December 2025 as provisional, shown with an asterisk (*).

## What does low season actually mean?

Low season is when demand, and with it prices, is at its lowest, and every business decides where its own begins. INE publishes no seasons, only monthly numbers: how full hotels were and what a room really cost. That makes it the best guide to when Spain is cheap and quiet.

A few official calendars do draw a line, and they disagree with each other:

- **The Balearic tourist tax** defines low season as 1 November to 30 April and charges 75% less then ([Law 2/2016, article 13](https://www.boe.es/buscar/act.php?id=BOE-A-2016-4175)).
- **IMSERSO**, the state's subsidised holiday programme for older people, runs its 2026–27 trips from roughly October 2026 to June 2027 to help [spread tourism across the year](https://www.dsca.gob.es/es/comunicacion/notas-prensa/viajes-imserso-podran-reservarse-partir-14-septiembre-0). Its [price list](https://www.boe.es/diario_boe/txt.php?id=BOE-A-2026-12865) adds €100 in October, May and June on the mainland coasts and the Balearics, but in December, January and February in the Canaries.
- **Italian coastal hotels** each publish their own price bands; our [guide to Italy's high and low season](/blog/alta-e-bassa-stagione-2026) explains how to read them.

One INE figure needs a warning. Its hotel price index (IPH) [can't be compared from one month to the next](https://www.ine.es/en/daco/daco42/prechote/meto_iph_base2008_en.pdf), only with the same month a year earlier. So this guide compares months with the average daily rate, which INE's methodology says is meant to reflect each month's real situation.

## When is low season in Spain in 2027?

For Spain as a whole, January is the bottom: the cheapest rooms (€112.69), the emptiest beds (47.5%) and the fewest hotels open, 11,464 against 17,067 in July ([INE ADR](https://www.ine.es/jaxiT3/Tabla.htm?t=2059), [INE occupancy](https://www.ine.es/jaxiT3/Tabla.htm?t=2066)). February (€113.10) and March (€113.58) cost almost the same, April (€116.59) and November (€117.09*) come next, and December was the second-emptiest month (49.4%). The 2026 months published so far repeat the pattern: January averaged €116.04 and August €166.90 (provisional).

### The mainland coasts: January and February, then November

Every mainland beach zone in the table is cheapest in January or February and dearest in August. Rooms on the Costa del Sol averaged €91.83 in February 2025, 56% less than August's €208.19; the gap was 50% on the Costa Brava and 67% on the Costa de la Luz (our calculations). March is nearly as cheap, and was the cheapest month of all on the Costa de Almería (€63.78) and in Lloret de Mar (€64.37).

Of the mainland beach zones in the table, the Costa del Sol and the Costa Blanca stay the most alive. Two thirds of their summer hotels were open in January 2025 (351 of 527, and 350 of 513), and Málaga and València average January highs of 16.8°C and 16.4°C (62°F), with 180 and 171 hours of sun ([AEMET Málaga](https://www.aemet.es/es/serviciosclimaticos/datosclimatologicos/valoresclimatologicos?l=6155A&k=and), [AEMET València](https://www.aemet.es/es/serviciosclimaticos/datosclimatologicos/valoresclimatologicos?l=8416&k=val), 1981–2010 normals).

**The trade-off:** elsewhere on the coast, many hotels close for the winter, and the Costa Brava closes most: only a quarter of its summer hotels were open in January (157 of 627), and INE published no figures at all for Salou from November to March. Lloret de Mar had 13 hotels open in February against 102 in July. In the south, late autumn is wetter: Málaga averages 100 mm of rain in November and again in December.

### The Balearics: April and October beat January

The islands were cheapest in February 2025, at €89.06 a night, but that average comes from very few hotels. INE counted 133 hotels open across the Balearics in January against 1,381 in August and September ([INE](https://www.ine.es/jaxiT3/Tabla.htm?t=2066)); its separate table for Palma counts 85 open there in January against 226 in summer ([INE Palma](https://www.ine.es/jaxiT3/Tabla.htm?t=2076)).

The better low season is the edge of summer. In April 2025, 702 hotels were open and rooms averaged €112.34; in October, 1,119 were open at €118.40*. That is 47% and 44% below August's €211.23 (our calculations). Ibiza and Formentera were cheapest in March: €76.57, against €266.23 in August.

The tourist tax adds a saving. From 1 November to 30 April it is cut by 75%, so the €3 a person a night charged at a four-star hotel becomes €0.75 (both plus 10% VAT), and under-16s pay nothing ([Law 2/2016](https://www.boe.es/buscar/act.php?id=BOE-A-2016-4175), as it stood on 20 November 2026; VAT from the [Balearic government's calculator](https://illessostenibles.travel/es/calculadora-its)). Proposals to change the tax had not been approved by then. For two adults over a week, that is €10.50 instead of €42 before VAT (our calculation).

**The trade-off:** January and February are cheap because they are closed. Mallorca had 102 hotels open in January against 961 in September, and INE published no figures for Ciutadella, in Menorca, from November to March. Palma averages 15.2°C (59°F) by day in January and 3.8°C at night ([AEMET](https://www.aemet.es/es/serviciosclimaticos/datosclimatologicos/valoresclimatologicos?l=B278&k=bal)).

### The Canary Islands: May and June

The Canaries run the other way, because their high season is winter. Rooms averaged €161.83* in December 2025, the year's dearest month, and IMSERSO charges its high-season supplement there from December to February. The cheapest month was June (€118.98) and the emptiest May (66.2% of beds filled), on both Tenerife and Gran Canaria too. June 2026 was again the cheapest month so far, at €124.56 (provisional).

The weather hardly changes: Tenerife Sur averages 23.9°C (75°F) by day in May and 25.4°C in June, with almost no rain ([AEMET](https://www.aemet.es/es/serviciosclimaticos/datosclimatologicos/valoresclimatologicos?l=C429I&k=coo)).

**The trade-off:** the saving is much smaller than on the mainland coasts. June was about 26% cheaper than December (our calculation), and the islands were still two-thirds full in their emptiest month. Hotels stay open all year, between 561 and 594 of them, but they are never empty, and August, in the middle of Spain's school holidays, was the fullest month (82.2%).

### Cities: Madrid and Seville in August, Barcelona and Valencia in winter

Cities keep their own calendars, and the cheapest month isn't always the emptiest. In 2025:

- **Madrid** was cheapest in August (€138.79 in the city) and emptiest in January (53.0%). August cost 32% less than October (€202.87*), the dearest month (our calculation).
- **Seville** was cheapest in August (€88.69), 52% below May (€183.43), and July (€103.13) came second. January was the emptiest month (55.1%).
- **Barcelona** was cheapest in December (€131.41*) and January (€132.93), about 43% below May (€231.81).
- **Valencia** was cheapest and emptiest in January (€78.96 and 48.2%), 41% below May.
- **Granada** and **Córdoba** were cheapest in August (€72.78) and July (€60.89).

Weekends fill far faster than weekdays. In January 2025 Seville's hotels were 55.1% full across the month but 66.8% at weekends, and Granada's 49.5% and 64.1%; in November, Toledo's were 46.9% and 67.2% ([INE](https://www.ine.es/jaxiT3/Tabla.htm?t=2076)).

**The trade-off:** summer bargains come with heat. Seville averages 36.0°C (97°F) by day in July and 35.5°C in August, and Madrid 32.1°C and 31.3°C ([AEMET Seville](https://www.aemet.es/es/serviciosclimaticos/datosclimatologicos/valoresclimatologicos?l=5783&k=and), [AEMET Madrid](https://www.aemet.es/es/serviciosclimaticos/datosclimatologicos/valoresclimatologicos?l=3195&k=mad)). The Madrid region also had its fewest hotels open in August (1,063). Winter is cool and short instead: Madrid gets 149 hours of sun in January against 355 in July.

### The mountains: spring and late autumn

Ski areas are dearest in winter and cheapest between seasons. In the Vall d'Aran, where Baqueira is, rooms averaged €65.15 in May 2025 against €188.09* in December; in the Aragonese Pyrenees, €77.24* in November against €121.63* in December. The Aragonese Pyrenees were emptiest in November (25.2% of beds) and April (30.0%).

**The trade-off:** the high resorts largely shut between seasons. INE published no figures for Naut Aran (Baqueira) or Sallent de Gállego (Formigal) in May, October or November 2025, and in April the Naut Aran hotels still open were only 20% full. Summer is a second high season: August was the Aragonese Pyrenees' fullest month (65.7%).

### The green north: January and February

Cantabria, Galicia and Asturias had the biggest swings in hotel occupancy of any Spanish region in 2025, in relative terms: Cantabria's hotels were 24.1% full in January and 74.8% in August ([INE](https://www.ine.es/jaxiT3/Tabla.htm?t=2066)). Asturias's Costa Verde averaged €58.05 a room in January against €127.78 in August, with 23.9% of beds filled. San Sebastián cost €90.00 in January and €252.00 in August, 64% less (our calculation).

**The trade-off:** rain and dark. San Sebastián averages 141 mm of rain on 13 days in January and 98 hours of sun ([AEMET](https://www.aemet.es/es/serviciosclimaticos/datosclimatologicos/valoresclimatologicos?l=1024E&k=pva)), and fewer than half of the Costa Verde's summer hotels were open (155 of 342).

## How much cheaper is low season?

On INE's 2025 averages, about 28% cheaper for Spain as a whole: €112.69 a night in January against €155.52 in August. Beach areas save far more, cities less, and the Canaries least:

- **Coasts:** January or February cost half of August or less: 50% less on the Costa Brava, 52% on the Costa Blanca, 56% on the Costa del Sol and the Costa Daurada, and 67% on the Costa de la Luz.
- **Islands:** 58% less across the Balearics in February and 71% less in Ibiza and Formentera in March; 26% less in the Canaries in June than in December.
- **Cities:** 32% less in Madrid in August than in October, 43% less in Barcelona in December than in May, and 52% less in Seville in August than in May.
- **Mountains:** 65% less in the Vall d'Aran in May than in December.

All are our calculations from INE's average daily rates. Two cautions. Winter averages describe the hotels that stay open, not the same hotels at a lower price. And prices are rising in every season: INE's hotel price index, which compares like with like, was 6.7% higher in September 2026 than a year earlier ([INE](https://www.ine.es/jaxiT3/Tabla.htm?t=12156), provisional), so expect 2027's low season to cost more than these 2025 figures.

Published seasonal prices show the same gap. IMSERSO's 2026–27 list adds a flat €100 in high season: a 10-day stay on the mainland coast with transport costs €309.22 from November to April and €409.22 in October, May and June, and a four-day trip to a provincial capital €132.91 or €232.91 ([BOE](https://www.boe.es/diario_boe/txt.php?id=BOE-A-2026-12865), read 20 November 2026).

Airlines publish no seasonal fare tables, so we give no flight figure; our [guide to cheap flights](/blog/cheapest-flights-2026-when-and-where-to-book) covers when to book.

## When is low season in the rest of the world?

Across the EU, January 2025 had 4.5% of the year's tourist nights and August 16.3%, 3.6 times as many ([Eurostat](https://ec.europa.eu/eurostat/databrowser/view/tour_occ_nim/default/table)). Further away, low season follows rain and storms more than the calendar.

### Portugal: November to March

Portugal's year looks like Spain's: January had 4.4% of its 2025 tourist nights and August 14.1% ([Eurostat](https://ec.europa.eu/eurostat/databrowser/view/tour_occ_nim/default/table)). The Algarve stays mild, with Faro averaging 16.1°C (61°F) by day in January ([WMO](https://worldweather.wmo.int/en/city.html?cityId=4), 1961–1990 normals).

**The trade-off:** it is the wet half of the year. Faro averages 94 mm of rain on 11 days in December.

### Italy: November, January and February

Italy's quietest month in 2025 was November, with 3.7% of the year's nights against 17.9% in August, and January and February were almost as quiet ([Eurostat](https://ec.europa.eu/eurostat/databrowser/view/tour_occ_nim/default/table)). On the coast, each hotel sets its own bands: see our [guide to Italy's high and low season](/blog/alta-e-bassa-stagione-2026).

**The trade-off:** November is the wettest month. Rome averages 115 mm of rain on about 10 days ([WMO](https://worldweather.wmo.int/en/city.html?cityId=201), 1971–2000 normals).

### France: November and January, outside the school holidays

France's quietest month in 2025 was November, with 3.9% of the year's nights; August had 19.3% ([Eurostat](https://ec.europa.eu/eurostat/databrowser/view/tour_occ_nim/default/table)). French school holidays come in three zones. In 2027 the winter break runs from 6 February to 7 March across them (Paris, in zone C, 6–21 February), and the spring break from 3 April to 2 May (Paris 3–18 April) ([Ministry of Education open data](https://data.education.gouv.fr/explore/dataset/fr-en-calendrier-scolaire/)).

**The trade-off:** the cheap months are cold. Paris averages 6.9°C (44°F) by day in January ([WMO](https://worldweather.wmo.int/en/city.html?cityId=194), 1971–2000 normals), and skiers should avoid the zones' February weeks.

### The Caribbean and Mexico: June to November

Here low season is the hurricane season. In the Atlantic it runs from 1 June to 30 November and peaks on 10 September, with most activity from mid-August to mid-October; on Mexico's Pacific side it starts on 15 May ([NOAA National Hurricane Center](https://www.nhc.noaa.gov/climo/)). Rain peaks in the same weeks: Cancún averages 225 mm in September and 221 mm on 16 days in October ([WMO](https://worldweather.wmo.int/en/city.html?cityId=1209), 1971–2000), and Punta Cana's wettest month is October, with 152 mm ([WMO](https://worldweather.wmo.int/en/city.html?cityId=596), 1961–1990).

Hotels feel it. Cancún's were 61.1% full in September 2025, against 72.3% on average from January to September ([DataTur](https://www.datatur.sectur.gob.mx/SitePages/hoteleria.aspx), Mexico's tourism ministry, preliminary figures).

**The trade-off:** one storm can spoil a week. Choose insurance that covers weather cancellations, and keep the first and last days of the trip flexible.

### Thailand and South-East Asia: the monsoon

Thailand's rainy season runs from mid-May to mid-October, and the south keeps getting heavy rain until December, more on the east (Gulf) side ([Thai government](https://thailand.go.th/issue-focus-detail/009_142)). Phuket averages 387 mm of rain on 23 days in September, against 23 mm in January ([WMO](https://worldweather.wmo.int/en/city.html?cityId=579)). Bali runs the other way: its wettest months are December to March, with 345 mm on 27 days in January ([WMO](https://worldweather.wmo.int/en/city.html?cityId=649), 1961–1990).

Our [monsoon guide](/blog/monsoon-season-where-to-go-and-avoid) maps it country by country, and our [budget guide to Asia](/blog/cheapest-destinations-in-asia) has daily costs.

**The trade-off:** the Thai government warns that the south's late-year rain can cause flooding, so check the forecast before booking islands in November and December.

### Japan: February, June and September

Japan's foreign visitors are spread over the year, but [February 2025](https://asset.japan.travel/image/upload/v1742444812/pdf/Number_of_visitor_arrivals_to_Japan_in_February_2025.pdf) had the fewest (3.26 million), with [September](https://asset.japan.travel/image/upload/v1760931871/pdf/Number_of_visitor_arrivals_to_Japan_in_September_2025.pdf) (3.27 million) and [June](https://asset.japan.travel/image/upload/v1752737386/pdf/Number_of_visitor_arrivals_to_Japan_in_June_2025.pdf) (3.38 million) next, against 3.91 million in [April](https://asset.japan.travel/image/upload/v1747966481/pdf/Number_of_visitor_arrivals_to_Japan_in_April_2025.pdf) and 42.68 million in the [whole year](https://asset.japan.travel/image/upload/v1769050010/pdf/Number_of_visitor_arrivals_to_Japan_in_December_2025.pdf) (JNTO's preliminary figures). June is the rainy season: around Tokyo it normally starts about 7 June and ends about 19 July ([JMA](https://www.data.jma.go.jp/cpd/longfcst/seasonal/202608/tsuyu2026.pdf)). Avoid Golden Week, 29 April to 5 May 2027, when Japan itself travels ([Cabinet Office](https://www8.cao.go.jp/chosei/shukujitsu/gaiyou.html)).

**The trade-off:** February is cold, with Tokyo averaging 10.9°C (52°F) by day, and September is wet, with 225 mm of rain ([WMO](https://worldweather.wmo.int/en/city.html?cityId=183), 1991–2020).

### The United States: winter, outside the holidays

In the national parks, winter is the quiet season. Grand Canyon had 191,862 visits in January 2025 against 508,642 in May, and Yosemite 120,601 in February against 616,551 in July ([National Park Service](https://irma.nps.gov/Stats/)). Avoid Thanksgiving week (Thursday 25 November 2027 is the federal holiday) and Christmas ([OPM](https://www.opm.gov/policy-data-oversight/pay-leave/federal-holidays/)).

**The trade-off:** some park roads close for the winter. Yosemite's Tioga Road and Glacier Point Road usually reopen only in late May or June ([NPS](https://www.nps.gov/yose/planyourvisit/conditions.htm)). New York averages 3.5°C (38°F) by day in January ([WMO](https://worldweather.wmo.int/en/city.html?cityId=278), 1981–2010), and Florida shares the Caribbean's hurricane season.

## How to plan a low-season trip in 2027

1. **Pick the place's own low month.** Madrid in August, the Canaries in June and the Pyrenees in May are all low season; the coasts in August are not.
2. **Check the 2027 dates above** for long weekends and school holidays where you're going, and at home if you travel with children.
3. **Travel midweek.** In January 2025 Spain's hotels were 47.5% full across the month and 52.4% at weekends, and the gap was wider in cities such as Seville and Granada.
4. **Check what's open before you book.** In the Balearics and on the Costa Brava most hotels close in winter, and the ski resorts empty out between seasons, so confirm that your hotel, and anything you plan around, is open on your dates.
5. **Plan around the light.** Madrid gets 124 hours of sun in December, about a third of July's 355, so put outdoor sights in the middle of the day.
6. **Put it on paper.** [Lay out your days](/trips/new) in the planner, change them by chatting and share them with whoever is coming. It doesn't book anything.

## Frequently asked questions

### When is low season in 2027?

In Spain, from the end of the Christmas school holidays (7 to 11 January, depending on the region) to mid-March, and from November to early December, outside the long weekends. The Canaries' low season is May and June, and Madrid's and Seville's is August. Abroad, it is roughly November to March in Europe, June to November in the Caribbean and Mexico, and mid-May to mid-October in Thailand.

### What are the cheapest months to travel in 2027?

For Spanish hotels, January, February and March. In 2025 they averaged €112.69, €113.10 and €113.58 a night, against €155.52 in August, and April (€116.59) and November (€117.09, provisional) came next.

### When is low season in the Canary Islands?

May and June. In 2025 May had the fewest beds filled (66.2%) and June the cheapest rooms (€118.98). December was the dearest month.

### Is September low season in Spain?

Not on the coast. September 2025 was Spain's third-dearest hotel month (€131.84), with 66.8% of beds filled. Prices drop after September, most of all on the mainland coasts and in the Balearics, and the real low season starts in November.

### Is everything closed in low season?

Not in Madrid or the Canaries, where the number of open hotels barely changes through the year. In the Balearics about one hotel in ten was open in January 2025, and on the Costa Brava one in four.

### When are Spain's school holidays in 2027?

Children go back after Christmas on 7 January in the Valencia region, 8 January in Catalonia and 11 January in Madrid. Easter breaks run 19–29 March in Madrid, 20–29 March in Catalonia and 25 March–5 April in the Valencia region, and schools close on 18 June in Madrid and the Valencia region and 21 June in Catalonia.

### When is low season in the Caribbean?

From June to November, the Atlantic hurricane season, which peaks on 10 September.
