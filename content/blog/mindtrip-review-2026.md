# Mindtrip Review 2026: Features, Pricing & the Best Alternatives

Mindtrip is, on paper, one of the most complete AI travel tools of 2026 — a database Mindtrip says covers over 11 million points of interest, in-app booking, a place on Fast Company's Most Innovative Companies 2025 list, and a maps integration most competitors still get wrong. It's also free. So what's the catch, and is it the right tool for *you*?

We tested Mindtrip on real trips across solo, couple, and group scenarios. Here's the honest read — what it does genuinely well, where it frustrates, and the best alternatives if you plan in a group or would rather not create an account.

We're not affiliated with Mindtrip and earn nothing from it. MonkeyTravel is one of the alternatives below, and we'll be straight about where Mindtrip beats us.

## Quick Verdict

Mindtrip is the most complete free AI travel tool if you want to research, plan, and book in one interface. The 11M+ point database (Mindtrip's own figure) and maps are the real deal, and there's no paywall on planning — reservations are handed to third-party partners inside the app.

Its limits are specific: you create an account before you plan, and — like most tools here — it's built around solo and couple planning, with collaborative editing rather than a way for a group to actually *decide*.

## Is Mindtrip Free? (2026 Pricing)

Yes — genuinely. Mindtrip has **no premium tier, no message limits, and no locked core features**. You plan for free; when you book, the reservation is handled by third-party partners such as Priceline and Viator. How Mindtrip earns from those bookings isn't documented, so we won't guess.

| Plan | Price | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Mindtrip | Free | No paywall on planning features; reservations handled by third-party partners (Priceline, Viator) |

That's a real strength. Where Layla's free/paid line has moved during 2026 (early-2026 reviews put the day-by-day behind its ~$49/year premium; Layla's own June-2026 copy says generation is free and premium buys live pricing), Mindtrip's full planning experience is simply free.

## Where Mindtrip Wins

**The data depth.** Mindtrip says its database covers over 11 million points of interest, plus insight from tens of thousands of local experts, and in practice its recommendations are grounded in real places, not just model guesses. For accuracy on what actually exists where, it's near the top of the category.

**Research and booking in one place.** You can go from "where should I go" to a booked hotel without leaving the app — Priceline and Viator integrations keep confirmations and receipts in one organized spot.

**Maps done right.** Seeing your itinerary plotted geographically — with photos, reviews, and tips — is something most AI planners still fumble. Mindtrip nails it.

**Nice extras.** The Magic Camera feature (real-time photo translation and insights) and Google Docs-style collaborative editing round out a genuinely polished product.

## Where Mindtrip Falls Short

**An account before you plan.** Mindtrip asks you to sign in before it shows you an itinerary. If you'd rather see the output first and decide afterwards, that's real friction.

**Collaboration, not decision-making.** Multiple people can edit a trip together, but there's no structured way for a group to *vote* and settle disagreements. For a group of friends who each want something different, co-editing a doc isn't the same as deciding.

### 1. MonkeyTravel — for groups and multilingual travelers

[MonkeyTravel](/trips/new) matches Mindtrip on the thing most people actually want — a free, full day-by-day itinerary with real venue names and budget tiers — and adds the one thing Mindtrip doesn't have: a way for a group to *decide*.

- **Four languages.** Plan in English, Spanish, Italian, or Portuguese. Mindtrip covers these too (its app lists eight languages), so call this parity, not an edge.
- **Group voting.** When your travel companions can't agree, everyone votes on the options and the plan settles itself. Mindtrip lets you co-edit; MonkeyTravel lets you *decide*. See our [group trip planner](/group-trip-planner) guide for how that works.

And there's no signup wall — you can generate a full plan in about 30 seconds without an account.

Where Mindtrip still wins: real-time data depth and in-app booking. MonkeyTravel is planning-focused and doesn't book flights or hotels, so if booking-in-one-place is your must-have, stay with Mindtrip.

### 2. Layla — if you want live pricing

If catching a fare drop matters more than breadth, Layla's premium tier (about $49/year) offers live Skyscanner and Booking.com pricing plus PriceLock drop alerts. Two things to know: where Layla's free tier stops has moved during 2026 (early-2026 reviews put the day-by-day behind the paywall; Layla's own June-2026 copy says generation is free), so check before you count on it — and Expedia Group acquired Layla on 31 July 2026, so a planner owned by a booking company now has an interest in where your bookings land. Full breakdown in our [Layla AI review](/blog/layla-ai-review-2026).

### 3. Wonderplan — for a fast budget plan

Free, budget-first, PDF export. It's more template than deep personalization, but for a single-destination trip you want quickly, it's fine.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is Mindtrip really free?

Yes. Mindtrip has no premium tier and no locked core planning features. Hotels, activities, and — since May 2026 — flights are booked through third-party partners inside the app (Priceline, Viator, and Sabre with PayPal for flights); how Mindtrip earns from that isn't documented.

### What's the best alternative to Mindtrip?

For group trips and no-signup planning, [MonkeyTravel](/trips/new) is the strongest free alternative — it adds group voting and works without an account (it plans in English, Spanish, Italian, and Portuguese, as Mindtrip also does). For live flight and hotel pricing, Layla. For a quick budget plan, Wonderplan.

### Is Mindtrip or Layla better?

Mindtrip is free and stronger on data breadth and in-app booking; Layla is stronger on live pricing and fare-drop alerts, though where its free tier stops has moved during 2026 (premium is ~$49/year), and it has been owned by Expedia Group since 31 July 2026. If you don't want to pay, Mindtrip (or MonkeyTravel). If you want fare tracking, Layla premium.

### Does Mindtrip work in other languages?

Yes. As of September 2026 Mindtrip's app lists eight languages — English, Dutch, French, German, Italian, Norwegian Bokmål, Portuguese, and Spanish — so Spanish, Italian, and Portuguese are covered. MonkeyTravel plans in English, Spanish, Italian, and Portuguese.

### Does Mindtrip require an account?

Yes — Mindtrip asks you to sign in before you plan, not just to save and book. If you want to generate a full itinerary with no account at all, MonkeyTravel lets you plan anonymously first.
