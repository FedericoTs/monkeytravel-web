# Valentine's Day 2027 Getaways: Where to Go for the 12–15 February Weekend

Valentine's Day 2027 is a Sunday, so the natural trip runs from Friday 12 to Monday 15 February, which is a long weekend in the US (Presidents' Day) and in several Canadian provinces (Family Day). For the day itself, go to Verona or to Terni, Saint Valentine's own town; for a city break, Paris, Lisbon or Budapest; for warmth, Gran Canaria (its carnival is still on), Mexico's Riviera Maya or Udaipur. For snow, go to Finnish Lapland, or to Québec City, where the winter carnival ends on the 14th.

Every date, price and opening time below was read on 20 November 2026 on the organiser's, operator's, tourism board's or government's own page, unless it is marked as reported by the press or an independent site. Most 2027 programmes aren't out yet; where one isn't, we say so and give 2026's. Weather figures are official February averages for 1991–2020. Times are local: CET in Italy, France, mainland Spain and Hungary; WET in Lisbon and the Canaries; EET in Finland; IST in India.

## When is Valentine's Day 2027, and what else is on that week?

Valentine's Day is Sunday 14 February 2027. Monday 15 February is Washington's Birthday, the US federal holiday most people call Presidents' Day ([OPM](https://www.opm.gov/policy-data-oversight/pay-leave/federal-holidays/)), and Family Day falls on the same Monday in several Canadian provinces, among them [Alberta](https://www.alberta.ca/general-holidays-pay) and [British Columbia](https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/employment-business/employment-standards-advice/employment-standards/statutory-holidays). Our [2027 travel calendar](/blog/2027-travel-calendar) has the rest of the year's long weekends.

These dates around it change prices and crowds:

Two things follow. From England, the southern Netherlands and France's Zone A, the Valentine's weekend is also the first weekend of the school holidays, so compare flights early. And Ramadan is expected to start six days before, so a weekend in Marrakech, Istanbul or Dubai would fall in its first week; Eid al-Fitr is listed for 10 March in Singapore and Malaysia, and our guide to [travelling during Ramadan](/blog/travel-during-ramadan) covers what changes for visitors. Lunar New Year has its own [guide](/blog/lunar-new-year-where-to-celebrate), and our [carnival guide](/blog/carnival-around-the-world) has the carnival dates city by city.

## Where to go for Valentine's Day 2027

### 1. Verona: Juliet's city, with Venice an hour away

**Why that weekend:** Verona in Love, the city council's own Valentine's festival of events, guided visits, music and crafts across the centre ([Comune di Verona](https://www.comune.verona.it/Vivere-il-comune/Eventi/Verona-in-Love)). The 2027 dates aren't announced; in 2026 it ran from 13 to 15 February, and the organiser's [programme page](https://lovinverona.com/programma) still shows that edition. On Sunday 14 February 2027 the city also hosts the Romeo & Giulietta Run half marathon ([Verona Run Events](https://www.veronarunevents.it/?lang=en)).

**Crowds:** in 2026, Juliet's House sold out on Saturday 14 February and took almost 1,800 visitors on the Sunday, and the Destination Verona & Garda observatory measured 84.6% occupancy, with peaks of 90% in the city (reported by [VeronaSera](https://www.veronasera.it/attualita/verona-in-love-dati-turismo-2026.html)). For safety, the council ran a one-way entry to the courtyard that weekend, in from piazzetta Navona through the Teatro Nuovo and out onto via Cappello.

**What to book first:** a time slot at Juliet's House, then a hotel for both nights. Since 1 April 2026 the courtyard costs €5 (through the Teatro Nuovo) and the courtyard with the house €12. Tickets are sold online only, there is no ticket office, and booking is required even for free entry, VeronaCard holders included. It opens 9:00–19:00 Tuesday to Sunday and 14:00–19:00 on Mondays, with the last slot at 18:30 ([Visit Verona](https://www.visitverona.it/it/luoghi/casa-museo-di-giulietta)).

**Weather:** 9.6°C (49°F) by day and 0.1°C (32°F) at night, with 36 mm of rain on about five days.

**Getting there:** for Friday 12 February 2027, Trenitalia showed Frecciarossa trains from Milano Centrale in 1 hour 14 minutes at €12.90 to €33 depending on the hour, and from Venezia Santa Lucia in 1 hour 12 minutes from €10.90, when we checked on 20 November ([Trenitalia](https://www.trenitalia.com/)). Venice's carnival ends on 9 February, so a night there on the way doesn't clash with it.

**The trade-off:** the balcony is newer than the legend. The council bought the house in 1905, and Antonio Avena had the balcony added during a restoration around 1939–40 ([Visit Verona](https://www.visitverona.it/it/luoghi/casa-museo-di-giulietta)). On Valentine's weekend you share it with thousands.

### 2. Terni: Saint Valentine's own town

**Why that weekend:** Valentine was "vescovo e martire di Terni", bishop and martyr, who most probably died in 347, and he is the city's patron ([Diocese of Terni-Narni-Amelia](https://www.diocesi.terni.it/diocesi/san-valentino/)). In 2027 his feast falls on Sunday 14 February. The 2027 programme isn't out; 2026's poster went online in January. In 2026 the festivities ran from 5 February to 16 March: engaged couples made their promise at the Basilica on Sunday 8 February at 11:00, there was a pontifical Mass there on the 14th at 11:00, the saint's urn was carried to the cathedral for a vigil at 21:00, and on the 15th a pontifical Mass at 10:30 was followed by a procession with a historical parade back to the Basilica ([Diocese](https://www.diocesi.terni.it/festa-san-valentino-2026/); [Comune di Terni](https://turismo.comune.terni.it/eventi/terni-citta-dellamore)). Couples marking 25 and 50 years of marriage had their own Masses on 22 February and 1 March.

**What to see:** the Basilica of St Valentine, about 2 km (1.2 miles) from the centre, keeps his reliquary statue in a crystal shrine under the high altar ([Comune di Terni](https://turismo.comune.terni.it/en/must-see/basilica-of-st-valentine)).

**What to book first:** a bed in Terni, or in Rome if you'd rather make it a day trip. We found nothing in the programme that needs a ticket.

**Weather:** Terni has no station in the official normals we used. Viterbo, about 50 km (31 miles) west, averages 11.8°C (53°F) by day and 1.9°C (35°F) at night in February, with 56 mm of rain.

**Getting there and costs:** for Sunday 14 February 2027, Trenitalia showed most Regionale Veloce trains from Roma Termini taking 1 hour 4 to 1 hour 12 minutes for €9.30, such as the 9:25, which arrives at 10:29, and the last train back leaving Terni at 22:38. The Marmore waterfall is a Busitalia bus ride from beside Terni station, €1.30 on top of the train fare, about every 30 minutes, every day ([Trenitalia, Marmore Link](https://www.trenitalia.com/it/regionale/collegamenti-regionale/marmore-link.html)). The falls run on a published water-release timetable, so check the [official site](https://cascatadellemarmore.info/orari-apertura/) before you go.

**The trade-off:** this is a religious feast in a working city, not a romantic resort, and you won't know the 2027 times until the diocese publishes them.

### 3. Paris: the classic, with a late night at the Louvre

**Why that weekend:** the Louvre stays open until 21:00 on Wednesdays and Fridays, so Friday 12 February can end in the galleries ([Louvre](https://www.louvre.fr/en/visit/hours-admission)). Paris is on its Zone C school holidays from 6 to 22 February. Our [three-day Paris itinerary](/blog/3-day-paris-itinerary) has a plan to start from.

**Costs:** the Louvre charges €22 to citizens and residents of the European Economic Area and €32 to everyone else, rates in force since 14 January 2026, and it's closed on Tuesdays. For dinner on the Seine, Bateaux Parisiens' everyday 20:30 sailing started at €115 a head on the [Paris tourist office's shop](https://ticket.parisjetaime.com/en/cruises-on-the-river-seine-c2/bateaux-parisiens-dinner-cruise-830pm-102) on 20 November 2026. We found no official price yet for its Valentine's sailing on 14 February 2027.

**What to book first:** your train or flight, then the dinner, then a Louvre time slot, which the museum recommends.

**Weather:** 8.8°C (48°F) by day and 3.3°C (38°F) at night, with rain on about nine days in the month.

**Getting there:** Eurostar runs London to Paris in 2 hours 16 minutes, from £39 one way ([Eurostar](https://www.eurostar.com/uk-en/train/france/paris)).

**The trade-off:** grey skies, and Valentine's prices you can't see yet. An average February brings rain on about nine days, and the 2027 Valentine's cruises weren't priced on any official page we found.

### 4. Lisbon: the mild one

**Why that weekend:** at 16.4°C (62°F) by day and 9.1°C (48°F) at night, Lisbon is the warmest mainland European pick in this guide. Portugal celebrates Dia dos Namorados on 14 February, as the government's equality commission notes ([CIG](https://www.cig.gov.pt/?p=10142)); in Brazil it's 12 June. Our [three days in Lisbon](/blog/lisbon-3-day-itinerary) itinerary has a plan to start from.

**Costs:** Lisbon's city tax has been €4 per guest per night since 1 September 2024 (reported by [Observador](https://observador.pt/2024/07/24/assembleia-municipal-de-lisboa-aprova-aumento-da-taxa-turistica-a-partir-de-setembro/)). A 24-hour Carris and Metro ticket costs €7.25 and a single journey €1.90, plus €0.50 for the reusable navegante card, and the Metro has a station at the airport ([Metro de Lisboa](https://www.metrolisboa.pt/en/wp-content/uploads/sites/3/2025/12/Tarifas_site_2026_UK-1.pdf), fares since 1 January 2026).

**What to book first:** flights, then a room, with a view if you want one.

**Getting there:** fly in and take the Metro into town.

**The trade-off:** mild isn't dry. Lisbon gets 78 mm of rain in an average February, on about eight days ([IPMA](https://www.ipma.pt/bin/file.data/climate-normal/cn_91-20_LISBOA_GEOFISICO.pdf)).

### 5. Budapest: thermal baths on a small budget

**Why that weekend:** warm water in a cold month. On the Széchenyi Thermal Bath's 2026 price list, a day ticket with a locker costs 14,800 Ft from Friday to Sunday and 13,200 Ft from Monday to Thursday. For the occasion, its Private Spa Experience in a deluxe room, with a 45-minute couples' aroma massage, costs 54,000 Ft per person at weekends and 47,000 Ft on weekdays ([Széchenyi](https://www.szechenyibath.hu/en/prices)). For more low-cost cities, see our guide to the [cheapest destinations in Europe](/blog/cheapest-destinations-in-europe).

**What to book first:** the private spa slot, if you want one. Day tickets can be bought online as Fast Track (16,800 Ft at weekends) or at the entrance, where it's card only.

**Weather:** 7.1°C (45°F) by day and 0.1°C (32°F) at night in the city centre, with 30 mm of rain on about six days.

**Getting there:** the airport bus to the centre costs 2,500 Ft ([BKK](https://bkk.hu/en/tickets-and-passes/prices/)).

**The trade-off:** the other famous spa, the Gellért, took its last guests on 30 September 2025 and is expected to stay closed for renovation until 2028, a timeline the reporter doubted (reported by [Telex](https://telex.hu/english/2025/10/02/gellert)). And Széchenyi charges its higher price on the Friday as well as the weekend.

### 6. Gran Canaria: sun, and a carnival that's still going

**Why that weekend:** the Las Palmas de Gran Canaria carnival runs from 22 January to 28 February 2027 on the provisional calendar the organiser published in June ([LPA Carnaval](https://lpacarnaval.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/avance-carnaval-2027.pdf)). Its theme is music, and the Canary Islands' public broadcaster reported the same dates ([RTVC](https://rtvc.es/presentacion-fechas-carnaval-las-palmas-de-gran-canaria-2027/)). That calendar lists a drag preselection and a carnival night on Friday 12 February, a daytime street party with a parade from the Estadio Insular and a family carnival on Saturday 13, and the children's carnival on Sunday 14. The big parade, the Gran Cabalgata, follows on Saturday 20 February.

**Weather:** 21.4°C (71°F) by day and 15.6°C (60°F) at night at the airport, with 20 mm of rain on about three days.

**What to book first:** flights, early. England's half-term begins on the Monday and the southern Dutch spring break on the Saturday.

**Getting there:** from the airport, bus line 60 runs to Las Palmas twice an hour from 6:15 to 20:15, for €2.30 to San Telmo or €2.95 to Santa Catalina ([Aena](https://www.aena.es/en/gran-canaria/getting-there/bus.html)).

**The trade-off:** the calendar is a preview that can still change, times included, and a carnival is a crowd, not a quiet dinner. If you'd rather go to Tenerife, note that Santa Cruz moved its 2027 street carnival two weeks later, to 19–28 February (reported by [RTVC](https://rtvc.es/santa-cruz-fija-los-festivos-de-2027-y-ajusta-el-calendario-del-carnaval-27-marzo-2026/)).

### 7. Finnish Lapland: snow and a fair chance of auroras

**Why that weekend:** the Finnish Meteorological Institute names February and March, with September and October, as the best months for northern lights, and 21:00 to 01:00 as the best hours ([FMI](https://en.ilmatieteenlaitos.fi/northern-lights)); its space-weather pages put the best time in northern Finland a little later, in March and early April. On a dark, cloudless night, auroras appear on roughly half of nights in central Lapland, around Levi, Ylläs and Saariselkä, and on 75% of nights at Kilpisjärvi ([FMI](https://space.fmi.fi/aurorasnow/auroras-in-finland/)).

**Weather:** Rovaniemi averages −7.3°C (19°F) by day and −13.3°C (8°F) at night; Saariselkä, further north, −8.2°C and −15.3°C.

**What to book first:** a glass igloo or aurora cabin, if that's the plan. We found no official 2027 rates, so ask for quotes now. Then the night train.

**Getting there and costs:** there are over 40 direct flight connections from Europe to Rovaniemi ([Visit Rovaniemi](https://www.visitrovaniemi.fi/plan-your-trip/getting-here/)). By train from Helsinki it takes just over eight hours, 8 hours 22 minutes at fastest, with seats from €22.90 ([VR](https://www.vr.fi/en/helsinki-rovaniemi)). On the night train a private cabin starts at €59, or €84 with a shower, and the price is for the cabin, whatever the number of passengers ([VR](https://www.vr.fi/en/night-trains/sleeper-cars)).

**The trade-off:** cloud and cold. The aurora odds are for clear nights only, Rovaniemi has rain or snow on about ten days in an average February, and nights average −13°C.

### 8. Québec City: carnival's last weekend and an ice hotel

**Why that weekend:** the Carnaval de Québec runs from 5 to 14 February 2027, so it ends on Valentine's Day ([Carnaval de Québec](https://carnaval.qc.ca/en/)). Its pass, the Effigy, costs CA$29 plus tax until 17 January and CA$39 plus tax from 18 January to 14 February; children aged 12 and under don't need one ([Carnaval de Québec](https://carnaval.qc.ca/en/shop/effigy/)). The Hôtel de Glace, the ice hotel at Saint-Gabriel-de-Valcartier, is listed for overnight stays from 4 January to 14 March 2027, with 14 rooms and 16 suites ([Bonjour Québec](https://www.bonjourquebec.com/en/listing/accommodation/hotel-de-glace/3sqo)).

**Weather:** −5.0°C (23°F) by day and −15.7°C (4°F) at night, with 64 cm (25 inches) of snow falling on about 13 days and an average snow depth of 54 cm (21 inches) ([Environment and Climate Change Canada](https://climate.weather.gc.ca/climate_normals/bulk_data_e.html?lang=e&prov=QC&stnname=QUEBEC+JEAN+LESAGE+%28AIRPORT%29&yr=1991&stnID=129000000&climate_id=7016294&submit=Download+Data)).

**What to book first:** a night at the ice hotel, as there are only 30 rooms and suites, then the Effigy before the price rises on 18 January.

**Getting there:** VIA Rail trains from Montréal take 3 hours 26 minutes on average for 270 km (168 miles), with 34 departures a week ([VIA Rail](https://www.viarail.ca/en/explore-our-destinations/trains/ontario-and-quebec/montreal-quebec-city)). Travellers from visa-exempt countries who fly to Canada need an eTA, which costs CA$7 ([Government of Canada](https://www.canada.ca/en/immigration-refugees-citizenship/services/visit-canada/eta.html)); US citizens are exempt ([Government of Canada](https://www.canada.ca/en/immigration-refugees-citizenship/services/visit-canada/eta/facts.html)).

**The trade-off:** −16°C nights, and a holiday weekend for many of the visitors you'll share it with, since Presidents' Day and Family Day in several Canadian provinces both fall on Monday 15.

### 9. Riviera Maya, Mexico: warm, fairly dry and busy

**Why that weekend:** February is one of the drier months on this coast. Tulum averages 29.6°C (85°F) by day and 19.1°C (66°F) at night, with 42 mm of rain on about three days; Cancún 30.1°C (86°F) and 20.6°C (69°F), with 59 mm on about six days (Mexico's weather service, SMN: [Tulum](https://smn.conagua.gob.mx/tools/RESOURCES/Normales_Climatologicas/Normales9120/qroo/nor9120_23025.txt), [Cancún](https://smn.conagua.gob.mx/tools/RESOURCES/Normales_Climatologicas/Normales9120/qroo/nor9120_23155.txt)).

**Costs:** Quintana Roo charges foreign visitors over 15 a state tourist tax, the Visitax. For 2026 it was 285 pesos per person, and travellers reported being asked for up to 550 pesos at Cancún airport (reported by [Diario de Yucatán](https://www.yucatan.com.mx/mexico/2026/11/08/controversia-por-cobros-excesivos-del-visitax-en-el-aeropuerto-de-cancun.html) on 8 November 2026). Check the 2027 rate with the state before you go.

**What to book first:** flights, which fill on a US holiday weekend, then the hotel.

**Getting there:** fly to Cancún, the gateway for the coast down to Tulum.

**The trade-off:** you won't be alone, since it's a three-day weekend in the US and in several Canadian provinces. From Europe it's a long-haul flight for a short break, so it works better as the start of a longer trip; our [honeymoon destinations guide](/blog/best-honeymoon-destinations-2026) has ideas for one.

### 10. Udaipur: lakes and palaces before the heat

**Why that weekend:** February is dry and warm: 27.9°C (82°F) by day, 10.3°C (51°F) at night and 1.3 mm of rain in an average month. By March, highs average 33.6°C (92°F).

**Costs:** the HRH group, which runs heritage hotels in Udaipur, publishes its rates for 1 October 2026 to 31 March 2027: a palace room not facing the lake at Shiv Niwas Palace, inside the City Palace complex, costs ₹20,000 a night, and a standard room at its Garden Hotel ₹11,000, room only, taxes extra. A set meal at the Jagmandir Island Palace, on Lake Pichola, starts at ₹5,500 a head, plus a ₹6,000 cover charge for a couple ([HRH Hotels](https://hrhhotels.com/pdfs/Tariff-oct-26-mar-27.pdf)). The City Palace Museum lists visiting hours of 09:00 to 21:00 ([City Palace Museum](https://citypalacemuseum.org/planyourvisit)).

**What to book first:** the hotel. A lake view costs more: a terrace suite at Shiv Niwas is ₹35,000 facing away from the lake and ₹40,000 facing it.

**Getting there:** fly to Maharana Pratap Airport at Dabok ([Airports Authority of India](https://www.aai.aero/en/airports/udaipur)).

**The trade-off:** the music festival is usually earlier. The Vedanta Udaipur World Music Festival ran from 6 to 8 February in 2026 (reported by [EventFAQs](https://in.eventfaqs.com/2026/01/22/vedanta-udaipur-world-music-festival-returns-for-10th-anniversary-edition-from-february-6-to-8-2026/)), and its 2027 dates aren't out.

### 11. Teruel: the Lovers' festival, the weekend after

**Why then:** Las Bodas de Isabel de Segura retells the story of the Lovers of Teruel with medieval scenes, the entrance of the Aragonese troops, concerts, local food, crafts, falconry and sword fights ([Spain's tourism board](https://www.spain.info/en/calendar/bodas-isabel-segura/)). Since 2009 it has been held on the weekend of the third Friday of February. It now has more than 150 groups in medieval dress and was declared a Fiesta of International Tourist Interest in December 2025 ([Ministry of Industry and Tourism](https://www.mintur.gob.es/es-es/gabineteprensa/notasprensa/2025/documents/20251223%20np%20fiti%20bodas%20isabel%20de%20segura.pdf)). For 2027, the organiser's rules for its poster contest give the dates as Thursday 18 to Sunday 21 February ([Fundación Bodas de Isabel](https://www.bodasdeisabel.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/11/BASES-CONCURSO-DE-CARTEL-2027-corregido.pdf)), which fits that rule, while Spain's tourism board still lists them as to be confirmed. In 2026 it ran from 19 to 22 February ([Turismo de Aragón](https://www.turismodearagon.com/agenda-aragon/bodas-de-isabel-de-segura/)).

**Costs:** the Mausoleum of the Lovers costs €9 with the Mudéjar church complex or €10 for the full visit, and opens 10:00–14:00 and 16:00–20:00 ([Fundación Amantes](https://www.amantesdeteruel.es/)).

**Weather:** 12.3°C (54°F) by day and −1.6°C (29°F) at night, with 14 mm of rain on about three days.

**Getting there:** Teruel is 145 km (90 miles) from Valencia, 170 km (106 miles) from Zaragoza and 300 km (186 miles) from Madrid, with Renfe trains and a bus station ([Turismo de Teruel](https://turismo.teruel.es/como-llegar/)).

**The trade-off:** it isn't Valentine's weekend, the 2027 programme isn't out yet, and nights are below freezing.

## Does Valentine's weekend cost more?

Often, yes. These are the cases where we found evidence.

- **Minimum stays:** some hotels sell Valentine's weekend only as two nights. The Greenbrier in West Virginia, for one, says its Valentine's weekend package "requires two-night minimum stay", from $1,249.06 for the two nights including taxes and fees; the page doesn't say which year ([The Greenbrier](https://www.greenbrier.com/offers/valentines-weekend-package/)).
- **Weekend pricing:** Széchenyi's day ticket is 1,600 Ft dearer from Friday to Sunday, and its couples' spa 7,000 Ft per person dearer at weekends.
- **Deadlines:** Québec's carnival pass goes from CA$29 to CA$39 on 18 January.
- **Demand:** accommodation in Verona and around Lake Garda was 84.6% full on the 2026 festival weekend, with peaks of 90% in the city. In the US, a National Retail Federation survey of 7,791 adults ranked an evening out among the top Valentine's gifts for 2026, with $6.3 billion expected to be spent on evenings out ([NRF](https://nrf.com/media-center/press-releases/valentine-s-day-spending-expected-to-reach-new-records)).

### Saturday, Sunday or another weekend?

**Saturday 13:** with the 14th on a Sunday, we expect Saturday to be the busier night out, since most people don't work the next day. In the US, Sunday also brings the Super Bowl.

**Sunday 14:** the day itself, and Terni's feast day. Check that your restaurant opens on Sundays.

**The weekend before, 5–7 February:** Lunar New Year on the Saturday, China's holiday week, the last carnival weekend before Carnival Tuesday and the start of Paris's school holidays. Busy in other ways.

**The weekend after, 19–21 February:** France's Zone B holidays begin and the northern and middle Netherlands start their spring break on the 20th. It's also Teruel's festival, Tenerife's street carnival and the Gran Cabalgata in Las Palmas. Our guide to [where to go in February](/blog/where-to-go-in-february) covers the rest of the month.

## Where to go if you'd rather skip the 14th

Go the weekend after, to Teruel, for a love story told in costume rather than over a set menu. Or keep the date but drop the dinner: Terni's feast is a saint's day of Masses and processions. If you'd rather move the date altogether, Brazil celebrates Dia dos Namorados on 12 June ([Agência Gov](https://agenciagov.ebc.com.br/noticias/202506/dia-dos-namorados-sem-dor-de-cabeca-conheca-seus-direitos-na-hora-de-comprar-o-presente)), and our [best places to travel in 2027](/blog/best-places-to-travel-2027) has more ideas for the year.

## How to plan the long weekend

Four days, Friday to Monday, fit most of these picks without rushing:

- **Venice and Verona:** Thursday night in Venice, a Friday train to Verona (1 hour 12 minutes), the weekend in Verona for the festival or the run, and Monday home from Verona or Milan.
- **Rome and Terni:** Friday and Saturday in Rome, then Sunday by train to Terni for the feast (about an hour), back to Rome at night.
- **Las Palmas:** Friday's carnival night, Saturday's street party, then a quiet Sunday on the beach.
- **Québec City:** Friday at the carnival, Saturday night at the ice hotel, Sunday for the carnival's last day.

You can [lay out the weekend](/trips/new) in the planner, change it by chatting and share it with your partner; it doesn't book anything. Our guide to [planning a weekend getaway](/blog/plan-weekend-getaway-with-ai) has more on how.

## What to book first, and when

No authority sets booking windows, so this is our advice, with the official dates we found.

1. **Now:** the Hôtel de Glace, glass igloos in Lapland, Verona hotels, and flights leaving England, the southern Netherlands or France's Zone A, where school holidays start that weekend, or the US, where it's a long weekend.
2. **By 17 January:** the Québec carnival pass at its lower price.
3. **As soon as they go on sale:** Juliet's House time slots, online only, and any Valentine's dinner cruise on the Seine.
4. **In January:** look for the Terni and Verona programmes. Terni's 2026 poster went online in January.
5. **The week before:** the Las Palmas carnival times, the cloud forecast in Lapland and the water-release times at the Marmore falls.

## Frequently asked questions

### What day is Valentine's Day 2027?

Sunday 14 February 2027. Monday 15 February is Presidents' Day in the US.

### Is Valentine's weekend 2027 a long weekend?

In the US, yes: Washington's Birthday is a federal holiday on Monday 15 February. It's also Family Day in several Canadian provinces, among them Alberta and British Columbia. In England, the February half-term in many council schools starts the same Monday.

### Where is warm in Europe for Valentine's Day?

Gran Canaria, at about 21°C by day, with its carnival still on. On the mainland, Lisbon averages 16°C by day.

### Where can we go for Valentine's Day on a budget?

Budapest, Terni and Teruel are the low-budget picks here. The train from Rome to Terni costs €9.30, and a weekend day at Budapest's Széchenyi baths costs 14,800 Ft at 2026 prices.

### When is Verona in Love 2027?

Not announced yet. In 2026 it ran from 13 to 15 February. The Romeo & Giulietta Run half marathon is on Sunday 14 February 2027.

### Does Ramadan 2027 overlap with Valentine's Day?

Yes. Ramadan is expected to begin on 8 February 2027, subject to change, so the 14th falls in its first week. Our guide to [travelling during Ramadan](/blog/travel-during-ramadan) has the dates by country.

### When is Dia dos Namorados in Brazil?

On 12 June. Portugal celebrates on 14 February.
