# Where to Go in December 2026: 12 Destinations Compared

December is two seasons wearing the same name. One is cold and northern: Christmas markets steaming with Glühwein, ski lifts turning for opening week, the aurora over the Arctic on the longest nights of the year. The other is upside down: high summer in the Southern Hemisphere, and the dry season finally settled on Southeast Asia's best beaches.

The complication is price, and the 2026 calendar is unusually clear about it. Christmas Day and New Year's Day both fall on a Friday, so the effective peak runs from Saturday 19 December 2026 to Sunday 3 January 2027, one of the year's most expensive stretches for most destinations on this list. Early and mid-December is the quiet window: markets open, Arlberg lifts turning, beaches dry, far less to pay. Below are twelve destinations where December is the right month, each with climate normals from the national weather service, the 2026 dates that matter, a sourced cost line and a note on which weeks to book.

## December 2026 at a glance

- **Thursday 26 November: US Thanksgiving.** Let the Sunday return wave pass and fly out in the first days of December, when the same destinations are quiet.
- **Friday 27 November to Friday 15 January 2027: polar night in Tromsø.** The sun does not rise above the horizon at any point in December, so pick dates by moon and cloud forecast, not by daylight.
- **Wednesday 2 December: Ski Arlberg opens**, with the Stanton Ski Open festival from Friday 4 to Sunday 6 December. The first weekend is a party weekend; a mid-week arrival gets emptier pistes.
- **Wednesday 9 December: South African schools break up** (teachers finish on Friday 11 December), and the 2027 school year starts Wednesday 13 January. Cape Town's domestic crush runs roughly 10 December to 12 January; a Cape trip that ends by the 9th dodges it.
- **Saturday 19 December: peak pricing switches on.** Ski Arlberg's adult day pass moves from €80.50 to €85.00, and the fortnight created by the two Friday holidays begins. Anything booked for the 18th or earlier sits in the cheaper tier.
- **Wednesday 23 December: most Vienna markets close** (Spittelberg, Karlsplatz, Am Hof, Freyung and others), with Rathausplatz and Stephansplatz following on Saturday 26 December. The same Wednesday is Unduvap Poya in Sri Lanka, when alcohol sales are restricted.
- **Friday 25 December: Christmas Day**, a public holiday in Austria, South Africa, India, Mexico and Brazil alike, with Saturday 26 December also a holiday in Austria and South Africa: expect Friday closures and a long weekend of full beaches.
- **Thursday 31 December: Réveillon on Copacabana** (2.6 million people on the beach at the last edition, per Riotur) and Vienna's free New Year's Eve Trail from 2 pm to 2 am.

## 1. Vienna, Austria — Christmas Markets at Full Voltage

No city runs the whole Christmas-market tradition better than Vienna. The Rathausplatz market glows in front of the neo-Gothic city hall from Friday 13 November to Saturday 26 December 2026, Spittelberg and Karlsplatz trade tat for actual craft, and between them the coffee houses, opera and museums give you a full city rather than a row of huts. It is cold and grey: alongside the 4.0 °C highs in the table, the Hohe Warte normals give 8.6 wet days and just 57.1 hours of sunshine, the year's lowest monthly total. US and Canadian passports need no visa for up to 90 days in any 180, and non-EU arrivals are registered in the EU Entry/Exit System (fingerprints and a photo, no fee).

**Cost reality:** $205 (€177) per person a day (Budget Your Trip average); book the sleep first.
**The trade-off:** most markets close December 23–26 (Rathausplatz on the 26th, Spittelberg and Karlsplatz on the 23rd), so the classic experience is early-to-mid December. Only Schönbrunn and the Riesenradplatz winter market carry on to 6 January 2027, along with the ice rink on Rathausplatz — enough for a visit between Christmas and New Year, but not the full spread.

## 2. Tromsø, Norway — The Aurora at Its Best

Well north of the Arctic Circle, Tromsø spends December in polar night: from Friday 27 November 2026 to Friday 15 January 2027 the sun does not rise above the horizon. The long dark hours are prime aurora time, and Tromsø sits under the auroral oval with operators who chase clear sky for a living. It is milder than the latitude suggests (highs around 0.3 °C), but 109.7 mm of precipitation falls on 15 wet days, and that cloud is the real enemy of an aurora trip.

**Cost reality:** Budget Your Trip's average for Norway as a whole is $137 (kr1,279) per person a day; its Tromsø page shows $88 (kr822), but Norway is expensive and the national figure is the safer planning number. A guided aurora chase runs roughly NOK 1,000–1,250 by big bus or NOK 1,600–2,500 by small-group minibus in December, about $105–265 at the September 2026 rate (Northern Horizon, Best Arctic, Norges Bank). Operators price per date and November–February is the top of the range; a self-drive chase night is the budget play.
**The trade-off:** you are gambling on cloud cover, and there is no sunlight for landscape photos. Give it more than one night so one clears, and read our [northern lights guide](/blog/best-places-to-see-northern-lights) before you book a chase.

## 3. St. Anton am Arlberg, Austria — Opening Week, Real Snow

The Austrian Alps are one of the few places where December reliably delivers early-season snow on serious terrain. St. Anton, at the heart of the vast Ski Arlberg network, opens on Wednesday 2 December 2026 (limited operation possible) with high, snow-sure pistes and the après-ski culture that made its name — the Mooserwirt's DJ party kicks off at 3.30pm and ends on the dot at 8pm. GeoSphere Austria's normals for the village (1,304 m) give December 74.9 cm of fresh snow and snow cover of at least 1 cm on 24.6 days.

**Cost reality:** Budget Your Trip has no St. Anton page; its Austria-wide average is $204 (€176) per person a day, which a ski resort in peak season will exceed. Add an adult 1-day Ski Arlberg pass (€80.50 from 2–18 December, €85 from 19 December, plus a refundable €5 card deposit). Early-December lodging is far cheaper than the Christmas block.
**The trade-off:** natural snow in the first half of December is good but not guaranteed at lower elevations — stay on the high Arlberg sectors (the lift-served terrain tops out at 2,811 m) and be flexible on exact dates; if you need a snow guarantee, a Tyrolean glacier resort such as Hintertux (lifts to 3,250 m, skiing almost all year round) is the safer bet. Christmas and New Year weeks (19 December to 10 January in the St. Anton rate card we checked) are booked out well ahead and cost far more — one St. Anton hotel's published 2026/27 rates run roughly 40–65% above the first ten days of December for the same room.

## 4. Krabi & Railay, Thailand — The Andaman Turns Dry

While northern Europe bundles up, Thailand's Andaman coast is entering its best stretch of the year. December starts the dry season around Krabi and Railay: Ko Lanta, the nearest station in the WMO normals, drops from 148.4 mm of rain in November to 64.6 mm on 7.8 wet days, and the limestone karsts stand crisp against clear sky instead of monsoon haze. This is when Railay's climbing walls and the longtail island hops are at their best. Two entry rules have changed: since 15 September 2026 the tourist visa exemption for 60 nationalities (US, UK, Canada and Australia included) allows 30 days, not 60, and every visitor must file the Thailand Digital Arrival Card online before entry, up to three days ahead.

**Cost reality:** $71 (฿2,352) per person a day in Krabi (Budget Your Trip average); Railay Beach itself runs pricier at $134 (฿4,441). Still one of the strongest value-per-degree picks on this list.
**The trade-off:** everyone knows December is Andaman season, so beaches and boats get busy and rates ratchet up sharply once the Christmas–New Year fortnight starts; Thai holidays add domestic crowds over 5–7 December and on Thursday 10 December. Come in the first half of the month, and base in Railay or Ao Nang rather than crowded Phi Phi.

## 5. Kerala, India — Backwaters in the Cool, Dry Season

December is peak season in Kerala: the monsoon is long gone, humidity drops, and coastal days run to daytime highs of about 32–33 °C with nights around 22 °C, while the Munnar hills are noticeably cooler. Kochi sees measurable rain on only 3.8 days in the month, and a houseboat night on the Alleppey backwaters is the month's great slow-travel experience. Christmas Day, Friday 25 December, is a closed holiday on India's central-government list and, with Kerala's large Christian community, a major celebration in Kochi. Apply for the e-Tourist Visa online at least four days before you fly (30 days, US$25 for US, UK, Canadian and Australian citizens between July and March).

**Cost reality:** $40 (₨3,809) per person a day in Kochi (Budget Your Trip average); a private houseboat splits well between two people and is worth the splurge.
**The trade-off:** the best houseboats and hill-station stays fill early, and Christmas-week rates jump. Book Munnar and Alleppey ahead; the coast stays easier.

## 6. Southern Vietnam & Phu Quoc — Dry-Season Sun in the South

Vietnam's north, centre and south run on different weather calendars, and in December the south is where you want to be. Ho Chi Minh City, the Mekong Delta and the island of Phu Quoc sit in their dry season, with the city's rainfall down from 128 mm in November to 41 mm on 5 rain days, while the central coast still catches the tail of its wet months. Pair the Delta's floating markets with a few days on Phu Quoc's west-coast beaches for a genuinely warm December at a fraction of Caribbean prices. Citizens of the UK, Italy, Spain, Germany, France, Denmark, Sweden, Norway and Finland are visa-exempt for up to 45 days; US, Canadian, Australian and Portuguese citizens need the e-visa, good for a 90-day multiple-entry stay.

**Cost reality:** $55 (₫1,426,719) per person a day on Phu Quoc and $88 (₫2,297,852) in Ho Chi Minh City (Budget Your Trip averages); street food, cheap domestic flights and beach bungalows keep budgets low.
**The trade-off:** the north (Hanoi, Sapa) is cool and can be grey-drizzly, and central Hoi An and Da Nang can still see rain early in the month. Build the route around the south, not a full north-to-south sweep.

## 7. Sri Lanka's South Coast — The Beaches Start Drying Out

Sri Lanka runs on two monsoons, and December inverts the calendar we wrote about for August. Now it's the southwest coast that's drying out fast. Mirissa, Weligama and Unawatuna are back in play, with more sunny days than showers, although January to March are the genuinely dry beach months. Mirissa's main whale-watching season (November to April, per local operator Raja & the Whales) is in full swing, and the hill country at Ella is at its most pleasant. Galle's normals spell out the transition: 175.9 mm on 11.2 wet days, against 282.6 mm in November and 86.8 mm in January. Since 25 May 2026 nationals of 40 countries (US, UK, Canada, Australia, Italy and Spain among them) get the 30-day double-entry tourist ETA free, though it must still be obtained before travel.

**Cost reality:** $56 (LKR18,464) per person a day, Budget Your Trip's Sri Lanka-wide average (the site has no Galle page). Superb value, with boutique guesthouses on the beach.
**The trade-off:** December is peak on the south coast, so Mirissa and Unawatuna get busy and prices firm up over the holidays, and Wednesday 23 December is Unduvap Poya, when alcohol sales are restricted. The east coast (Arugam Bay) is now off-season — the opposite of an [August trip to Sri Lanka](/blog/where-to-go-in-august), so don't reuse a summer itinerary.

## 8. Dubai, UAE — The City Finally Cools Down

Dubai is unbearable in summer and close to perfect in December. The airport normals give December 256.9 hours of sunshine, roughly 8.3 a day, on top of the 26.5 °C highs in the table, which is why this is the emirate's headline month: beach clubs, desert safaris and the whole outdoor city come alive without the furnace. British passports get a free visitor visa on arrival, valid up to 90 days in a 180-day period.

**Cost reality:** $289 (AED1,060) per person a day (Budget Your Trip average), the highest figure on this list; early-December hotel deals are where the value hides.
**The trade-off:** December is high season, so rates and crowds climb, peaking hard over the New Year fireworks. The first half of the month is the smart window.

## 9. Cape Town & the Cape Winelands, South Africa — Southern Summer Peak

Flip the hemisphere and December is high summer at the tip of Africa. The airport normals log 338.4 hours of December sunshine, about 11 a day, against the 12.8 mm of rain in the table. Table Mountain is clear, the beaches and the coastal drive to Cape Point are at their best, and the Winelands around Stellenbosch and Franschhoek are at full summer green, with the estates in high season ahead of a harvest that only begins in late summer. It is a favorite among warm-weather honeymooners, as our [2026 honeymoon guide](/blog/best-honeymoon-destinations-2026) notes. US, UK, Canadian, Australian, German, Italian, Spanish and Portuguese passports enter visa-free for up to 90 days.

**Cost reality:** $188 (R3,111) per person a day (Budget Your Trip average), which puts Cape Town with the European cities on cost rather than the Asian beaches; wine-country stays climb on top.
**The trade-off:** December is also local summer holidays: the school term ends for learners on Wednesday 9 December and the 2027 year starts 13 January, with public holidays on Wednesday 16, Friday 25 and Saturday 26 December, so beaches, restaurants and the Winelands get busy and prices rise into the New Year. Book the marquee restaurants and wine estates ahead, and expect the coast to be lively rather than quiet.

## 10. Torres del Paine, Patagonia — The One Warm Window

Patagonia's comfortable walking season is short, and December opens it. In the far south of Chile this is the start of summer: some of the longest days on the continent (about 16 and a half hours of daylight near the solstice, with sunset around 10pm), temperatures close to the warmest the region gets (January and February run slightly warmer), and the wind, while still famous, at its most forgiving. Puerto Natales, the gateway town, averages a December high of 17.2 °C and a low of 3.8 °C; Punta Arenas, the nearest WMO station, logs 232.5 hours of sunshine, its sunniest month. This season's rules: since 1 May 2026 the park sells a single-day entry or a multi-day entry valid for up to 10 continuous days, bought ahead on pasesparques.cl. The guide requirement for Base Torres and the W was lifted on 16 September 2026. The full O circuit stays closed until 1 November 2026; when it reopens, expect the rules of its last reopening: a cap of 100 people a day, walked one-way, with registration at the Coirón ranger station.

**Cost reality:** $99 (CL$91,856) per person a day inside the park and $106 (CL$99,855) in Puerto Natales (Budget Your Trip averages); refugio beds and park logistics add up.
**The trade-off:** December and January are the park's two busiest months (CONAF counted 60,181 visits in December 2024, just behind January's 61,027), so refugios and campsites inside the park book out months ahead — reserve now or you don't walk. The weather is still Patagonia: pack for four seasons in a day.

## 11. Rio de Janeiro, Brazil — Summer Beaches and Réveillon

December in Rio is beach season at full tilt. The city runs hot and bright (INMET's 1981–2010 normals: high 30.3 °C, low 23.2 °C) and Copacabana and Ipanema fill with the easy rhythm of Carioca summer. The honest footnote is rain: on the 1961–1990 normals in the table, December is the wettest month of the year at 169 mm on 13 rain days, so build the beach days around it. The month climaxes with Réveillon on Thursday 31 December, the New Year's Eve celebration on Copacabana beach, when more than 2.5 million people in white watch the fireworks over the Atlantic. Riotur put the last edition at 2.6 million, and Guinness World Records certifies it as the world's largest New Year's celebration. Check your passport first: since 10 April 2025 US, Canadian and Australian nationals need a Brazilian eVisa (US$80.90, obtained online); British nationals still enter visa-free for up to 90 days.

**Cost reality:** $120 (R$615) per person a day (Budget Your Trip average); everyday Rio is affordable, Réveillon week is not.
**The trade-off:** if you want the fireworks, book Copacabana or Ipanema lodging months out and expect minimum-stay rules and holiday pricing. If you don't, come earlier in December for the same beaches at far lower cost.

## 12. Tulum & the Riviera Maya, Mexico — The Drier Season on the Caribbean

The Mexican Caribbean shakes off its wet months in December: humidity drops, the sargassum that plagues summer is usually at its lowest, and Tulum and the wider Riviera Maya settle into warm days. Cancún's normals still show 96.6 mm of rain on 11.7 rain days, so "dry" here means drier, not rainless; cenote swims and the ruins above the turquoise sea work without summer's afternoon downpours. Air arrivals no longer get the paper FMM form; the digital version is downloaded via the QR code at immigration or from the INM portal. Friday 25 December is a mandatory rest day in Mexico, as is Friday 1 January 2027.

**Cost reality:** $144 (MX$2,443) per person a day in Tulum and $167 (MX$2,817) in Cancún (Budget Your Trip averages), so the whole coast carries a premium; shop its length rather than fixing on the Tulum hotel zone.
**The trade-off:** December starts high season, so it's busier and pricier than the fall, and the holiday week is a Tulum scene at full markup. Early December is the value pocket.

## Practical Tips for December Travel

- **Book the peak fortnight, or dodge it entirely.** Saturday 19 December 2026 to Sunday 3 January 2027 is among the year's most expensive stretches for most destinations here; the first half of December delivers the same markets, snow and beaches for far less.
- **For sun, follow the dry season, not just the latitude.** Krabi and southern Vietnam turn dry in December, Sri Lanka's south coast is still drying out, and the central Vietnamese coast is still catching rain. The [MonkeyTravel free AI trip planner](/trips/new) can sanity-check the weather for your exact route.
- **Check the entry rule that changed this year.** Thailand's visa exemption is now 30 days, not 60; Sri Lanka's tourist ETA is free for 40 nationalities; Brazil needs an eVisa from US, Canadian and Australian passports; and non-EU arrivals in Austria and Norway are registered in the EU Entry/Exit System, fully operational since 10 April 2026. ETIAS is not live and has no published start date, so ignore any page that gives one; the fee will be EUR 20 once it launches.
- **If you can shift into January, prices fall.** The first weeks of January bring near-identical weather at far lower rates, and Schönbrunn's market and the Rathausplatz rink stay open to 6 January. See how the months line up in our [2026 travel calendar](/blog/2026-travel-calendar).

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### Where is the best place to go in December 2026?

It depends which December you want. For the classic winter experience, Vienna's Christmas markets (before most close on 23–26 December) and Tromsø for the polar-night aurora are hard to beat. For sun and value, Krabi, Kerala and southern Vietnam are in dry season; for full summer, Cape Town and Rio de Janeiro are at their peak.

### Where can I find winter sun in December?

Follow the dry season and the Southern Hemisphere summer. Krabi, Kerala and southern Vietnam are hot and dry on the December normals in the table; Dubai runs a 26.5 °C mean high with 256.9 hours of sunshine; Cape Town and the Riviera Maya are in summer or its Caribbean equivalent.

### Is December too expensive to travel?

Saturday 19 December to Sunday 3 January is the peak-price window for most destinations on this list; early and mid-December is the sweet spot. Shifting into the first half of December or after the second week of January cuts hotel costs sharply — at St. Anton, for example, early-December rooms run roughly a third cheaper than the Christmas tier.

### Where is it cheap to travel in December?

South and Southeast Asia deliver the best value on Budget Your Trip's averages: Kochi $40 a day, Phu Quoc $55, Sri Lanka $56 and Krabi $71, all in prime season. Among the summer-hemisphere picks, Torres del Paine ($99) and Rio ($120) are the cheaper end, while Cape Town ($188) sits with the European cities.

### Early December or Christmas week: how big is the price gap?

Large enough to decide the trip. The one published rate card we checked, a St. Anton hotel's 2026/27 tariff, prices the 19 December to 10 January block roughly 40–65% above the first ten days of December for the same room, and the weeks either side of Christmas are only about 20% cheaper than Christmas itself; Ski Arlberg's day pass rises from €80.50 to €85 on 19 December.

### Where should I go for one week in December?

Match the week to a single base. For city and markets, Vienna alone fills it; for aurora, Tromsø with several nights in one place beats hopping between towns. For beach, pick one coast: Krabi and Railay, Phu Quoc, or Kerala's backwaters plus one beach. Cape Town covers city, beach and wine without a domestic flight; Torres del Paine's W Trek is a multi-day hike, and once you add the transfers in and out via Punta Arenas and Puerto Natales it fills the whole week.

### Is it worth booking the 19 December to 3 January window at all?

Yes, if the event is the reason: Réveillon on Copacabana, Vienna's free New Year's Eve Trail on 31 December, the Schönbrunn and Riesenradplatz markets running to 6 January, or peak-season snow at St. Anton. No, if weather is the reason: every climate figure here is a monthly average, so the normals give you no reason to expect a better beach day in Krabi on 28 December than on 8 December — and the earlier date is cheaper and quieter.

### Where is it warm and dry in December?

On the 1991–2020 normals, the driest warm picks are Cape Town (12.8 mm of rain on 2.6 wet days) and Dubai (14.8 mm on 3.4 days), followed by Ho Chi Minh City (41 mm on 5 days), Kochi (53.4 mm on 3.8 days) and Ko Lanta for Krabi (64.6 mm on 7.8 days). Cancún (96.6 mm on 11.7 days) is not dry, Galle (175.9 mm on 11.2 days) is still in transition, and Rio's December is its wettest month (169 mm on the 1961–1990 normals). (Wet-day counts use each service's own threshold, so compare the millimetres, not the day counts.)
