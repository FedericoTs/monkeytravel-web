# Travelling During Ramadan and Eid 2027: Dates, What Changes and Where It's Easiest

Ramadan 1448 is expected to begin on Monday 8 February 2027 and end with Eid al-Fitr on Tuesday 9 or Wednesday 10 March, depending on the country and the new moon. You can travel normally through the month: the big sights we checked stay open, some on shorter hours, and what you notice most is the rush home before sunset for iftar, the meal that breaks the fast, followed by busy evenings. The crowds come with Eid, above all in Indonesia and Turkey.

This guide is for anyone heading to Morocco, Turkey, Egypt, the UAE, Jordan, Oman, Tunisia, Malaysia or Indonesia in February or March 2027, whether Ramadan is new to you or you'll be fasting yourself. Every date, rule and opening time was read on 20 November 2026, on the government's, tourism board's or venue's own page wherever one exists. Press and independent sources are marked. Where 2027 hours aren't published, we give 2026's and say so. Times are local, and the iftar table names each time zone.

"Expected" means not yet confirmed. Most of these countries announce the start and end of the month after looking for the new crescent, often only the evening before, so any date here except Turkey's can move by a day.

## When is Ramadan 2027?

Ramadan 1448 is expected to begin on **Monday 8 February 2027**. In Turkey that is already the official date: the Diyanet, the state's religious authority, lists 8 February as the first day of Ramadan in its published 2027 calendar ([Diyanet](https://vakithesaplama.diyanet.gov.tr/icerik.php?icerik=154)). 8 February is also the expected first day in:

- **Saudi Arabia's Umm al-Qura calendar**, as tabulated by the astronomer Robert van Gent of Utrecht University ([van Gent](https://webspace.science.uu.nl/~gent0113/islam/ummalqura_principal.htm)). He notes that the Saudi religious authorities often adjust its Ramadan and Shawwal dates after reported sightings.
- **Malaysia's 2027 holiday list**, where Johor and Kedah give a holiday for the start of Ramadan (Awal Ramadan) on 8 February, marked "subject to change" ([Prime Minister's Department](https://www.kabinet.gov.my/storage/2026/08/HKA_2027.pdf)).
- **The UAE Astronomy Centre's projection** (reported by [The National](https://www.thenationalnews.com/news/uae/2026/10/06/ramadan-2027-uae-dates-eid-al-fitr-holiday/)).
- **The calendar of Muhammadiyah**, the Indonesian Islamic organisation (reported by [Metro TV](https://www.metrotvnews.com/read/b7WCmPGl-idulfitri-2027-tanggal-berapa-ini-perkiraan-1-syawal-1448-h)). Indonesia's government sets its own date by decree of the Minister of Religious Affairs ([State Secretariat](https://setneg.go.id/baca/index/inilah_skb_3_menteri_libur_nasional_dan_cuti_bersama_2027)).

The month lasts 29 or 30 days. The fast runs from dawn to sunset: about 12 to 13½ hours a day in these countries in February and March 2027, by our count from [timeanddate](https://www.timeanddate.com/sun/)'s twilight and sunset tables. The exact start of the fast depends on the dawn rule each authority uses.

Laylat al-Qadr is observed on one of the odd-numbered nights among the last ten of the month ([Visit Abu Dhabi](https://visitabudhabi.ae/en/plan-your-trip/article-hub/abu-dhabi-ramadan-questions-answered)). Turkey's calendar marks it as Kadir Gecesi on Friday 5 March 2027.

## When is Eid al-Fitr 2027?

Eid al-Fitr falls on 1 Shawwal, the day after Ramadan ends. Turks call it Ramazan Bayramı, Malaysians Hari Raya Puasa or Aidilfitri, Indonesians Idul Fitri or Lebaran, and Tunisians Aïd Esseghir.

- **Tuesday 9 March 2027:** Turkey's official calendar, with the eve (Arefe) on Monday 8 March. It is also the expected date in the Umm al-Qura calendar, which the Saudi authorities often adjust after sightings, and in Muhammadiyah's calendar.
- **Wednesday 10 March 2027 (expected):** the official holiday lists of Malaysia and Indonesia. Malaysia marks it "subject to change", and Indonesia leaves the final date to a ministerial decree.
- **9 or 10 March (expected):** the UAE, by its Astronomy Centre's projection; the month runs to 30 days if the crescent isn't seen on the evening of the 29th (reported by The National).

The new moon falls on 8 March 2027 ([van Gent's visibility maps](https://webspace.science.uu.nl/~gent0113/islam/islam_lunvis_next_year.htm)). For countries that start Ramadan on 8 February, that evening is the 29th of the month, and any crescent will be less than a day old. That is why the expected dates split between the 9th and the 10th.

## Why do the dates differ, and when are they confirmed?

Most of these countries begin and end the month on the sighting of the new crescent on the evening of the 29th; if nobody sees it, the month runs to 30 days. Morocco's Ministry of Habous announced on the evening of 18 February 2026 that Ramadan began the next day, and on the evening of 19 March that Eid was the next day (reported by Le Matin, [start](https://lematin.ma/nation/jeudi-1er-jour-du-mois-de-ramadan-au-maroc-ministere/330638) and [Eid](https://lematin.ma/nation/aid-al-fitr-celebre-le-vendredi-20-mars-au-maroc/335924)). Jordan's Board of Iftaa' ruled on 17 February ([Board of Iftaa'](https://aliftaa.jo/decision-en/1749/ZkahEn.aspx)), Tunisia's Mufti announced on television that evening that the crescent hadn't been seen (reported by [Webdo](https://www.webdo.tn/fr/actualite/national/tunisie-jeudi-19-fevrier-premier-jour-du-ramadan/393324/)), and Oman follows a national moon-sighting committee (reported by [Oman Observer](https://www.omanobserver.om/article/1181777/oman/labour/except-for-eids-oman-announces-public-holidays-for-2026)).

In 2026 the split was a full day. Saudi Arabia, the UAE and Qatar started fasting on Wednesday 18 February, while Jordan, Tunisia, Malaysia and Indonesia started on Thursday 19 February (reported by [Tunisie Numérique](https://www.tunisienumerique.com/debut-du-ramadan-quels-sont-les-pays-qui-jeunent-mercredi-et-ceux-qui-attendent-jeudi/)), and so did Morocco. At the end of the month, Morocco celebrated Eid on Friday 20 March, while Indonesia's government chose Saturday 21 March, a day after Muhammadiyah (reported by [CNN Indonesia](https://www.cnnindonesia.com/nasional/20260320092228-20-1339816/ketum-pp-muhammadiyah-soal-lebaran-tak-perlu-memvonis-yang-beda)).

Turkey works differently. The Diyanet publishes its calendar years ahead, with its online table running to 2035 ([Diyanet](https://vakithesaplama.diyanet.gov.tr/dini_gunler.php)), so 9 March 2027 is already the official first day of the bayram.

**What this means for your trip:** treat every other date as expected until the evening before. Keep the first and last days of a trip flexible, and avoid plans that hinge on Eid falling on one particular day.

**Morocco's clocks.** A decree of 25 June 2026 put Morocco back on Greenwich Mean Time from 20 September 2026 and repealed the 2018 decree that had kept it an hour ahead ([Bulletin officiel No. 7521](https://www.sgg.gov.ma/BO/AR/3111/2026/BO_7521_Ar.pdf)). The new decree doesn't mention Ramadan, and by 20 November 2026 no clock change had been announced for Ramadan 2027. Morocco is now on GMT, an hour behind Madrid and Paris in winter.

## What changes for visitors during Ramadan?

**Fasting and eating in public.** Visitors aren't expected to fast. Dubai's government says non-Muslims "are not required to fast" ([Dubai government](https://www.dubai.ae/living/religious-affairs/the-holy-month-of-ramadan)). What varies is the rule on eating, drinking and smoking in public by day:

- **Forbidden for everyone in Oman,** says its foreign ministry ([Oman Ministry of Foreign Affairs](https://www.fm.gov.om/en/visitors/travel-advice-for-visitors/)).
- **An offence in Jordan:** article 274 of the penal code punishes breaking the fast in public with up to a month in prison or a fine (reported by [Roya News](https://en.royanews.tv/news/67837/Jail-or-15-JD-fine%3A-Penalties-for-public-iftar-in-Ramadan-in-Jordan)).
- **A courtesy in the UAE:** Visit Abu Dhabi says visitors can eat in restaurants, hotels and designated areas, and should simply avoid public outdoor spaces in daylight ([Visit Abu Dhabi](https://visitabudhabi.ae/en/plan-your-trip/article-hub/abu-dhabi-ramadan-questions-answered)).
- **A rule for Muslims in Malaysia:** in Penang, for example, the state's Islamic law can fine or jail healthy Muslims who eat or drink in public during Ramadan (reported by [The Star](https://thestar.com.my/news/nation/2022/03/31/healthy-muslims-who-eat-drink-in-public-during-ramadan-can-be-fined-imprisoned-in-penang) in 2022).

**Restaurants by day.** They stay open in Dubai and Abu Dhabi, and some are always open in Turkey's big cities. In Morocco, "most restaurants are closed" during the month (reported by [Le Petit Journal](https://lepetitjournal.com/casablanca/comprendre-maroc/tout-savoir-ramadan-2026-maroc)), and in Tunis most cafés and restaurants close by day outside tourist areas (reported by [Le Petit Journal](https://lepetitjournal.com/vivre-a-tunis/religion-tout-savoir-sur-le-ramadan-2025-406279)). The UK government warns that hotels and restaurants serving during fasting hours may seat non-fasting guests apart, for example behind screens ([FCDO, Turkey](https://www.gov.uk/foreign-travel-advice/turkey/safety-and-security)).

**Opening hours.** Offices work short days: 09:00–15:00 in Morocco's administrations in Ramadan 2026 (reported by [Hespress](https://fr.hespress.com/462007-ramadan-adoption-de-lhoraire-continu-par-les-administrations-les-etablissements-publics-et-les-collectivites-territoriales.html)), 09:00–14:30 in Jordan's government ([Petra](https://pt1.petra.gov.jo/en/news/ramadan-working-hours-from-900-am-to-230-pm)), and two hours less than usual in Dubai's public and private sectors. Some museums close earlier, and some mosques open late into the night; the country sections give the hours.

**Alcohol.** Tunisia bans alcohol sales during Ramadan, except at holiday resorts, says the UK government ([FCDO, Tunisia](https://www.gov.uk/foreign-travel-advice/tunisia/safety-and-security)). In Abu Dhabi, licensed hotels, bars and restaurants keep serving, usually indoors, and service may start only after iftar. In Morocco, many nightclubs close for the month (reported by [Bladi](https://www.bladi.net/maroc-alcool-boites-nuit-debut-treve-avant-ramadan,119033.html)). For the other countries we found no official Ramadan-specific rule, so ask your hotel.

**The hour before iftar.** The UK government notes that fasting can cause tiredness in the late afternoon and early evening, and that driving "may be erratic, particularly when people are trying to get home at dusk" ([FCDO, UAE](https://www.gov.uk/foreign-travel-advice/united-arab-emirates/safety-and-security)). Aim to be wherever you'll eat before sunset.

**Evenings.** Then cities come alive. Abu Dhabi's malls open until 1am or 2am at weekends during Ramadan; Egypt let shops, restaurants and cafés stay open until 02:00 throughout Ramadan and Eid 2026 ([Cairo Governorate](https://www.cairo.gov.eg/en/news/news-eng/2026/1f2be5ecf4bb42069031d5b640ceb291); also reported by [Egyptian Streets](https://egyptianstreets.com/2026/02/16/shops-and-cafes-to-stay-open-until-2am-throughout-ramadan-and-eid-in-egypt/)); and in 2026 Kuala Lumpur ran Ramadan bazaars at 41 sites, nine supervised by City Hall and 32 by hawker associations (reported by [Bernama](https://bernama.com/en/news.php?id=2515996)).

## What is each country like during Ramadan 2027?

### Morocco: is it a good idea to visit during Ramadan?

Yes, if you're happy with slow afternoons and long evenings. Morocco sets its dates by sighting, as above; in 2026 it started a day after Saudi Arabia.

**Daily life:** "most restaurants are closed" during the month and the streets are "almost deserted" in the afternoon (reported by Le Petit Journal). Public offices worked 09:00–15:00, Monday to Friday, in Ramadan 2026. The UK government advises discretion about eating, drinking or smoking in public by day and asking before you photograph people ([FCDO, Morocco](https://www.gov.uk/foreign-travel-advice/morocco/safety-and-security)).

**Getting around:** in Ramadan 2026 the rail operator ONCF ran 22 Al Boraq high-speed trains a day, more frequent 07:00–10:00 and 12:00–17:00, plus a 22:00 departure from Tangier and from Casablanca Voyageurs. Al Atlas trains on the Fès–Casablanca–Marrakech line ran between 04:30 and 17:30 (reported by [Le Matin](https://lematin.ma/economie/ramadan-2026-loncf-adapte-ses-horaires-et-renforce-son-offre-de-trains/330675)). The 2027 timetable isn't out.

**Crowds:** don't count on empty riads. A Marrakech hotelier quoted by L'Economiste described sustained demand in what used to be a quiet period, when hotels and restaurants often closed for the month (reported by [Le360](https://fr.le360.ma/economie/ramadan-ou-pas-le-tourisme-bat-son-plein-a-marrakech_CKQBVDZLF5BEFCHYZUAJRYUPJI/)).

**Eid:** the days are confirmed after the sighting. In 2026 the government added Monday 23 March as a day off for public administrations ([maroc.ma](https://maroc.ma/fr/actualites/aid-al-fitr-lundi-23-mars-jour-de-conge-exceptionnel-dans-les-administrations-publiques-et-les)), and ONCF ran a special Eid plan from 17 to 29 March, asking travellers to buy early and choose return tickets ([maroc.ma](https://www.maroc.ma/fr/actualites/loncf-met-en-place-un-dispositif-special-aid-al-fitr-et-fin-des-vacances-scolaires)).

**The trade-off:** fewer places to eat by day, quiet afternoons, and intercity trains bunched before sunset, so a travel day needs planning around iftar.

### Turkey: the smallest change, and a fixed calendar

**Dates:** Ramadan runs from 8 February to 8 March 2027 in the Diyanet's calendar, with the bayram on 9–11 March.

**Daily life:** Turkey's official tourism site describes Ramadan through its traditions: drummers waking neighbourhoods for sahur, the pre-dawn meal; messages in lights on mosques; Ramazan pide, a bread mostly baked only this month; iftar menus at restaurants and hotels; and Karagöz shadow plays ([Go Türkiye](https://goturkiye.com/halaltourism/ramadan-and-ramadan-feasts)). In the larger cities "there are always restaurants open all day", though in small towns you may need a picnic lunch, and city traffic may be heaviest just before sunset, says the independent guide [Turkey Travel Planner](https://turkeytravelplanner.com/Religion/ramazan_travel.html). The UK government still advises discretion about eating in public by day. In 2026 Istanbul's municipality set up free iftar tables in squares including Eminönü and Üsküdar (reported by [TGRT Haber](https://www.tgrthaber.com/aktuel/istanbul-iftar-cadiri-yerleri-2026-uskudar-tuzla-maltepe-esenler-eminonu-sisli-sultan-3283307)).

**The bayram:** by law the holiday lasts three and a half days from 13:00 on the eve ([Law 2429](https://www.mevzuat.gov.tr/mevzuatmetin/1.5.2429.pdf)), so from 13:00 on Monday 8 March to the end of Thursday 11 March 2027. The government can add leave for civil servants, as it did in May 2026, when Kurban Bayramı became a nine-day break (reported by [memurlar.net](https://www.memurlar.net/haber/1166565/)). We found no such announcement for March 2027 by 20 November 2026.

**What closes:** on the first day of the 2026 Ramazan Bayramı, Topkapı and Dolmabahçe palaces were shut, the culture ministry's museums opened only in the afternoon and Istanbul's municipal museums at 13:00 (reported by [Habertürk](https://www.haberturk.com/bayramda-muzeler-acik-mi-kapali-mi-2026-ramazan-bayraminda-muzeler-saraylar-ucretsiz-mi-rmz06-3868750)). Move Topkapı off 9 March; our [three-day Istanbul itinerary](/blog/istanbul-3-day-itinerary) reshuffles easily.

**The travel surge:** over 19–22 March 2026, Turkey's airports handled 2.53 million passengers on 16,851 flights, and intercity buses carried 1.63 million (reported by [TRT Haber](https://www.trthaber.com/haber/turkiye/ramazan-bayraminda-otoyol-ve-koprulerden-123-milyon-arac-gecti-938782.html)). State motorways and two of the Bosphorus bridges were free from 19 to 22 March, and Marmaray, Başkentray, İZBAN and the Istanbul Airport metro line were free from 20 to 22 March (reported by [TRT Haber](https://www.trthaber.com/haber/ekonomi/bakan-uraloglu-ramazan-bayraminda-herkesin-yolunu-kolaylastirmayi-amacliyoruz-938204.html)).

**The trade-off:** days barely change, but the bayram fills planes and buses and shuts palaces on its first day, so book bayram-week transport early.

### Dubai and Abu Dhabi: easy days, late nights

**Dates and Eid:** the UAE's Astronomy Centre expects 8 February and 9 or 10 March. By Cabinet Resolution No. 27 of 2024, the Eid holiday is 1–3 Shawwal, and the 30th of Ramadan is added if the month runs 30 days ([FAHR](https://www.fahr.gov.ae/en/news/eid-al-fitr-holiday-for-the-federal-government-from-1st-to-3rd-shawwal/)). In 2026 the federal government's break ran from Thursday 19 to Sunday 22 March ([FAHR circular](https://www.fahr.gov.ae/wp-content/uploads/2026/02/Circular-No.-2-of-2026-Regarding-the-Eid-Al-Fitr-Holiday-for-the-Year-1447H.pdf)). If Ramadan 1448 runs 30 days, the holiday would be 9–12 March, by our reading of the rule, with the weekend making six days to 14 March.

**Daily life:** Dubai's government says there are "no specific restrictions" for non-Muslims, that restaurants "operate as normal", that abstaining from eating, drinking or smoking in public is "an act of kindness", and that working hours are two hours shorter. It asks for modest clothing and no loud music. Visit Abu Dhabi adds that licensed venues serve alcohol, usually indoors and sometimes only after iftar, and that malls open until 1am or 2am at weekends.

**Getting around:** in Ramadan 2026 the Dubai Metro ran 05:00 to midnight Monday to Thursday and Saturday, 05:00 to 01:00 on Friday and 08:00 to midnight on Sunday, and the tram until 01:00 every day ([RTA](https://www.rta.ae/wps/portal/rta/ae/home/news-and-media/all-news/NewsDetails/announcing-service-hours-during-the-holy-month-of-ramadan)).

**Mosques and iftar:** Abu Dhabi's Sheikh Zayed Grand Mosque opens to visitors 09:00–18:00 and 21:30–01:00 in the first 20 days of Ramadan (Fridays 14:30–18:00 and 21:30–01:00), then 09:00–18:00 in the last ten, and visitors pre-book an online pass ([SZGMC](https://szgmc.gov.ae/en/worshippers/en-to-visiting-hours)). It served 980,010 iftar meals in Ramadan 2026 ([Abu Dhabi Media Office](https://www.mediaoffice.abudhabi/en/community/sheikh-zayed-grand-mosque-and-sheikh-khalifa-grand-mosque-key-destinations-during-ramadan-and-eid-al-fitr-2026/)). In Dubai, the Sheikh Mohammed Centre for Cultural Understanding runs an iftar in Al Fahidi with a talk, dates, an Emirati buffet, a mosque tour and questions, at Dh180 a person in 2025 (reported by [Khaleej Times](https://www.khaleejtimes.com/lifestyle/restaurant-reviews/how-a-humble-courtyard-iftar-outshines-dubais-flashiest-ramadan-tents)). Global Village opened 17:00–01:00 Sunday to Wednesday and 17:00–02:00 Thursday to Saturday in Ramadan 2026 (reported by [Khaleej Times](https://www.khaleejtimes.com/uae/dubai-global-village-ramadan-2026-timings)).

**Before you book:** on 18 November 2026 the UK government warned of possible flight cancellations and airspace closures across the Middle East ([FCDO](https://www.gov.uk/foreign-travel-advice/united-arab-emirates)). Check your own government's advice and prefer refundable bookings.

**The trade-off:** daytime meals move indoors and drinks may wait until after iftar, and the regional situation, not Ramadan, is the bigger unknown.

### Egypt: museums by day, Cairo by night

**Daily life:** the UK government calls eating, drinking or smoking in public in daylight "culturally insensitive" during Ramadan, and advises against loud music. Drinking alcohol is legal only in licensed venues, private homes and some resorts, it says ([FCDO, Egypt](https://www.gov.uk/foreign-travel-advice/egypt/safety-and-security)).

**Hours:** in Ramadan 2026 the Grand Egyptian Museum's complex opened 08:30–17:00 and its main galleries 09:00–16:00, with the last tickets sold at 15:00 (reported by [Egypt Telegraph](https://www.egypttelegraph.com/article/221090/مواعيد-وأسعار-تذاكر-المتحف-المصري-الكبير-في)). The 2027 hours aren't announced. Shops, restaurants and cafés could stay open until 02:00 throughout Ramadan and Eid 2026 ([Cairo Governorate](https://www.cairo.gov.eg/en/news/news-eng/2026/1f2be5ecf4bb42069031d5b640ceb291)); the 2027 rule isn't announced.

**Eid:** set by the prime minister each year. In 2026 the public sector's paid holiday ran from 19 to 23 March ([Cairo Governorate](https://www.cairo.gov.eg/en/news/news-eng/2026/0a1f1510fc5940479a6f3bbab133859f)).

**The trade-off:** museum days end mid-afternoon, so you fit fewer sights into a day; the late evenings make up for it.

### Jordan: plan where you eat and drink

**Daily life:** the Jordan Tourism Board's advice is direct: "Don't openly consume food, beverages, or cigarettes in public places during the holy month of Ramadan" ([Jordan Tourism Board](https://international.visitjordan.com/page/11/local-customs/)). That matches the penal code above. Government offices worked 09:00–14:30 in Ramadan 2026.

**Eid:** in 2026 the prime minister set the public-sector holiday from Friday 20 to Monday 23 March ([Petra](https://pt1.petra.gov.jo/en/news/prime-minister-announces-eid-al-fitr-holiday-from-march-2023)).

**Before you book:** the UK government advises against all travel within 3 km (about 2 miles) of the Syrian border and warns of regional tensions ([FCDO, Jordan](https://www.gov.uk/foreign-travel-advice/jordan)).

**The trade-off:** you need private places to eat and drink by day, which takes planning on long sightseeing days.

### Oman: the firmest daytime rule

**Daily life:** Oman's foreign ministry says that in Ramadan "eating, drinking and smoking in public in daylight hours are forbidden", for people of all faiths. Tourists can drink alcohol in licensed venues, and should ask before photographing people.

**Mosque visits:** the Sultan Qaboos Grand Mosque in Muscat opens to non-Muslims Saturday to Thursday, 08:00–11:00, with shoulders and knees covered and women's heads covered; admission was 8 Omani rials when we checked ([Experience Oman](https://experienceoman.om/destinations/muscat/sultan-qaboos-grand-mosque)). The page gives no Ramadan hours, so check before you go.

**Eid:** in 2026 the holiday ran from Thursday 19 to Monday 23 March for both the public and private sectors (reported by [Times of Oman](https://timesofoman.com/article/169350-eid-al-fitr-holidays-announced-in-oman-1)).

**The trade-off:** with Jordan, Oman has the firmest daytime rule we found, so every lunch and coffee needs a private place.

### Tunisia: resorts are the exception

**Daily life:** outside tourist areas most cafés and restaurants close by day, and after dinner people go out for tea or traditional shows (reported by Le Petit Journal). Alcohol sales are banned during Ramadan and fewer restaurants and bars serve, but the rules don't apply at holiday resorts, says the UK government.

**Eid:** in 2026 public agents had three days off, 20–22 March, by decision of the head of government's office (reported by [Business News](https://businessnews.com.tn/2026/03/16/aid-esseghir-trois-jours-de-conge-pour-les-agents-publics-en-tunisie/1392305/)).

**The trade-off:** outside a resort, daytime cafés and any alcohol are hard to find for the whole month.

### Malaysia: bazaars, and two holidays back to back

**Dates:** the official 2027 list gives the start of Ramadan (8 February) as a holiday in Johor and Kedah; Nuzul Al-Quran (Wednesday 24 February) as a holiday in Kuala Lumpur, Selangor, Penang and seven other states and territories; and Hari Raya Puasa on Wednesday 10 and Thursday 11 March nationwide, plus Friday 12 March in Melaka. Awal Ramadan and Hari Raya Puasa are marked "subject to change".

**Daily life:** Ramadan rules on eating in public bind Muslims; the UK government notes that Muslims "may be subject to local Sharia law" ([FCDO, Malaysia](https://www.gov.uk/foreign-travel-advice/malaysia/safety-and-security)). For visitors the draw is the evening bazaars: in 2026 Kuala Lumpur had nine supervised by City Hall (DBKL) and 32 by hawker associations (reported by Bernama).

**Lunar New Year first:** Chinese New Year falls on Saturday 6 and Sunday 7 February 2027, two days before Ramadan. Our [Lunar New Year guide](/blog/lunar-new-year-where-to-celebrate) covers Malaysia's celebrations.

**Hari Raya:** before Hari Raya 2026, the government halved tolls for private cars on all highways on 18 and 19 March, except at the two crossings to Singapore ([RTM](https://berita.rtm.gov.my/?p=588398)). The same year, ministries and agencies dropped their Hari Raya open houses to save money ([RTM](https://berita.rtm.gov.my/?p=579448)), so don't plan around official open houses.

**The trade-off:** the bazaars only come alive in the late afternoon and evening, and the official Hari Raya open houses you may have read about were cancelled in 2026.

### Indonesia and Bali: Nyepi and Lebaran in the same week

**Dates:** Indonesia's 2027 holiday decree makes Idul Fitri a national holiday on Wednesday 10 and Thursday 11 March, with collective leave days (cuti bersama) on 9, 12 and 15 March. Those leave days bind civil servants and are optional for private firms ([Kemenko PMK](https://kemenkopmk.go.id/pemerintah-tetapkan-18-hari-libur-nasional-dan-8-cuti-bersama-tahun-2027)). With Nyepi on Monday 8 March and two weekends, civil servants are off for ten days in a row, from 6 to 15 March, by our count. Muhammadiyah's calendar puts Eid on 9 March, so Indonesia may again celebrate on two different days, as in 2026.

**Bali:** 86.5% of Bali's people were Hindu in 2024 (Katadata, [Databoks](https://databoks.katadata.co.id/en/demographics/statistics/3f7a042c5684228/865-of-balis-population-is-hindu)), and the date that shapes a Bali trip is Nyepi, the Hindu day of silence. It has clashed with the Eid homecoming before: in 2025 it fell in the expected peak (reported by [Antara](https://mataram.antaranews.com/berita/417634/bali-airport-to-shut-down-for-nyepi-impacting-eid-homecoming)). In 2026 the island's airport stopped regular flights from 06:00 WITA (UTC+8) on 19 March to 06:00 WITA on 20 March (reported by [Antara](https://bali.antaranews.com/berita/400962/bandara-ngurah-rai-berhenti-operasi-sementara-24-jam-selama-nyepi)), and ferries to and from Java stopped too (reported by [CNBC Indonesia](https://www.cnbcindonesia.com/news/20260316104813-4-719313/catat-bandara-terminal-pelabuhan-di-bali-tutup-di-tanggal-segini)). The 2027 closure times aren't announced. Our [seven-day Bali itinerary](/blog/bali-7-day-itinerary) is easy to shift around 8 March.

**Mudik:** Indonesians call the journey home for Lebaran mudik. 147.55 million people travelled during the 2026 Lebaran transport period, 13–29 March (reported by [Kontan](https://nasional.kontan.co.id/news/angkutan-lebaran-2026-resmi-ditutup-total-pergerakan-capai-14755-juta-orang)). The rail operator KAI sells Lebaran tickets 45 days before departure; in 2026 sales opened on 25 January for 11 March trains (reported by [Antara](https://jogja.antaranews.com/berita/798778/tiket-kereta-api-lebaran-bisa-dipesan-mulai-25-januari)). Domestic airfares climbed high enough to draw complaints in March 2026, and the transport ministry's answer was that they stayed within the legal fare cap (reported by [CNN Indonesia](https://www.cnnindonesia.com/gaya-hidup/20260317180703-269-1339143/kemenhub-tanggapi-keluhan-tarif-tiket-pesawat-mahal-saat-libur-lebaran)).

**Jakarta:** afternoon takjil markets sell snacks for breaking the fast; Jakarta's government describes the Benhil market in Central Jakarta, where one seller traded 14:00–19:00 ([Berita Jakarta](https://m.beritajakarta.id/en/read/49796/hunting-favorite-takjil-at-benhil-market)).

**The trade-off:** 6–15 March 2027 is the stretch to avoid moving around Indonesia: Nyepi comes first, when Bali's airport closed for a day in 2026, then the exodus and high fares.

## Is it a good idea to travel during Ramadan?

**The case for going:**

- **Evenings:** iftar crowds, lit-up mosques, bazaars and late openings, from Egypt's 02:00 shop hours in 2026 to the Sheikh Zayed Grand Mosque's late-night visiting slot.
- **Ways in for visitors:** Dubai's cultural-centre iftar, public iftar tables in Istanbul's squares, and Kuala Lumpur's bazaars.
- **The sights we checked stay open,** often on shorter hours.

**The case against:**

- **Shorter days:** galleries closing at 16:00 at the Grand Egyptian Museum, offices at 14:30 or 15:00.
- **Fewer places to eat by day** in Morocco and Tunisia, and strict rules on eating in public in Jordan and Oman.
- **Alcohol is harder to find,** and banned from sale in Tunisia outside resorts.
- **Busy roads at dusk,** as people head home for iftar, and slow afternoons.
- **Eid week** brings crowds, closures and, in Indonesia, high domestic fares.

**Quieter or cheaper?** We found no official figures showing lower hotel prices during Ramadan. In Marrakech, a hotelier describes demand holding up in what used to be a slack month (reported by Le360).

Go if evenings and atmosphere matter more to you than long sightseeing days. If you dislike crowds, avoid 6–15 March 2027.

## How to be a good guest during Ramadan

- **Clothes:** dress modestly in public, and cover shoulders and knees at mosques. The UK government advises dressing modestly "especially in rural areas, mosques and souqs" in Egypt; Oman's Grand Mosque asks for covered shoulders and knees, and for women to cover their heads.
- **Eating, drinking and smoking:** follow the local rule above, and in doubt, eat indoors.
- **Noise:** skip loud music in public, as Dubai's government asks.
- **Photography:** ask before photographing people, as Oman's foreign ministry and the UK government advise. In Egypt, the UK government says you need a person's written permission to photograph them, and that taking pictures of children is prohibited.
- **Prayer:** "Don't interrupt, or pass in front of, a Muslim who may be praying in a public place," says the Jordan Tourism Board.
- **Being a guest at iftar:** Dubai's government asks guests to show respect when invited. Etiquette advice quoted by The National: never arrive earlier than invited, avoid being late, follow your host's lead and dress modestly; no gift is expected, but bringing something, such as dates, is appreciated, and "Ramadan Kareem" is a kind greeting (reported by [The National](https://www.thenationalnews.com/lifestyle/2026/02/23/ramadan-etiquette-for-non-muslims/)). Your host may insist on refilling your plate, as the Jordan Tourism Board notes.
- **Fasting yourself:** non-Muslims don't have to, but joining in is seen as respectful (The National).

## Frequently asked questions

### When does Ramadan 2027 start and end?

It's expected to start on Monday 8 February 2027 and end with Eid al-Fitr on 9 or 10 March. Turkey's official calendar runs from 8 February to 8 March, with Eid on 9 March. Elsewhere the dates are confirmed by moon sighting, often the evening before, and can move by a day.

### When is Eid al-Fitr 2027?

Tuesday 9 March in Turkey's official calendar, and expected that day in the Umm al-Qura and Muhammadiyah calendars; expected on Wednesday 10 March in the official holiday lists of Malaysia and Indonesia. Most countries confirm the evening before, so dates outside Turkey can move by a day.

### Can tourists eat and drink in public during Ramadan?

It depends on the country. It's forbidden by day in Oman; in Jordan, breaking the fast in public is an offence and the tourism board asks visitors not to eat, drink or smoke openly; in Dubai and Abu Dhabi restaurants stay open and the courtesy is not to eat outdoors in public; in Malaysia the rules apply to Muslims.

### Is alcohol available during Ramadan?

In Tunisia sales are banned for the month outside resorts. In Abu Dhabi licensed venues keep serving, sometimes only after iftar. Elsewhere, ask your hotel.

### Are museums and sights open during Ramadan?

The ones we checked stay open, often on shorter hours: the Grand Egyptian Museum's galleries closed at 16:00 in Ramadan 2026, while Abu Dhabi's Grand Mosque opens late at night in the first 20 days of Ramadan. The big exception is the first day of Eid in Turkey, when palaces closed in 2026.

### Is Bali affected by Ramadan?

Less than by Nyepi, Bali's day of silence, on Monday 8 March 2027 (in 2026 its airport closed for 24 hours), and by Indonesia's Lebaran travel around 9–15 March.

### Do Ramadan and Chinese New Year overlap in 2027?

They're back to back: Lunar New Year is Saturday 6 February and Ramadan is expected to start on Monday 8 February.

### Is Easter 2027 during Ramadan?

No. Easter Sunday is 28 March 2027, more than two weeks after Eid.

### Does Morocco change its clocks for Ramadan 2027?

No change had been announced by 20 November 2026. Morocco has been on GMT since 20 September 2026, under a decree that repealed the GMT+1 rule it used to suspend for Ramadan; the decree itself doesn't mention Ramadan.
