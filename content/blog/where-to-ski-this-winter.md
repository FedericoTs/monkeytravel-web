# Where to Ski in Winter 2026-27: Snow-Sure Resorts, Cheapest Weeks and New Year on the Slopes

For snow you can count on at New Year, go high: Zermatt, Saas-Fee, Sölden and Hintertux ski on glaciers from the autumn, and Tignes and Val Thorens open on 21 November 2026 and stay open until 2 May 2027. The cheapest weeks are roughly 7–24 January and 8–26 March, and the stretch to avoid runs from 6 February to 7 March 2027, when French, Dutch, German and English schools take turns on holiday. On a tighter budget, an adult day costs €70–74 at Baqueira-Beret in the Pyrenees and €56–72 at Sierra Nevada, against CHF 86 in Saas-Fee and from CHF 89 in Zermatt.

This guide is for anyone planning a ski or snow trip between December 2026 and April 2027, from a first week on skis to a group of friends chasing powder in Japan. Each pick ends with its honest downside.

Every date and price was read on the resort's own website or an official calendar on 20 November 2026; figures from the press or independent sites are marked. Opening dates depend on snow, and several resorts now change prices by date. Times are local: CET in the Alps, the Pyrenees and Scandinavia. Euro conversions use the European Central Bank's rate of 19 November 2026.

## When does the 2026-27 ski season start and end?

On the glaciers it barely stops. Of the resorts below, the high ones start in late November and most others in early December. These are the resorts' planned dates; early on, only part of each area runs, and [Sölden](https://www.soelden.com/ski-pass-prices) says the start and the number of open lifts depend on snow.

- **Glaciers:** [Hintertux](https://www.hintertuxergletscher.at/en/) is open 365 days a year. Sölden's glacier calendar began on 25 September, [Cervinia's](https://cervinia.it/en/impianti) on 24 October, [Saas-Fee's](https://www.saas-fee.ch/en/winter-in-the-saas-valley) on 31 October and [Zermatt's winter timetable](https://www.matterhornparadise.ch/ZBAG/17-PDF/flyer/flyer-winter/2026-2027/fahrplan_winter%202026_27.pdf) on 1 November. Austria's tourist board lists the [Stubai Glacier from 9 October and the Kitzsteinhorn from 10 October](https://www.austria.info/en/things-to-do/skiing-and-winter/winteropenings-and-winterclosings).
- **France:** [Tignes](https://www.tignes.net/), Val Thorens and Orelle on 21 November, Courchevel on 4 December and most of the rest of [Les 3 Vallées](https://www.les3vallees.com/en/live/lifts-and-trails-opening) on 5 December. Tignes, Val Thorens and Orelle close on 2 May; most of the rest of Les 3 Vallées on 18 April.
- **Austria:** Obergurgl-Hochgurgl on 19 November, Ischgl on 26 November, Saalbach (in part) on 27 November, St. Anton and Lech Zürs on 2 December, Mayrhofen on 4 December, SkiWelt on 5 December and all of KitzSki on 11 December, according to [Austria's tourist board](https://www.austria.info/en/things-to-do/skiing-and-winter/winteropenings-and-winterclosings).
- **Switzerland:** Zermatt's Rothorn and Gornergrat areas from 28 November to 18 April. Saas-Grund and Saas-Almagell from 19 December to 4 April.
- **Italy:** Dolomiti Superski from 28 November, when its season pass starts, with areas closing between 29 March (Civetta) and 2 May (Cortina d'Ampezzo), as listed by [Dolomiti.it](https://www.dolomiti.it/en/dolomites/news/dolomiti-superski-winter-ski-season). Cervinia runs to 2 May, and Valtournenche from 28 November to 25 April.
- **Pyrenees and Andorra:** [Baqueira-Beret](https://www.baqueira.es/en/noticias) from 28 November to 4 April and [Grandvalira](https://www.grandvalira.com/en/faqs/general-information) from 4 December to 4 April.
- **Sierra Nevada:** the official price calendar runs from 28 November to 18 April, but the resort's pages we checked on 20 November gave no opening date.
- **Bulgaria:** [Bansko](https://www.banskoski.com/en/page/prices) from 1 December to 11 April.
- **Scandinavia:** [Trysil](https://www.skistar.com/en/ski-destinations/trysil/winter-in-trysil/weather-and-slopes/opening-hours-lifts/) from 4 December to 18 April; [Åre](https://www.skistar.com/en/ski-destinations/are/winter-in-are/weather-and-slopes/opening-hours-lifts/) from 11 December, or earlier if snow allows, to 2 May.
- **Japan:** all four [Niseko United](https://www.niseko.ne.jp/en/lift) areas on 28 November, closing on 4 April (Niseko Village), 11 April (Hanazono) and 5 May (Grand Hirafu and Annupuri). Hakuba's resorts have their own dates.
- **North America:** Vail Mountain on 13 November and Whistler Blackcomb and Park City on 20 November were [Vail Resorts' targets](https://news.vailresorts.com/2026-08-18-Is-Winter-About-to-Show-Off-Vail-Resorts-Announces-2026-27-Opening-Dates-as-Super-El-Nino-Builds-Anticipation-for-Great-Powder), "subject to change based on weather and conditions".

## Is any ski resort guaranteed to have snow over New Year?

No resort can promise natural snow. What gets you close is glacier terrain, height and snowmaking. In Tignes, 93% of runs are above 2,000 m, and its piste service puts average snowfall at 142 cm (56 in) in December and 143 cm (56 in) in January, according to the [resort](https://www.tignes.net/ski/enneigement-garanti). In Les 3 Vallées, over 2,800 snow guns cover 55% of the area, according to its [own page](https://www.les3vallees.com/en/ski-pass/ski-guarantee/).

Tignes's site says "neige garantie tout l'hiver" (snow guaranteed all winter), but its snow page explains the height and snowfall rather than offering money back. Les 3 Vallées' [ski guarantee](https://www.les3vallees.com/en/guide/the-world-s-largest-ski-area/ski-guarantee) does pay out, in a narrow case: if another European ski area has more connected runs open, it refunds the days you didn't ski as a credit note. It covers 3 Vallées passes of six days or more bought online or in a package (not season passes), only closures for lack of snow count, and you must send the proof yourself within two months.

The plainest guarantee is in Scandinavia. SkiStar's [Alpine Snow Guarantee](https://skistar.com/en/inspiration/snow-guarantee/) runs from 18 December 2026 to 4 April 2027, so it covers New Year. If fewer than a set number of slopes are open (30 in Åre and Sälen, 20 in Vemdalen, 15 in Trysil and 13 in Hemsedal), you can cancel a stay booked with SkiStar for a full refund, until 11:00 the day after you arrive. It doesn't cover temporary closures for bad weather or technical faults.

## Snow-sure picks for early season and New Year

### Zermatt and Cervinia, Switzerland and Italy

**Why here:** skiing 365 days a year, 360 km (224 mi) of pistes and a top at almost 4,000 m, according to the [lift company](https://www.matterhornparadise.ch/en/experience/skiing).

**When:** the winter timetable runs from 1 November 2026 to 2 May 2027. The Sunnegga–Rothorn and Gornergrat areas open from 28 November to 18 April. Cervinia, on the Italian side, runs from 24 October to 2 May.

**Price:** these are [starting prices](https://www.matterhornparadise.ch/ZBAG/13-trade/01-downloads/03-prospekte-broschueren/starting-prices-ski-passes-winter-26-27.pdf) that change daily. A Zermatt day starts at CHF 89 (about €96), or CHF 100 (about €108) in peak periods. With Cervinia it's from CHF 104 and CHF 116. Six days start at CHF 384 (about €415) before 28 November and CHF 488 (about €527) at peak. Children under 9 ski free, and under-16s ski free in Zermatt on Saturdays.

**Getting there:** Zermatt is [car-free](https://zermatt.swiss/en/arrival). Drivers park in Täsch, 5 km (3 mi) away, and take a shuttle train that runs every 20 minutes and takes 12. Zurich Airport is about three and a half hours away, and Geneva and Basel about four.

**The trade-off:** the dearest pick here, with prices that change daily, and the peak rate covers both New Year (19 December to 3 January) and 25 January to 7 March. From 12 April the Sunnegga funicular closes for works.

### Saas-Fee, Switzerland

**Why here:** a glacier season from 31 October 2026 to 18 April 2027.

**Price:** an adult day costs [CHF 86](https://www.saas-fee.ch/en/winter-in-the-saas-valley/ski-pass-prices/saas-fee) (about €93) and six days CHF 419 (about €452) on the Saas-Fee Pass, which also covers Saas-Almagell. Under-6s ski free. From 1 November to 18 December, a [pre-season deal](https://www.saas-fee.ch/en/accommodations/packages-and-deals) takes 10% off stays of three nights or more, with an optional 10% off the pass.

**New Year:** parties in the village on 31 December; the [full programme](https://www.saas-fee.ch/en/events/top-events-in-winter/new-years-eve) was still to come on 20 November.

**The trade-off:** Saas-Grund and Saas-Almagell don't open until 19 December and close on 4 April, so early in the season only Saas-Fee's own area runs, and the season ends two weeks before Tignes and Val Thorens.

### Tignes, France

**Why here:** runs from 1,550 m to 3,456 m, linked with Val d'Isère for over 300 km (186 mi), on a season from 21 November 2026 to 2 May 2027.

**Price:** an adult day on [Tignes–Val d'Isère](https://www.tignes.net/ski/forfaits-ski) costs €67 from 28 November to 11 December and €78 from 12 December to 23 April. Tignes alone is €60 or €70. Six days cost €402 or €468, with a seventh day free. Under-8s and skiers aged 75 and over ski free.

**Getting there:** Eurostar's ski train ends at Bourg-Saint-Maurice, where [Eurostar says](https://www.eurostar.com/uk-en/train/ski-train) coaches run on to Tignes.

**The trade-off:** the link to Val d'Isère is only planned from 28 November, and the full price starts on 12 December, so New Year is charged at the top rate.

### Val Thorens, France

**Why here:** Les 3 Vallées calls it [the highest resort in Europe](https://www.les3vallees.com/en/ski-pass/ski-guarantee/), at 2,320 m, and its lifts connect to 600 km (373 mi) of runs. Val Thorens and Orelle run from 21 November 2026 to 2 May 2027, two weeks longer at each end than the rest of Les 3 Vallées.

**Price:** a [Les 3 Vallées](https://www.les3vallees.com/en/guide/all-skipasses/day-passes) adult day costs €84.20 from 19 December, or €67.30 on Saturdays. Six days cost €421, or €378.50 in the [early-December window](https://www.les3vallees.com/en/news/skipass-early-december-special-rates) (the page gives both 5–18 and 6–19 December). Under-5s ski free.

**Getting there:** Eurostar's ski train stops at Moûtiers.

**The trade-off:** the dearest French day pass in this guide, and outside Val Thorens and Orelle the area closes between 9 and 18 April.

### Sölden, Austria

**Why here:** two seasons in one. The [glacier calendar](https://www.soelden.com/ski-pass-prices) started on 25 September, the whole area runs from 12 November 2026 to 18 April 2027, and the glacier alone carries on to 2 May. From 19 November, passes of three days or more also cover the Ötztal's other five ski areas, Obergurgl-Hochgurgl among them.

**Price:** at the ticket desk, an adult day costs €81 until 4 December, €84.50 from 5 to 18 December, 7 to 29 January and 27 February to 18 April, and €86.50 in the top band, 19 December to 6 January and 30 January to 26 February, on Sölden's [2026/27 price list](https://www.soelden.com/en/activities/winter/skiing-snowboarding/skipasses-prices/winter). Online, passes are cheaper the earlier you buy. The Epic Pass includes [five days here](https://www.soelden.com/en/activities/winter/skiing-snowboarding/epic-pass).

**Getting there:** [83 km (52 mi) from Innsbruck Airport](https://www.soelden.com/getting-here-by-airplane-winter), about 1 hour 15 minutes by car.

**The trade-off:** New Year and most of February fall in the top price band, and early in the season the number of open lifts depends on snow.

### Hintertux, Austria

**Why here:** the glacier is open 365 days a year, from 08:15 to 16:30 CET.

**Price:** [€82 for an adult day and €399 for six](https://www.hintertuxergletscher.at/en/tickets-rates/tickets-rates/rates-hintertux-glacier/), the same all season, from 17 October 2026 to 7 May 2027. From 4 December to 11 April, the day ticket also covers the Zillertal 3000 area, and multi-day passes all of the Zillertal.

**The trade-off:** no cheap weeks, and until 4 December your ticket covers the glacier only.

Sources: [French education ministry](https://data.education.gouv.fr/explore/dataset/fr-en-calendrier-scolaire/), [Dutch government](https://www.rijksoverheid.nl/themas/onderwijs/schoolvakanties/overzicht-schoolvakanties-per-schooljaar/overzicht-schoolvakanties-2026-2027), [KMK](https://www.kmk.org/service/ferienregelung/ferienkalender.html) for Germany, and the [Leeds](https://www.leeds.gov.uk/schools-and-education/school-holidays-and-term-dates) and [Ealing](https://www.ealing.gov.uk/info/201123/your_child_at_school/119/term_and_holiday_dates/2/) councils. French dates run to the day classes restart. In England, academies and some other schools set their own dates.

**Prices:** Zermatt's peak runs from 19 December to 3 January, Sölden's top band to 6 January, and the full Les 3 Vallées day price and SkiWelt's main season both start on 19 December. In Japan, Niseko's peak price starts on 24 December.

**Small print:** in Åre, all lifts close at 15:00 CET on 24 December, and there's evening skiing from 18:00 to 20:00 between 25 December and 3 January, except on Wednesdays and New Year's Eve. Saas-Fee lists New Year's Eve parties in the village. Our [New Year's Eve in the snow guide](/blog/new-years-eve-in-the-snow) covers torchlit ski shows, mountain huts and Arctic cabins.

**What to book first, and when.** No authority sets booking windows, so this is our advice.

1. **Now:** your bed and your travel. Eurostar's ski train leaves London on Saturdays from 19 December, so for New Year you'd go on 26 December and return on Sunday 3 January.
2. **Next:** ski school and childcare, which fill up in holiday weeks.
3. **Once dates are fixed:** lift passes online. Sölden says passes are cheaper the earlier you buy online, Grandvalira's are up to 15% cheaper in advance, and Zermatt's are starting prices that change daily.
4. **Last:** equipment rental and a table for New Year's Eve.

If your dates can move, ski from 9 or 16 January instead. Zermatt's basic rate returns on 4 January and Sölden's lower band on 7 January, after most schools are back.

## Which are the cheapest weeks to ski in 2027?

Where resorts publish price calendars, the same windows come up.

- **Early to mid January (about 7–24 January):** Zermatt's basic rate runs from 4 to 24 January and Sölden's lower band from 7 to 29 January. Our [January guide](/blog/where-to-go-in-january) has more ideas for the month.
- **March before Easter (about 8–26 March):** Zermatt's basic rate runs from 8 to 26 March, Sölden's lower band from 27 February to 18 April, and SkiWelt's lower price from 13 March. Niseko's regular price returns on 1 March. Easter is on 28 March 2027 ([GOV.UK](https://www.gov.uk/bank-holidays.json) lists Good Friday as 26 March), and Zermatt's peak returns from 27 March. See our [March guide](/blog/where-to-go-in-march).
- **Early December:** Tignes–Val d'Isère is €67 a day until 11 December, Les 3 Vallées has early-December rates, SkiWelt charges €71.50 until 18 December, and Saas-Fee's pre-season deal runs to 18 December. Expect less of each area open.
- **Late April:** Tignes drops back to €67 from 24 April, Les 3 Vallées has late-April rates from 10 to 18 April, and Zermatt's pre-season band returns from 19 April.

"Late January" is not cheap everywhere: Zermatt's peak starts on 25 January and Sölden's top band on 30 January. Nor is all of January: SkiWelt charges its main-season price from 19 December to 12 March, Niseko's peak runs from 24 December to 28 February, and Hintertux charges one price all season. Les 3 Vallées also sells a Saturday day pass for €67.30, against €84.20 for a standard day.

### Bansko, Bulgaria

**Why here:** a season pass for €870 and, last winter, a €59 day, on a season from 1 December 2026 to 11 April 2027.

**Price:** on 20 November, Bansko had published only its [season pass](https://www.banskoski.com/en/page/prices): €870 for adults if bought by 1 December, with mountain insurance included. Last winter an adult day cost €59 and six days €341, on the [2025-26 price list](https://web.archive.org/web/20260201165457/https://www.banskoski.com/en/page/ceni) (archived copy).

**Getting there:** the closest airport is Sofia, [150 km (93 mi) away](https://www.banskoski.com/en/page/getting-there); Thessaloniki is 220 km (137 mi).

**The trade-off:** this winter's day prices aren't out, the season ends on 11 April, and the nearest airport is 150 km away.

### Andorra: Grandvalira

**Why here:** 215 km (134 mi) of slopes from 4 December 2026 to 4 April 2027, with lifts running from 9:00 to 17:00 CET.

**Price:** [prices are dynamic](https://www.grandvalira.com/en/ski-pass/day), shown only once you choose dates, and up to 15% lower online in advance. Passes for two or more consecutive days also work at Ordino Arcalís and Pal Arinsal. The Ikon Pass gives [up to seven days](https://www.grandvalira.com/en/ski-pass/ikon-pass) here.

**Getting there:** the nearest airports are La Seu d'Urgell, 27 km (17 mi) away, Toulouse at 195 km (121 mi) and Barcelona at 200 km (124 mi), with [buses from Barcelona and Toulouse](https://www.grandvalira.com/en/resort/how-to-arrive-park).

**The trade-off:** you can't compare prices until you pick dates, and the nearest big airports are about 200 km away.

### Baqueira-Beret, Spain

**Why here:** a Pyrenean season from 28 November 2026 to 4 April 2027, with a cheap first day for beginners.

**Price:** an adult day costs [€74 on a Baqueira Ticket or €70 on the Baqueira Pass card](https://www.baqueira.es/en/tarifas); six days €359 or €339.50. An adult beginner day costs €26 at the ticket offices, and insurance is €4 a day.

**The trade-off:** the cheaper Baqueira Pass card costs [€25 to issue](https://www.baqueira.es/en/faqs) and takes about five working days to arrive by post, so it pays only if you plan ahead or come back. The season ends on 4 April, the Sunday after Easter.

### Sierra Nevada, Spain

**Why here:** the lowest published day price in Europe that we found for this winter outside Tignes's opening week, close to Granada.

**Price:** the official [ticket-office price calendar](https://sierranevada.es/es/invierno/forfaits/tarifas-en-taquillas-y-cajeros/) charges an adult day €56 to €72 depending on the date: €72 from 25 December to 4 January and from 6 February to 1 March, and €56 only from 12 to 18 April. The resort says prices also vary with when and where you buy.

**Getting there:** Autocares Tocina buses leave [Granada's bus station](https://sierranevada.es/es/invierno/la-estacion/como-llegar/) at 8:00, 10:15 and 17:15 CET on weekdays, adding 15:00 at weekends and on holidays, on the winter timetable the site showed on 20 November (last updated in March 2026). Granada's railway station is 40 km (25 mi) away.

**The trade-off:** the opening date wasn't on the official pages when we checked, and what you pay depends on the day.

### Italy: the Dolomiti Superski pass

**Why here:** one pass for the Dolomites; the [season pass](https://www.dolomitisuperski.com/en/plan-and-book/skipassandprices) runs from 28 November 2026 to 2 May 2027.

**Price:** an adult day costs [€89 in high season](https://www.dolomitisuperski.com/dam/jcr:92f51c2d-ec03-4c05-9a48-8cf67db8e301/Dolomiti_Superski_Comunicato_Stampa_Inverno_2026_27_Novita_Prezzi.pdf) and six days €453; a season pass is €990 in presale.

**Rules in Italy:** skiers on the pistes must hold [third-party liability insurance](https://www.normattiva.it/uri-res/N2Ls?urn:nir:stato:decreto.legislativo:2021-02-28;40), and lift companies must offer a policy when you buy your pass. Val Gardena's tourist board says its [passes don't include it](https://www.valgardena.it/it/vacanze-invernali-dolomiti/skipass/), so check your home or travel policy first. Since 2025, skiers of every age must wear a helmet, with fines of €100 to €150.

**The trade-off:** €89 in high season is more than Hintertux or SkiWelt, and the price announcement doesn't say which dates count as high season.

### Austria: SkiWelt Wilder Kaiser–Brixental

**Why here:** a Tyrolean area that opens on 5 December 2026, with a simple two-price calendar.

**Price:** an adult day costs [€71.50 from 5 to 18 December and 13 to 29 March, and €79.50 from 19 December to 12 March](https://www.kitzbueheler-alpen.com/en/kam/wi/ski/prices.html); six days €372 or €413.50.

**The trade-off:** no January discount, since the main-season price runs from 19 December to 12 March, and the price list ends on 29 March (4 April in Ellmau), five weeks before Tignes, Val Thorens and Sölden's glacier close on 2 May.

## Scandinavia: a snow guarantee you can claim

**Why here:** the refund-backed [SkiStar snow guarantee](https://skistar.com/en/inspiration/snow-guarantee/) described above, from 18 December to 4 April, in Åre, Sälen, Trysil, Hemsedal and Vemdalen.

**When:** Trysil from 4 December to 18 April. Åre from 11 December, or earlier if snow allows, to 2 May; Björnen and Duved–Tegefjäll from 18 December.

**Lift hours:** until 7 February, most lifts in Åre run from 09:30 to 15:00 CET. Evening skiing runs from 25 December to 3 January (see New Year above).

**The trade-off:** short days. Until 7 February, most of Åre's lifts close at 15:00.

## When is the best time for powder in Japan?

Late December to February. The Japan Meteorological Agency's 1991–2020 averages for [Kutchan](https://www.data.jma.go.jp/obd/stats/etrn/view/nml_sfc_ym.php?prec_no=16&block_no=47433), the town by Niseko, show 253 cm (100 in) of snowfall in both December and January and 187 cm (74 in) in February, when the snow lies deepest, 181 cm (71 in) on average. In [Hakuba](https://www.data.jma.go.jp/obd/stats/etrn/view/nml_amd_ym.php?prec_no=48&block_no=0396), at 703 m, January brings 216 cm (85 in) and February 174 cm (about 69 in).

### Niseko United, Hokkaido

**When:** from 28 November 2026, with Grand Hirafu and Annupuri running to 5 May 2027.

**Price:** the [All Mountain day pass](https://www.niseko.ne.jp/en/lift) costs ¥8,800 (about €50) from 28 November to 11 December and 23 March to 4 April, ¥12,600 (about €72) from 12 to 23 December and 1 to 22 March, and ¥13,500 (about €77) at peak, 24 December to 28 February. Early-season passes aren't sold online.

**Getting there:** fly to New Chitose (Sapporo). Chuo Bus's [reservation-only bus](https://teikan.chuo-bus.co.jp/en/course/7768) to the Hirafu Welcome Center takes about 3½ hours; last season it cost ¥6,000 (about €34) one way.

**The trade-off:** the peak price covers the whole powder season, and the bus from the airport takes about 3½ hours.

### Hakuba Valley, Nagano

**Why here:** ten resorts on one [Hakuba Valley pass](https://www.hakubavalley.com/en/ticket_en): ¥11,100 (about €63) for an adult day in 2026-27. Tokyo is about 2 hours 50 minutes away at best, by Hokuriku Shinkansen and a limited express bus, according to [Hakuba Valley](https://www.hakubavalley.com/access/).

**The trade-off:** less snow than Niseko at the towns' weather stations (216 cm against 253 cm in January), and season dates differ by resort, so check the one you want.

## North America, Epic and Ikon in brief

Vail Resorts targeted Vail Mountain for 13 November and Whistler Blackcomb and Park City for 20 November, subject to conditions. The two big multi-resort season passes include European days: the Epic Pass gives five days in Sölden, and the Ikon Pass up to seven at Grandvalira Resorts (five on the Ikon Base Pass). They pay off mainly if you also ski in North America this winter.

**The trade-off:** for European skiers it's a long-haul trip, and the opening dates are targets, not promises.

## Getting there, first-timer tips, insurance and avalanches

**By train to the French Alps:** Eurostar's ski train runs from London St Pancras on Saturdays from [19 December 2026 to 20 February 2027](https://www.eurostar.com/uk-en/train/ski-train), with a change at Lille Europe. It stops at Chambéry, Albertville, Moûtiers, Aime-la-Plagne, Landry and Bourg-Saint-Maurice, and takes just under 8 hours to Bourg-Saint-Maurice. Trains back run on Sunday mornings or selected Saturdays, from 27 December to 28 February.

**Nearest airports:** Zurich, Geneva or Basel for Zermatt; Innsbruck for Sölden; La Seu d'Urgell, Toulouse or Barcelona for Andorra; Sofia for Bansko; New Chitose for Niseko. Distances and times are in each section above.

**First time on skis:** start where small children ski free (under 9 in Zermatt, under 8 in Tignes, under 6 in Saas-Fee, under 5 in Les 3 Vallées) or where a beginner day is cheap, like Baqueira's €26. Book lessons and rental before holiday weeks. Groups can find more ideas in our [group trip guide](/blog/best-group-trip-destinations-2026).

**Insurance:** the UK's GHIC and EHIC cards [don't cover ski or mountain rescue](https://www.nhs.uk/using-the-nhs/healthcare-abroad/apply-for-a-free-uk-global-health-insurance-card-ghic/), private treatment or the flight home, so buy travel insurance that names winter sports. In Italy, liability insurance is compulsory on the pistes. Some resorts sell daily cover with the pass, like Baqueira at €4 a day.

**Avalanches:** away from marked runs, check the regional bulletin, which uses the [European scale of five levels](https://www.avalanches.org/standards/avalanche-danger-scale/): 1 low, 2 moderate, 3 considerable, 4 high and 5 very high. Level 3 already means "dangerous avalanche conditions". The emergency number is [112](https://digital-strategy.ec.europa.eu/en/policies/112) across the EU and in Switzerland.

## Frequently asked questions

### When does the ski season start in 2026?

On the glaciers it already has: Hintertux never closes, and Sölden, Cervinia, Saas-Fee and Zermatt were scheduled to start between 25 September and 1 November. Tignes and Val Thorens open on 21 November; Baqueira-Beret, Niseko and Dolomiti Superski on 28 November; most other resorts in early December.

### Are there ski resorts with guaranteed snow over New Year?

Not natural snow. Glacier resorts like Zermatt, Saas-Fee, Sölden and Hintertux are the safest bets. SkiStar in Sweden and Norway refunds stays booked with it if too few slopes are open between 18 December and 4 April, and Les 3 Vallées gives credit for unused days if another European area has more connected runs open.

### What are the cheapest weeks for a ski holiday in 2027?

About 7–24 January and 8–26 March, going by Zermatt's and Sölden's price calendars, plus the first half of December 2026 if you accept less open terrain. Check your resort: SkiWelt and Niseko charge main-season or peak prices right through January.

### Which February 2027 weeks are busiest?

13–28 February, when French Zones A and C, then A and B, overlap with the Dutch holidays, and England's half-term falls on 15–19 February in areas such as Leeds and Ealing.

### Where is the cheapest skiing in Europe this winter?

Leaving aside Tignes's opening week (21–27 November), Sierra Nevada's ticket-office day starts lowest of the official prices we read, at €56 in mid-April, and tops out at €72. Baqueira-Beret's price list shows €70–74 with no seasonal bands, Tignes alone costs €60–70 after its opening week, and SkiWelt €71.50–79.50. Bansko hadn't published this winter's day prices; last winter it was €59.

### Which is the best week for a ski holiday in 2027?

A Saturday-to-Saturday week such as 16–23 January or 13–20 March misses every school holiday in the tables above and falls in Zermatt's and Sölden's cheaper bands. In Italy, remember the liability insurance, and a helmet for everyone.

### When do Spanish ski resorts open for 2026-27?

Baqueira-Beret on 28 November, the day Sierra Nevada's price calendar starts too. Grandvalira, in Andorra, on 4 December.
