# Layla AI Review 2026: Pricing, Features & the Best Free Alternatives

Layla is one of the most talked-about AI travel planners of 2026 — a 4.9-star rating, more than a million trips planned, and a slick interface that leans on video for destination inspiration. But the question we get asked most is simpler than any feature list: *is it actually free, or do you hit a paywall the moment you want the details?*

We compared Layla's free tier and its premium plan. This is the honest version — what Layla does genuinely well, where the paywall sits, what to check before you enter a card, and the best alternatives if you'd rather not pay to see your own itinerary.

We're not affiliated with Layla and we don't earn a commission from it. MonkeyTravel is one of the alternatives below, and we'll tell you plainly where Layla beats us.

## Quick Verdict

Layla is a well-built tool with one of the few live-pricing integrations in this category that actually works. If you travel often and you're flexible on dates, the $49/year premium can pay for itself in fare savings alone.

But the free tier is thinner than it looks. You get a trip overview and a total price — the actual **day-by-day breakdown is gated** behind a 3-day trial or the annual premium plan. If you just want a full itinerary to look at and share without paying, Layla isn't the fastest route to it.

## Layla AI Pricing (2026)

| Plan | Price | What you get |
|---|---|---|
| Free | $0 | Trip overview, total estimated price, itinerary generation and revision — but not the full day-by-day detail |
| Premium | ~$49/year (some plans list ~$9.99/mo) | Full day-by-day itineraries, live pricing from Skyscanner and Booking.com, PriceLock drop alerts, PDF export, priority support |
| Trial | 3 days | Temporary access to premium features |

*Pricing and features reflect Layla's publicly listed information as of July 2026 and can change — check [layla.ai](https://layla.ai) for the latest.*

The human-expert finalization step is included either way, which is a genuinely nice touch — you're not left entirely to the algorithm.

## Where Layla Wins

**Live pricing that works.** Layla's premium tier pulls real fares from Skyscanner and Booking.com, and its PriceLock feature alerts you when prices on a saved route drop. Most tools in this category *claim* live pricing; Layla is one of the few where it holds up. If you're price-sensitive and flexible, this is the reason to consider premium.

**Inspiration-first feel.** The video-led destination discovery gives Layla a different texture from the text-heavy competition. For the dreaming phase of a trip, it's genuinely pleasant to use.

**Responsive product.** The founder reportedly handles support personally, and it shows — the tool is polished and the team iterates fast.

## Where Layla Falls Short

**The paywall is on the part you actually want.** A trip overview and a total price aren't a plan. The day-by-day schedule — the thing you open the tool for — needs the trial or the $49/year premium. For a category where "free AI trip planner" is the single most-searched phrase, that's a meaningful gap.

**It auto-renews.** Like most subscription trials, premium renews automatically when the trial ends. If you're only testing it, set a calendar reminder to cancel before renewal so you aren't charged for a plan you didn't mean to keep.

**English-only.** If you plan in Spanish, Italian, or Portuguese, Layla isn't built for you.

### 1. MonkeyTravel — the free, no-paywall alternative

Where Layla gates the day-by-day, [MonkeyTravel](/free-ai-trip-planner) gives you the whole thing free — real venue names, estimated prices, three genuinely different budget tiers, and smart day routing — without creating an account. You can go from destination to a shareable plan in about 30 seconds.

Two things Layla doesn't do at all: MonkeyTravel plans in **English, Spanish, Italian, and Portuguese**, and it has a built-in **group voting** system for when your travel companions can't agree on what to do. If you're planning with friends or planning in a language other than English, it's not a close call.

What Layla still wins on: live flight and hotel pricing. MonkeyTravel focuses on the itinerary, not booking — you'll open a separate tab to book. If catching a fare drop is the whole point of your search, Layla premium is the better fit.

### 2. Mindtrip — if you want to book in the same place

Mindtrip is free with no paywall and folds booking (Priceline, Viator) into the same interface, on top of a large points-of-interest database and strong maps. It's English-only, and some reviewers note its hotel filters can be inconsistent, but for research-plus-booking without tab-switching, it's the most complete free option. ### 3. Wonderplan — for quick budget trips

Wonderplan is free and budget-first, with clean PDF export. Reviewers often describe it as closer to a template generator than a deeply personalized planner, but for a single-destination budget trip you want fast, it does the job.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is Layla AI free?

Partly. Layla's free tier generates and revises itineraries and shows a trip overview with a total price, but the full day-by-day detail is gated behind a 3-day trial or the ~$49/year premium plan. Tools like MonkeyTravel, Mindtrip, and Wonderplan give you the full itinerary free.

### How much does Layla AI cost?

The premium plan is about $49/year (some plans list roughly $9.99/month). It unlocks full day-by-day itineraries, live pricing from Skyscanner and Booking.com, PriceLock drop alerts, and PDF export.

### What's the best free alternative to Layla?

For a full day-by-day itinerary with no paywall, [MonkeyTravel](/free-ai-trip-planner) is the closest free alternative — and it adds group voting plus Spanish, Italian, and Portuguese support that Layla doesn't have. Mindtrip is the best free option if you also want in-app booking.

### Is Layla or Mindtrip better?

Layla is stronger on live pricing and fare tracking; Mindtrip is stronger on breadth (11M+ points of interest) and in-app booking, and it's fully free. If you want a plan without paying, Mindtrip or MonkeyTravel; if you want fare drop alerts, Layla premium.

### Does Layla AI auto-renew after the free trial?

Layla's premium plan auto-renews after the trial ends, as most subscription trials do. If you're only testing premium, set a reminder to cancel before it renews so you aren't charged automatically.
