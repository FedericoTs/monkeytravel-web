# Golden Week 2027 in Japan, China and Korea: Dates, and When to Go or Avoid

Golden Week 2027 runs from Saturday 1 to Wednesday 5 May in both Japan and China, and South Korea is off on every one of those days except Tuesday 4 May. Japan adds a stand-alone holiday on Thursday 29 April, and China works Saturday 8 May to pay for its five days off. Those are the days to avoid for trains and hotels; the week after, from Thursday 6 May, is the easy alternative, and it's the day seat charges on Japan's main shinkansen line drop to off-peak.

Every holiday date here was read on 21 November 2026 on a government's own page: Japan's Cabinet Office, China's State Council (which published its 2027 schedule on 5 November), Korea's official 2027 almanac, and the Hong Kong and Taiwan holiday lists. Rail rules and prices come from the railways' own pages, read the same day. Times are local: Japan and Korea are on UTC+9, China, Hong Kong and Taiwan on UTC+8.

## What are the Golden Week 2027 dates?

Golden Week is Japan's name for its run of national holidays from late April into early May. Hong Kong's government uses the same words for the mainland's May Day break, and travellers use them loosely for Korea's early-May holidays too. In 2027 they fall like this:

- **Japan:** Thursday 29 April (Shōwa Day), Monday 3 May (Constitution Day), Tuesday 4 May (Greenery Day) and Wednesday 5 May (Children's Day), according to the [Cabinet Office](https://www8.cao.go.jp/chosei/shukujitsu/gaiyou.html). Friday 30 April is an ordinary working day.
- **China:** five days off from Saturday 1 to Wednesday 5 May, with Saturday 8 May worked in exchange ([State Council](https://www.gov.cn/zhengce/zhengceku/202611/content_7085102.htm)).
- **South Korea:** Labour Day on Saturday 1 May, Monday 3 May off in its place, Children's Day on Wednesday 5 May and Buddha's Birthday on Thursday 13 May ([2027 almanac](https://www.korea.kr/briefing/pressReleaseView.do?newsId=156768474)).
- **Hong Kong:** Labour Day on Saturday 1 May and the Birthday of the Buddha on Thursday 13 May, with no weekday off for the Saturday ([GovHK](https://www.gov.hk/en/about/abouthk/holiday/2027.htm)).
- **Taiwan:** Labour Day on Saturday 1 May, with government offices closed on Friday 30 April instead ([DGPA](https://www.dgpa.gov.tw/information?uid=82&pid=12983)).

The simplest plan is to mark 27 April to 6 May as the expensive stretch and decide which side of it you want to be on. If your dates are still open, [lay out your days](/trips/new) in the planner, move them around by chatting, and share the plan with whoever is coming. It doesn't book trains or rooms; it helps you see the trip day by day before you pay for anything.

## How does Golden Week 2027 fall in Japan?

Japan's four spring holidays have fixed dates. When one lands on a Sunday, the next day that isn't already a holiday becomes one: that is why Wednesday 6 May was a day off in 2026, when Constitution Day fell on a Sunday ([Cabinet Office](https://www8.cao.go.jp/chosei/shukujitsu/gaiyou.html)). In 2027 none of the four falls on a Sunday, so there is no extra day.

That gives Japanese workers three likely shapes:

- **Five days,** Saturday 1 to Wednesday 5 May, for anyone who works on Friday 30 April.
- **Seven days,** 29 April to 5 May, for anyone who takes 30 April off.
- **Nine days,** 1 to 9 May, for anyone who takes Thursday 6 and Friday 7 May off.

The shape copies 2026, which also had a midweek holiday on 29 April and then Saturday to Wednesday off (2 to 6 May). That makes the 2026 rail figures the best guide to 2027.

### Which days fill the shinkansen?

JR Central and JR West publish their Golden Week bookings about three weeks ahead: on 10 April in both 2025 and 2026, so expect the 2027 figures around 10 April 2027. In 2026, [JR Central](https://jr-central.co.jp/news/release/_pdf/000045502.pdf) and [JR West](https://www.westjr.co.jp/press/article/2026/04/10/items/260410_00_press_gw_shiteisekiyoyaku.pdf) named the same peaks: Saturday 2 May leaving the big cities and Tuesday 5 May coming back.

On 9 April 2026 the trains already filling up were the morning Nozomi and Hikari leaving Tokyo on 2 May. JR Central said most return trains still had seats, and JR West said that apart from a few busy times and trains there was still plenty of room. JR West's bookings were 14% up on 2025 at that point, so the holiday is growing, not fading. JR Central also promised updated day-by-day booking tables on 14, 16, 21 and 23 April, the easiest way to see which trains are filling.

Carried over to 2027, the pressure points are Saturday 1 May for leaving and Tuesday 4 to Wednesday 5 May for returning. That is our reading, not a JR forecast. In 2025 the [return peak](https://jr-central.co.jp/news/release/_pdf/000044220.pdf) was the last day off, Tuesday 6 May; in 2026 it was the day before the last.

JR's calendar of seasonal seat charges for April 2027 onwards is already out, and it shows where JR expects demand ([JR Central](https://railway.jr-central.co.jp/ticket-rule/rule30.html)). The charge is the supplement for a reserved seat, on top of the basic fare:

- **Tokaido, Sanyo and Kyushu shinkansen:** 28 April to 5 May are "busiest season" days, when a reserved seat costs ¥400 more than in regular season. Thursday 6 May and Monday 10 and Tuesday 11 May are off-peak days, ¥200 less than regular.
- **JR East lines,** including the Tohoku, Joetsu and Hokuriku shinkansen: the busiest season runs from 27 April to 6 May, and 10 May is off-peak.

So a reserved seat from Tokyo to Kyoto costs ¥600 less on Thursday 6 May than on Wednesday 5 May, with the basic fare unchanged. These charges were listed on 21 November 2026.

Three more rules matter:

- **Nozomi has been reserved-only.** JR Central and JR West ran every Nozomi with no non-reserved cars over Golden Week in 2024, 2025 ([2025 release](https://jr-central.co.jp/news/release/_pdf/000044220.pdf)) and 2026, when the rule applied from 24 April to 6 May ([JR Central and JR West](https://jr-central.co.jp/news/release/_pdf/000044849.pdf)). Hikari and Kodama keep their non-reserved cars. The 2027 period isn't announced yet; the 2026 one came out on 7 January 2026.
- **When seats go on sale:** at 10:00 Japan time on the same date one month before travel ([JR Central](https://railway.jr-central.co.jp/ticket-rule/rule02.html)). For a train on 1 May 2027 that is Thursday 1 April; for 5 May, Monday 5 April. JR Central's smartEX (free) and Express Yoyaku (¥1,100 a year) services sell some seats up to a year ahead.
- **Families:** in 2026, 70 Nozomi between Tokyo and Shin-Osaka carried a car for passengers with young children from 1 to 6 May, and JR Central said in April that it was already in demand ([JR Central](https://jr-central.co.jp/news/release/_pdf/000045502.pdf)).

Japanese travellers are spreading out. In JTB's survey for Golden Week 2026, Saturday 2 May was the most common departure day for domestic trips (19.6% of domestic travellers surveyed), but 14.5% planned to leave on 24 April or earlier, and more people planned to travel only in the weeks before or after the holidays than only during them ([JTB](https://www.jtbcorp.jp/jp/newsroom/2026/04/02_jtb_2026gw-travel-trend-outlook.html), a travel company's survey). The edges of the holiday fill up too.

### Kyoto, the sights and the hotels

Kyoto puts the holiday in proportion. Over Golden Week 2026 (2 to 6 May) the city counted 316,043 people getting on and off its buses each day, and 598,704 on the subway. On the cherry-blossom weekends a month earlier the counts were higher: 345,568 and 668,589 ([Kyoto City](https://www.city.kyoto.lg.jp/sankan/cmsfiles/contents/0000358/358453/R08harunotorikumikekka.pdf)). The city said the roads were not very congested over the holiday, except on 4 May. In 2026 it added buses so that, with the regular routes, one left Kyoto Station for Kiyomizu-dera and Gion every 3 to 4 minutes at peak, plus a sightseeing express bus to Kiyomizu-dera, Gion, Heian Shrine and Ginkakuji every 7 to 8 minutes in the late morning ([Kyoto City Bus](https://www.city.kyoto.lg.jp/kotsu/cmsfiles/contents/0000352/352528/houdou.pdf)). For Golden Week 2026, Kyoto's official tourism site also published hour-by-hour crowd forecasts and live cameras ([Kyoto City](https://www.city.kyoto.lg.jp/sankan/cmsfiles/contents/0000353/353270/houdou.pdf)).

Hotel prices tell the same story. The Kyoto City Tourism Association's sample of main hotels averaged ¥31,296 a room a night in April 2026, with 85.1% of rooms sold, and ¥24,231 in May with 87.0% sold ([April](https://www.kyokanko.or.jp/report/hotel202604), [May](https://www.kyokanko.or.jp/report/hotel202605)). Those rates leave out consumption tax and Kyoto's lodging tax, which rose on 1 March 2026. It is charged per person per night, banded by the room rate per guest before meals: ¥200 below ¥6,000, ¥400 from ¥6,000, ¥1,000 from ¥20,000, ¥4,000 from ¥50,000 and ¥10,000 from ¥100,000 ([Kyoto City](https://www.city.kyoto.lg.jp/gyozai/page/0000236942.html)).

Japan's tourist board calls Golden Week one of the country's busiest travel periods, with packed trains and hotels, tells anyone who must travel in the first week of May to book early, and points to the calmer second half of the month ([JNTO](https://www.japan.travel/en/guide/may/)). For a first visit, our [guide to what you need to know](/blog/first-trip-to-japan-what-you-need-to-know) covers the basics.

### What stays quieter in Japan

- **Trains away from the two peaks.** On 9 April 2026, JR Central reported seats on all but a few trains outside the morning of 2 May, in both directions.
- **The non-reserved cars on Hikari and Kodama,** for short hops when you can't get a seat on a Nozomi.
- **The days after.** Thursday 6 May is an off-peak day for seat charges on the Tokaido line, and the second half of May is calmer, by the tourist board's own account.
- **Kyoto, relatively.** Its buses and subway were busier on the cherry-blossom weekends than over Golden Week.

**The trade-off:** the sights stay open over Golden Week, but you pay the busiest-season charge on reserved seats from 27 or 28 April to 5 or 6 May, compete with Japanese families for rooms, and lose the freedom to change trains on the day.

## When is China's Labour Day holiday in 2027?

The State Council General Office published the 2027 schedule on 5 November 2026. Labour Day runs from Saturday 1 to Wednesday 5 May, five days, and Saturday 8 May becomes a working day to make up for it ([State Council](https://www.gov.cn/zhengce/zhengceku/202611/content_7085102.htm)). In 2026 the break ran from Friday 1 to Tuesday 5 May, with Saturday 9 May worked ([Xinhua](https://www.news.cn/politics/20251104/88bcffd88ae249e58c699b8772548e3d/c.html)). The 2027 notice also encourages employers and staff to add paid leave to the holidays so that people travel at different times.

Here is what five days off did in 2026:

- **Trains:** the railways' holiday rush ran from 29 April to 6 May and carried 159 million passenger trips. Friday 1 May set a single-day record of 24.84 million, and 5 May had the most trains ever scheduled in a day, 14,576 ([Xinhua on gov.cn](https://www.gov.cn/yaowen/liebiao/202605/content_7067997.htm)). Counted the same way, two days before to one day after the break, the 2027 rush would again run from 29 April to 6 May (our count, not a railway forecast).
- **Flights:** airlines carried 10.54 million passengers over the five days, 2.11 million a day, according to Civil Aviation Administration figures reported by [China Daily](https://cn.chinadaily.com.cn/a/202605/06/WS69fae952a310942cc49aace0.html).
- **Sights:** the Ministry of Culture and Tourism counted 325 million domestic trips and 185.5 billion yuan of spending over the five days, about 571 yuan a trip by our arithmetic. It named the Palace Museum, Shanghai Disneyland, Nanjing's Fuzimiao and Hangzhou's West Lake among the most popular places ([Ministry of Culture and Tourism](https://www.mct.gov.cn/whzx/whyw/202605/t20260506_965708.htm)).
- **Borders:** 11.28 million crossings in and out of China, or 2.26 million a day, peaking on Saturday 2 May at 2.53 million. Foreigners made 1.26 million of them, 12.5% more than in 2025 ([National Immigration Administration](https://www.nia.gov.cn/n741440/n741567/c1783234/content.html)).

**Booking:** train tickets go on sale 15 days ahead, counting the day of travel ([China Railway 12306](https://kyfw.12306.cn/mormhweb/zxdt/202205/t20220531_37508.html)). That means 17 April 2027 for a train on 1 May and 21 April for one on 5 May, so you can't lock in a holiday train early. The Palace Museum in Beijing sells no same-day tickets, caps visits at 40,000 a day and asks foreign visitors for a passport number when booking online ([Palace Museum](https://intl.dpm.org.cn/visit.html?l=en)). It closes on Mondays except on public holidays, so check its notice for Monday 3 May, a day off in China that year. A ticket costs 60 yuan from April to October ([Palace Museum](https://www.dpm.org.cn/Visit.html)).

**China's other golden week** is National Day: Friday 1 to Thursday 7 October 2027, with Sunday 26 September and Saturday 9 October worked. In 2027 the Mid-Autumn Festival is a separate single day off, Wednesday 15 September. For the Lunar New Year travel rush, see our [Lunar New Year guide](/blog/lunar-new-year-where-to-celebrate).

**The trade-off:** the holiday mood is real and the sights are open, but you compete with hundreds of millions of domestic trips for trains you can only buy 15 days ahead. Arrive from Friday 7 May and you get much the same weather with the country back at work, including on Saturday 8 May.

## What are South Korea's holidays in May 2027?

Korea made Labour Day a public holiday in 2026, and because it falls on a Saturday in 2027, the following Monday is given in its place ([2027 almanac](https://www.korea.kr/briefing/pressReleaseView.do?newsId=156768474)). One newspaper, [Segye Ilbo](https://www.segye.com/newsView/20260629509331), reported in June that there would be no substitute day, but the government's almanac and the calendar data of the [Korea Astronomy and Space Science Institute](https://astro.kasi.re.kr/life/post/calendarData?year=2027) both list one. They give:

- **Saturday 1 May:** Labour Day, with **Monday 3 May** off in its place, so 1 to 3 May is a three-day weekend.
- **Tuesday 4 May:** a working day, and the obvious one to take off.
- **Wednesday 5 May:** Children's Day.
- **Thursday 13 May:** Buddha's Birthday, the eighth day of the fourth lunar month. With Friday 14 May taken off, 13 to 16 May becomes four days.

Memorial Day, 6 June, falls on a Sunday in 2027 and gets no substitute.

**Booking:** since 1 October 2026, KTX and other rail tickets can be booked two months before departure instead of one ([Ministry of Land, Infrastructure and Transport](https://www.molit.go.kr/USR/NEWS/m_71/dtl.jsp?id=95092440)). For the May long weekend that means early March. The ministry is also adding French, German and Mongolian to its rail web pages for foreigners and working on WeChat Pay and Alipay payments.

**Weather:** Seoul averages highs of 17.9°C (64°F) in April and 23.6°C (74°F) in May, with about seven days of rain in each month (Korea Meteorological Administration, 1991–2020).

**The trade-off:** the holidays split into a long weekend and a midweek day, so 1 to 5 May stays busy, even though many people only take the long weekend, and Buddha's Birthday brings a second long weekend eight days later.

## Is there a Golden Week in Hong Kong and Taiwan?

### Hong Kong: the mainland's Golden Week arrives

Hong Kong's own holidays barely touch the week: Labour Day is Saturday 1 May, with no weekday off, and the Birthday of the Buddha is Thursday 13 May ([GovHK](https://www.gov.hk/en/about/abouthk/holiday/2027.htm), [Labour Department](https://www.labour.gov.hk/eng/news/latest_holidays2027.htm)). What fills Hong Kong is the mainland's break.

For 1 to 5 May 2026 the government expected about 6 million crossings through its sea, land and air control points, with land crossings highest on Saturday 2 and Sunday 3 May, about 636,000 and 688,000 ([HKSAR Government](https://www.info.gov.hk/gia/general/202604/27/P2026042700506.htm)). In the event 1.19 million visitors arrived, 1.01 million of them from the mainland, with mainland arrivals peaking on 2 May at about 260,000. Hotels were 90% full, and the government said hotel prices were 10% higher than in past long holidays ([HKSAR Government](https://www.info.gov.hk/gia/general/202605/06/P2026050600707.htm)). Visitor numbers were high at the West Kowloon Cultural District, Ocean Park, Hong Kong Disneyland, the Peak Tram and Ngong Ping 360. The government's advice is to avoid the busy periods and check land-crossing waiting times on its Easy Boundary platform before setting off.

With the mainland off from 1 to 5 May 2027, expect the same pattern, with the crush on the first weekend, 1 and 2 May (our reading). Hong Kong's own long spring break comes earlier: Good Friday on 26 March to Easter Monday on 29 March, then Ching Ming on Monday 5 April.

**The trade-off:** a Hong Kong trip that overlaps 1 to 5 May costs more for the same room, and the cross-border trains and big attractions are crowded. A week later the mainland crowds have gone home.

### Taiwan: a long weekend, not a golden week

Taiwan's Labour Day is Saturday 1 May 2027. Government offices take Friday 30 April off instead, giving them 30 April to 2 May, while private employers follow the Ministry of Labor's rules ([DGPA](https://www.dgpa.gov.tw/information?uid=82&pid=12983)). Taiwan's big spring break comes earlier: Children's Day and Tomb Sweeping Day give four days, 3 to 6 April 2027.

**The trade-off:** Taiwan itself stays calm, but if you fly on from Taipei to Japan or Korea in early May, you land in their holidays.

### The week before: 19 to 27 April

**Why then:** an ordinary working week everywhere. JR's 2027 calendar marks 19 to 22 and 26 to 27 April as off-peak on the Tokaido line, with seat charges ¥200 below regular, and Kyoto averages 21.9°C (71°F) by day in late April. China doesn't start moving until the end of the month, and Korea is at work. Our [April guide](/blog/where-to-go-in-april) has more ideas for the month.

**What to book:** long-distance trains for dates before 27 April, when JR East's busiest-season charges begin, and rooms early if your stay runs into the holiday. If you want late blossom in the north, our [cherry blossom guide](/blog/japan-cherry-blossom-season-guide) has the regional timing.

**The trade-off:** you start in the shoulder but can end in the holiday. A trip that runs past 27 April pays the busiest-season charges, some Japanese travellers set off a week early, and April evenings are cool: Seoul and Kyoto average lows of 8 to 11°C. Kyoto's hotels also averaged more across April 2026 than across May, though April includes the cherry blossom.

### The week after: 6 to 14 May

**Why then:** the region goes back to work. Thursday 6 May and Monday 10 and Tuesday 11 May are off-peak days for seat charges on the Tokaido Shinkansen, and early May averages 24.1°C (75°F) in Kyoto. China works on Saturday 8 May, so the only rest day that week is Sunday 9 May. In Korea, 6 to 12 May sits between the two long weekends. Kyoto's Aoi Matsuri procession leaves the Imperial Palace at 10:30 on Saturday 15 May, or 16 May if it rains ([Kyoto City Tourism Association](https://kyoto.travel/en/travel-inspiration/aoi-matsuri-festival/)), and our [May guide](/blog/where-to-go-in-may) covers the rest of the month.

**What to book:** Kyoto rooms for 15 May if you want the procession, and KTX seats for 13 to 16 May if you'll be in Korea for Buddha's Birthday.

**The trade-off:** rain builds through May. Kyoto's normal for 11 to 20 May is 62 mm, against 35 to 42 mm in each of the three ten-day periods before; Hong Kong gets 291 mm in May against 153 mm in April. Japan's rainy season normally starts around 6 June in Kansai and 7 June in the Tokyo region ([JMA](https://www.data.jma.go.jp/cpd/baiu/sokuhou_baiu.html)), so late May is the last stretch before it.

### Go during Golden Week anyway

**Why then:** the dates are the only ones you have, or you want to see the holiday itself.

**How to make it work:** in Japan, buy seats at 10:00 Japan time a month ahead, travel against the flow (into Tokyo on 1 May, out of it on 4 or 5 May), sleep in one city over the middle days and see the big Kyoto sights early in the morning. In China, arrive before 29 April and stay put until 6 May. In Hong Kong, keep 1 and 2 May for neighbourhoods rather than the Peak or the theme parks.

**The trade-off:** reserved seats on most JR lines carry the busiest-season charge, Hong Kong's rooms cost more, and a missed connection is hard to fix when the next trains are full. "Travel against the flow" is our advice, based on JR's 2026 peaks, not a JR forecast.

## What to book first, and by when

1. **Now:** rooms in Kyoto, Tokyo and Hong Kong for any night from 28 April to 6 May, and flights into the region. Hong Kong's hotels were 90% full over the 2026 break.
2. **Early March 2027:** KTX seats for 1 to 5 May, two months ahead.
3. **1 April 2027, 10:00 Japan time:** shinkansen seats for 1 May, and so on day by day until 5 April for 5 May. smartEX and Express Yoyaku take some Tokaido bookings up to a year ahead, so check there first.
4. **Around 10 April 2027:** JR's Golden Week booking figures. Check which trains are filling and move yours if you can.
5. **17 April 2027:** Chinese train tickets for 1 May, and 21 April for 5 May.
6. **As soon as your date opens online:** the Palace Museum, which sells nothing on the day.

## Frequently asked questions

### When is Golden Week 2027?

In Japan, Thursday 29 April and then Saturday 1 to Wednesday 5 May. In China, 1 to 5 May, with Saturday 8 May worked. In Korea, 1 to 3 May and Wednesday 5 May. So 1 to 5 May is the week they share, apart from Korea's working Tuesday.

### Is Golden Week a bad time to visit Japan?

For trains and rooms, yes: the tourist board calls it one of Japan's busiest travel periods, and JR's reserved-seat charges are at their highest. Temples, museums and shops stay open, though, and Kyoto's transport was busier on the cherry-blossom weekends. If your dates are flexible, arrive from Thursday 6 May.

### When is China's Golden Week in 2027?

There are two. The Labour Day break runs from 1 to 5 May, with Saturday 8 May worked. National Day runs from 1 to 7 October, with Sunday 26 September and Saturday 9 October worked.

### Does Korea have a Golden Week?

Not by name, but 2027 comes close: Labour Day on Saturday 1 May with Monday 3 May off in lieu, Children's Day on Wednesday 5 May, and only Tuesday 4 May to take off for five days in a row. Buddha's Birthday follows on Thursday 13 May.

### Is Golden Week a good time to visit Hong Kong?

Not if you can avoid 1 to 5 May. Hong Kong has no weekday holiday of its own that week, but over the mainland's 2026 break mainland arrivals peaked at about 260,000 on 2 May, hotels were 90% full and prices were 10% above past long holidays. In 2027 the mainland's break ends on Wednesday 5 May.

### When do shinkansen tickets for Golden Week go on sale?

At 10:00 Japan time on the same date one month before travel: 1 April 2027 for 1 May. JR Central's smartEX and Express Yoyaku sell some Tokaido seats up to a year ahead.

## Plan your trip around Golden Week

Decide which side of 1 to 5 May you want to be on, then build the trip around it. You can [lay out your days](/trips/new) in the planner, change them by chatting, share them with your group and keep them on your phone during the trip; the trains and rooms are yours to book. If Japan is the plan, our [Golden Route itinerary](/blog/japan-golden-route-itinerary) links Tokyo, Kyoto and Osaka, and the [2027 travel calendar](/blog/2027-travel-calendar) shows the year's other crowded weeks.
