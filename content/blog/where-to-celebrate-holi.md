# Holi 2027: The Date and the Best Places to Celebrate in India and Nepal

Holi 2027 falls on Monday 22 March, with the Holika Dahan bonfires on the evening of Sunday 21 March, according to the [Drik Panchang](https://www.drikpanchang.com/festivals/holi/festivals-holika-dahan-timings.html?year=2027) calendar for New Delhi. The official holiday comes a day later: the Government of India closes its Delhi offices for Holi on Tuesday 23 March, and Uttar Pradesh, the state that holds Mathura and Vrindavan, closes on both the 22nd and the 23rd. Kathmandu plays a day early, on Sunday 21 March. Plan to be in place from the 21st to the 23rd.

This guide is for anyone timing a trip around Holi, from abroad or from elsewhere in India: what happens where and on which day, how to get there, what's announced and what isn't, and the catch. Dates and rules were read on official pages on 21 November 2026; anything from the press or an independent guide is marked, and prices are what each source showed that day, mostly for 2026. Times are Indian Standard Time (IST, UTC+5:30); Nepal is 15 minutes ahead.

| Place | 2027 date(s) | What's special | Crowds | Best for |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Barsana and Nandgaon (Braj) | Lathmar Holi not announced (25 and 26 Feb in 2026); UP Tourism's Braj programme runs 16–30 Mar | Women swing bamboo sticks at men from the next village | Very heavy | Photographers, devotees |
| Vrindavan and Mathura | Banke Bihari 18–21 Mar (temple's calendar); main day 22 or 23 Mar | Temple Holi with gulal and flower petals | Very heavy, crush risk inside temples | Holi as a religious festival |
| Bharatpur | 17–18 Mar | Rajasthan Tourism's Braj Holi Festival | Moderate | Families |
| Jaipur | 22 Mar | Rajasthan Tourism's Dhulandi Festival, herbal colours only | Managed at the event | First-timers, families |

## When is Holi 2027?

Holi has two parts: Holika Dahan, the bonfire on the full-moon night of the month of Phalguna, and Rangwali Holi or Dhulandi, the colours the next morning. In 2027 the calendars disagree by a day.

| Calendar | Holika Dahan (bonfire) | Holi (colours) |
|---|---|---|
| Panchang: Drik Panchang, New Delhi | Sunday 21 March, 18:33–20:55 IST | Monday 22 March |
| Government of India, central offices in Delhi | Monday 22 March (optional holiday, "Holika Dahan, Dolyatra") | Tuesday 23 March (closed) |
| Uttar Pradesh government | Monday 22 March (public holiday) | Tuesday 23 March (public holiday), plus Wednesday 24 March as an optional holiday |
| Rajasthan Tourism, Jaipur event | Not stated | Monday 22 March |
| Nepal, Kathmandu and the hill districts | Not stated | Sunday 21 March (public holiday) |
| Nepal, Terai districts | Not stated | Monday 22 March (public holiday) |

Sources: [Drik Panchang](https://www.drikpanchang.com/festivals/holi/festivals-holika-dahan-timings.html?year=2027); the Department of Personnel and Training's [2027 holiday order](https://master-dopt.digifootprint.gov.in/static/uploads/2026/08/2ffd159748162a48c025f7e6ca5807d5.pdf) of 16 July 2026; Uttar Pradesh's [notification of 17 November 2026](https://staticimg.amarujala.com/assets/applications/2026/11/17/list-of-holidays-in-year-2027_6afc500c823fe.pdf), scanned by the newspaper Amar Ujala; Rajasthan Tourism's [Dhulandi Festival listing](https://www.incredibleindia.gov.in/en/festivals-and-events/rajasthan/dhulandi-festival); and Nepal's Home Ministry notice in the Nepal Gazette, on the [ministry's site](https://moha.gov.np/page/government-and-public-holidays-in-2083) (in Nepali; English summary by the law firm [Pradhan & Associates](https://pradhanlaw.com/publications/list-of-public-holidays-2083-202627)).

Why they differ: the full moon begins at 18:21 IST on 21 March and ends at 16:13 IST on 22 March, by Drik Panchang's New Delhi reckoning, so the full-moon evening is the 21st but the full-moon day can be counted as the 22nd, which is how Uttar Pradesh's list labels it, with the 23rd as the first day after the full moon. Outside Delhi, Holi is one of 12 optional festivals on the central list, from which each state's coordination committee picks three, so not every central office closes.

Dates split by place in 2026 too. Nepal's hill districts played on 2 March and the Terai on 3 March (reported by the [Kathmandu Post](https://kathmandupost.com/visual-stories/2026/02/24/phagu-purnima-celebrations-begin-with-chir-installation)), Udaipur's City Palace lit its bonfire on 2 March ([Eternal Mewar](https://eternalmewar.in/media/newsletter/templates/2026/nl250/vol250_27.html)), most Mumbai parties were on 3 March (reported by the [Free Press Journal](https://www.freepressjournal.in/lifestyle/biggest-2026-holi-parties-to-attend-in-mumbai-starting-at-499-only)), and central offices closed on 4 March (as in the [2026 list for central offices in Uttarakhand](https://dfe.gov.in/uploads/documents/list-of-gazetted-holidays-2026.pdf)).

**What it means for you:** arrive by the afternoon of Sunday 21 March, keep Monday 22 and Tuesday 23 March free, and ask your hotel which morning the colours start in that town. Government offices in Delhi close on the 23rd, and Uttar Pradesh's on the 22nd and 23rd.

**What it marks:** the bonfire recalls the legend of Prahlad, a boy devoted to Vishnu, whose father, King Hiranyakashipu, had his sister Holika take him into a fire. Holika wore a shawl that protected her from flames, but it passed to Prahlad: she burned and he survived ([Drik Panchang](https://www.drikpanchang.com/festivals/holi/festivals-holi-legends.html?lang=en)). It stands for good winning over evil. In Braj, the region around Mathura tied to Krishna's birth and childhood, Holi is also about Krishna and Radha, and the temples there play it as worship as well as fun.

Holi overlaps Holy Week in 2027, with Easter on 28 March; our guide to [where to go in March](/blog/where-to-go-in-march) covers the rest of that month. Once you've picked a place, [lay out your Holi week](/trips/new) in the planner, move the colour day if your town plays on the 23rd, and share the plan with whoever's coming. It plans the days; it doesn't sell tickets, trains or rooms.

## Holi in Braj 2027: Mathura, Vrindavan, Barsana and Nandgaon

Braj celebrates for about two weeks around the main day. Uttar Pradesh Tourism lists its Braj festival, Rangotsav 2027, for 16–30 March in Mathura and Vrindavan, taking in the Lathmar Holi of Barsana and Nandgaon, the flower Holi in Vrindavan, the widows' Holi and the main colour day ([Incredible India](https://www.incredibleindia.gov.in/en/festivals-and-events/uttar-pradesh/rangotsav-2027), the national tourism site). On 21 November 2026 we found no day-by-day programme from UP Tourism or the Mathura administration, and of the temples we checked, only Banke Bihari had posted 2027 dates (below).

The programme moves with the moon. Holi falls 18 days later than in 2026, when it was on 4 March, so don't plan around 2026 dates.

### Lathmar Holi in Barsana and Nandgaon

Not announced yet. In 2026 Barsana's Lathmar Holi was on Wednesday 25 February, the ninth day of the bright half of Phalguna, and Nandgaon's on Thursday 26 February, the tenth (reported by [PTI via ThePrint](https://theprint.in/india/lathmar-holi-preparations-in-full-swing-in-barsana-authorities-review-arrangements/2853309)). That was a week before the colour day. That year's Rangotsav opened in Barsana on Tuesday 24 February (reported by [PTI](https://theprint.in/india/rangotsav-2026-begins-with-grand-holi-celebrations-in-ups-barsana/2863124)).

**What it is:** two days. On the first, men from Nandgaon, Krishna's village, come to the Radha Rani temple in Barsana, Radha's village, and the women of Barsana swing bamboo sticks (lathis) at them while they shield themselves. On the second, the play moves to Nandgaon ([Incredible India](https://www.incredibleindia.gov.in/en/uttar-pradesh/mathura/barsana)). Before it comes the Laddumar Holi, when priests from Nandgaon bring an invitation and the temple hands out sweets, which has grown into an event of its own (PTI).

**Crowds and safety:** the district magistrate's 2026 checks covered barricading and crowd movement around the temple (PTI). Security staff disguised as revellers and ordinary devotees were to mix with the crowds to watch for trouble (reported by [PTI](https://theprint.in/india/rangotsav-2026-begins-with-grand-holi-celebrations-in-ups-barsana/2863124)).

**The trade-off:** the dates come out late, the lanes up to the temple are packed solid, and you're there to watch a ritual, not to join in: the sticks are for the men of Nandgaon.

### Vrindavan, Mathura, Gokul and Baldev

- **Vrindavan:** UP Tourism lists the Phoolon ki Holi, played with flower petals ([Incredible India](https://www.incredibleindia.gov.in/en/festivals-and-events/uttar-pradesh/rangotsav-2027)). At the Banke Bihari temple, colour play runs from the evening of Rangbharni (Aamla) Ekadashi to Holika Dahan, with priests sprinkling the devotees with tesu-flower water and dry colours; the temple's website lists [Thursday 18 March](https://bihariji.org/festivaldesc.php?f=MTg=) to [Sunday 21 March](https://bihariji.org/festivaldesc.php?f=MTk=) 2027. In 2026 the first day was Friday 27 February, when priests threw gulal over the devotees inside and extra police were on duty (reported by [DD India](https://ddindia.co.in/2026/02/devotees-celebrate-holi-utsav-at-banke-bihari-temple-rangbhari-ekadashi-marked-in-ayodhya/)).
- **Gokul:** the Chhadimar Holi, in which women strike the men playing Krishna with sticks, began with a procession at about 10:00 on Sunday 1 March 2026 (reported by [PTI](https://theprint.in/india/gokul-celebrates-chhadimar-holi-devotees-immerse-in-colours-across-mathura/2867262)).
- **Mathura:** we found no official 2026 or 2027 dates for Holi at the city's big temples, including Dwarkadhish and the Krishna Janmabhoomi. Ask at your hotel once the programme is out.
- **Baldev and Govardhan:** the day after Holi, the Huranga at the Dauji temple in Baldev, where women tear the men's clothes into whips, comes with the Charkula dance, a woman balancing 108 lit lamps on her head. In 2026 it was on Thursday 5 March, with Govardhan's on the Friday, and the colours included tesu and rose flowers, turmeric and sandalwood (reported by [PTI](https://theprint.in/india/age-old-huranga-tradition-celebrated-with-vibrant-colours-spiritual-devotion-in-mathura/2870548)).

**Getting there:** Mathura is 145 km (90 mi) south-east of Delhi and 58 km (36 mi) north-west of Agra, with two stations, Mathura Junction (MTJ) and Mathura Cantt (MRT), served by many trains between Delhi and Agra ([Mathura district](https://mathura.nic.in/how-to-reach/)). The nearest airport is Agra's Kheria, 46 km (29 mi) away, and Delhi's Indira Gandhi International is 136 km (85 mi). Noida International Airport at Jewar began domestic flights on 15 June 2026 (reported by [All India Radio](https://newsonair.gov.in/noida-international-airport-in-jewar-begins-commercial-flight-operations-this-morning/)). Barsana is 40 km (25 mi) from Mathura on [Incredible India's Barsana page](https://www.incredibleindia.gov.in/en/uttar-pradesh/mathura/barsana) and 42 km (26 mi) on [its Mathura page](https://www.incredibleindia.gov.in/en/uttar-pradesh/mathura), and Govardhan about 25 km (16 mi). We found no official distances for Vrindavan, Nandgaon or Baldev.

**Where to stay:** Mathura, on the main line, or Vrindavan, with Barsana and Nandgaon as day trips. Vrindavan and Barsana were declared holy sites in 2017, and no meat or alcohol is sold there (reported by [ThePrint](https://theprint.in/india/yogi-adityanath-imposes-ban-meat-liquor-sale-in-mathura-says-traders-can-sell-milk-instead/725154)). Since 2021 the same rule has covered 22 wards of the Mathura-Vrindavan municipal corporation (reported by [Scroll](https://scroll.in/latest/1005152/scroll_in)). We found no published occupancy figures for Holi week, so book as soon as the programme is out.

**Crowds and safety:** temples here can get dangerously full. Two women died of suffocation in the Janmashtami crowd at Banke Bihari in 2022, and in 2024 the temple management asked elderly people, children and anyone unwell to avoid peak hours. It also told devotees not to come in shoes or slippers, because the entry and exit gates are separate (reported by [PTI via ThePrint](https://theprint.in/india/mathura-banke-bihari-temple-management-issues-crowd-advisory-for-janmashtami/2235402/)).

**The trade-off:** this is the most intense Holi on this list, with most days run by temples rather than for visitors. The programme isn't fixed until close to the date, the crowds are the densest anywhere on this list, and you can't buy a drink in Vrindavan or Barsana.

## Holi in Rajasthan 2027: Jaipur, Udaipur, Pushkar and Bharatpur

We found no 2027 state holiday list for Rajasthan on 21 November 2026. The 2026 list came out on 16 October 2025 ([TeamLease RegTech](https://www.teamleaseregtech.com/updates/article/49338/government-of-rajasthan-declaration-of-public-and-optional-holidays-in/)).

### Jaipur

**What's on:** Rajasthan Tourism's Dhulandi Festival at Hotel Khasa Kothi on Monday 22 March 2027, with folk music and dance, and only organic, herbal colours ([Incredible India](https://www.incredibleindia.gov.in/en/festivals-and-events/rajasthan/dhulandi-festival)). Entry terms and times aren't published yet.

**Getting there:** Jaipur International Airport (JAI) and Jaipur Junction station (JP) ([Incredible India](https://www.incredibleindia.gov.in/en/rajasthan/jaipur)).

**The trade-off:** it's an organised event at a hotel, not the old city's street Holi. It's the easiest start for a first Holi, but you won't know the times or entry terms until Rajasthan Tourism publishes them.

### Udaipur

**What happens:** the City Palace's Holika Dahan. A band leads a procession from the royal residence to Manek Chowk, the palace's courtyard, priests perform the puja, and the fire is lit as twilight falls on the full-moon night ([Eternal Mewar](https://eternalmewar.in/microwebsites/holika-dahan), the palace's own foundation). In 2026 it was on 2 March, led by Lakshyaraj Singh Mewar, with Gair dancers from Bhilwara ([Eternal Mewar newsletter](https://eternalmewar.in/media/newsletter/templates/2026/nl250/vol250_27.html)).

**2027:** not announced. The full moon begins at 18:21 IST on Sunday 21 March, by Drik Panchang's New Delhi times. The palace's pages give no entry policy or ticket price, though visitors' comments on its site describe tourists joining in.

**Getting there:** Maharana Pratap Airport (UDR) and Udaipur City station (UDZ) ([Incredible India](https://www.incredibleindia.gov.in/en/rajasthan/udaipur)).

**The trade-off:** it's an evening ceremony, not a colour day, and neither the date nor the entry rules are out yet. Pair it with the colour morning in the old city.

### Pushkar

**What happens:** Holi in Pushkar is a street party in a small lake town with a large backpacker crowd. The Tribune reported in 2017 on a Holi staged for foreign visitors ([The Tribune](https://www.tribuneindia.com/news/archive/nation/puskhar-in-rajasthan-to-host-unique-holi-for-foreigners-on-march-13-374989)), and Deccan Herald described a shirt-tearing "kapda phaad" Holi for tourists at Varah Ghat ([Deccan Herald](https://www.deccanherald.com/india/foreigners-pushkar-rip-off-each-1995492), undated). We found no official programme and no recent report.

**Getting there:** Ajmer is 14 km (9 mi) from Pushkar and 130 km (81 mi) south-west of Jaipur; trains stop at Ajmer Junction (AII), and the nearest airports are Kishangarh (KOH), Jaipur and Delhi ([Incredible India](https://www.incredibleindia.gov.in/en/rajasthan/ajmer)).

**Where to stay:** in town if you want to walk back covered in colour. Pushkar is a pilgrimage town: restaurants are vegetarian and alcohol is banned, according to [Wikivoyage](https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Pushkar), which also notes the bhang lassi cafés by the lake.

**The trade-off:** no programme, no recent reporting and a reputation for rowdiness. Go with friends, not alone, and skip the bhang (see below).

### Bharatpur's Braj Holi Festival

Rajasthan Tourism holds a Braj Holi Festival in Bharatpur on Wednesday 17 and Thursday 18 March 2027, with processions, Raslila performances of Krishna's life, folk music and crafts ([Incredible India](https://www.incredibleindia.gov.in/en/festivals-and-events/rajasthan/braj-holi-festival)). The nearest airport is Agra, and Keoladeo National Park, a UNESCO World Heritage site known for its birds, is in Bharatpur.

**The trade-off:** it's a staged two-day festival, five days before the colour day, so it works as the start of a Rajasthan week rather than the main event.

## Is Basanta Utsav in Shantiniketan open in 2027?

Not announced. On 21 November 2026, Visva-Bharati's notices had nothing on Basanta Utsav for 2027. In 2026 the university held it, as Basanta Bandana, on Thursday 5 and Friday 6 March, the days after Holi, for its own students, teachers, staff, alumni and residents only: the entry gates were for them, and no colour or abir was allowed ([notice of 5 March 2026](https://www.visvabharati.ac.in/home/wp-content/uploads/2026/03/Entry-Gates-and-Parking-for-Vasanto-Bandana-2026.pdf); [programme, in Bengali](https://www.visvabharati.ac.in/home/wp-content/uploads/2026/03/BBP.pdf)).

**Recent years:** in 2025 the university held Basanta Utsav early, from Monday 10 March, ahead of Dol Yatra on the 14th. Outsiders were kept off the campus and abir, the coloured powder, was banned, which the university put down to the campus's UNESCO World Heritage status (reported by [ETV Bharat](https://www.etvbharat.com/en/!bharat/visva-bharati-celebrates-basanta-utsav-ahead-of-holi-enn25031103084)). A university spokesperson said opening the campus would mean more than five lakh (500,000) people (reported by [PTI](https://theprint.in/india/bengal-govt-imposes-ban-on-holi-celebrations-in-santiniketan-sonajhuri-haat/2545399/)), and [The Statesman](https://www.thestatesman.com/bengal/visva-bharati-university-to-hold-basant-utsav-on-11-march-outsiders-not-allowed-1503398965.html) called it the fourth year running that visitors were excluded. That year the West Bengal government also banned Holi at Sonajhuri Haat, the market in the woods outside the campus (PTI).

**When it would be:** Basanta Utsav is held on Dol Purnima, which coincides with Holi ([Incredible India](https://www.incredibleindia.gov.in/en/festivals-and-events/west-bengal/vasanta-utsav)), though the university moved it to just before Dol Yatra in 2025 and just after Holi in 2026. The central government lists Dolyatra on Monday 22 March 2027. West Bengal's 2027 holiday list wasn't out when we checked.

**What you can do anyway:** the university runs heritage walks on Fridays and Saturdays at 15:00 and on Sundays at 10:00, 11:00, 12:00, 14:30 and 15:30. Tickets are sold only at the Rabindra-Bhavana counter on those days ([notice of 30 January 2026](https://www.visvabharati.ac.in/home/wp-content/uploads/2026/01/img20260130_15045390.pdf)), though in February the university said online sales would follow ([press statement](https://www.visvabharati.ac.in/home/wp-content/uploads/2026/02/Press-Statement.pdf)).

**Getting there:** Bolpur, which takes in Shantiniketan, is 150 km (93 mi) north of Kolkata, with trains from Howrah and Kolkata. The nearest airport is Kazi Nazrul Islam at Durgapur, about 70 km (43 mi) away ([Birbhum district](https://birbhum.gov.in/tourist-place/bolpur/)).

**The trade-off:** unless Visva-Bharati changes course, there is nothing on campus for visitors to join, and the woods outside were closed to Holi crowds in 2025. Wait for a notice before planning around it.

## Holi in Hampi

**When:** Hampi plays on the main day. In 2026 that was Wednesday 4 March, the same as the central holiday.

**What happens:** in 2026, crowds gathered from about 10:00 by the Virupaksha temple, moved along the old chariot street (Rathabeedi) towards Janata Plot and finished in the Tungabhadra river, washing off the colour. It lasted about three hours, with drums instead of loudspeakers and colour sold from pushcarts (reported by [Organiser](https://organiser.org/2026/03/05/342758/bharat/holi-2026-unesco-heritage-site-hampi-turns-into-canvas-of-colours-1000s-of-foreign-tourists-celebrate-the-festival/amp/)).

**Getting there:** Hosapete Junction station is 13 km (8 mi) from the Virupaksha temple, and Hampi is about 340 km (211 mi) from Bengaluru ([Karnataka Tourism](https://karnatakatourism.org/destinations/hampi)). The nearest airports are Jindal Vijayanagar (VDY), Hubballi (HBX) and Bengaluru (BLR) ([Incredible India](https://www.incredibleindia.gov.in/en/karnataka/hampi)).

**Where to stay:** Hosapete hotels and guesthouses reported high occupancy for Holi 2026 (Organiser), so don't leave it late.

**Safety:** at about 22:30 on 6 March 2025, three men attacked a group stargazing by the Tungabhadra canal at Sanapur, near Hampi. Police said two women were raped, and one of the men in the group was found dead in the canal two days later (reported by [Scroll](https://scroll.in/latest/1080063/scroll_in)). Stay in company after dark.

**The trade-off:** three hours of colour for a journey of at least two nights, and nobody has announced which day Hampi will play in 2027.

## Delhi and Mumbai: one day of colour

**When:** the main day. In 2026 most Delhi parties were on 4 March and most Mumbai parties on 3 March.

**Tickets:** 2027 events aren't listed yet. In 2026 Delhi's ticketed parties ran from ₹199 at Dilli Haat Pitampura to ₹2,499 at the Shangri-La Eros, most at ₹499–999 (reported by [Outlook Traveller](https://www.outlooktraveller.com/destinations/india/holi-2026-in-delhi-12-colour-parties-with-dhol-djs-rain-dance-unlimited-fun-you-cant-miss)). Mumbai's ran from ₹499 to ₹2,999 (reported by the [Free Press Journal](https://www.freepressjournal.in/lifestyle/biggest-2026-holi-parties-to-attend-in-mumbai-starting-at-499-only)).

**Getting around:** on Holi 2026 the Delhi Metro didn't run until 14:30 on any line, including the Airport Express (DMRC notice, reported by [Time Out Delhi](https://www.timeout.com/delhi/news/holi-plans-delhi-metro-will-roll-in-only-after-230pm-030326)). Check DMRC's notice for 2027 before you plan the morning.

**Police:** in 2026 Delhi Traffic Police put staff at about 134 major junctions, and drink-driving and other serious offences meant a seized licence, liable to suspension for at least three months (reported by [PTI via ThePrint](https://theprint.in/india/delhi-134-intersections-under-watch-on-holi-drunken-driving-to-cost-3-month-licence-suspension/2868964)). Mumbai Police warned of immediate action against forced colouring, water balloons, obscene behaviour and harassment of women (reported by the [Free Press Journal](https://www.freepressjournal.in/mumbai/holi-2026-mumbai-police-issue-strict-guidelines-ahead-of-celebrations-warn-of-action-against-misconduct)).

**Alcohol:** Delhi took Holi off its dry-day list in 2026, and licensed shops opened (reported by [The Tribune](https://www.tribuneindia.com/news/delhi/delhi-govt-removes-holi-from-2026-dry-day-list/)). Its January–March 2027 list isn't out.

**The trade-off:** a ticketed party is the most controlled way to play, but it's DJs and rain dances rather than ritual, and outside the gates the police spend the day on drink-driving checks.

## Holi in Nepal 2027: Kathmandu

**When:** Sunday 21 March 2027 in Kathmandu and the other hill and mountain districts, and Monday 22 March in the 21 districts of the Terai, the southern plains, on the Home Ministry's holiday list for the Nepali year 2083 ([Home Ministry](https://moha.gov.np/page/government-and-public-holidays-in-2083), in Nepali; summarised in English by [Pradhan & Associates](https://pradhanlaw.com/publications/list-of-public-holidays-2083-202627)).

**What happens:** about a week before, a tall pole hung with strips of cloth, the chir, goes up at Basantapur in Kathmandu's Durbar Square; in 2026 it was raised on Tuesday 24 February (reported by the [Kathmandu Post](https://kathmandupost.com/visual-stories/2026/02/24/phagu-purnima-celebrations-begin-with-chir-installation)). In past years water balloons, called lola, started flying once it was up (Kathmandu Post).

**Rules:** in 2026 the Kathmandu District Administration Office asked people not to force colour on anyone or throw water balloons against anyone's will, and warned of legal action (reported by [The Himalayan Times](https://thehimalayantimes.com/ampArticle/1036937)). In 2025 police deployed more than 2,700 officers in the Valley and said they'd seen no harassment, which had been common in earlier years (reported by the [Kathmandu Post](https://kathmandupost.com/province-no-3/2025/03/14/in-kathmandu-holi-celebration-is-becoming-more-civilised-police-say)).

**Getting in and costs:** a multiple-entry tourist visa costs US$30 for 15 days, US$50 for 30 days or US$125 for 90 days, and you can fill in the form online before you arrive. Indian citizens need no visa, but must carry a passport or an Election Commission voter card; Aadhaar isn't accepted ([Nepal Tourism Board](https://ntb.gov.np/plan-your-trip/before-you-come/tourist-visa)). Basantapur is inside the Kathmandu Durbar Square heritage zone, which charges foreign visitors NPR 1,000 and SAARC nationals NPR 500, with under-10s free ([Nepal Tourism Board](https://ntb.gov.np/plan-your-trip/before-you-come/heritage-site-entry-fees)). We don't know whether the gates are staffed on Holi.

**The trade-off:** Basantapur is the one big gathering point, so it's packed, and Kathmandu plays a day before most of India: choose, or do both.

## Is Holi safe? Women, crowds and what police advise

Harassment at Holi is documented. In 2023 a video showed a group of men in Delhi's Paharganj forcing colour on a Japanese woman, one of them smashing an egg on her head, and police detained three people, one a juvenile (reported by [Scroll](https://scroll.in/latest/1045412/scroll_in)). The UK government warns of a risk of sexual assault, including attacks on foreign women in tourist areas and cities, and of spiked food and drinks ([FCDO](https://www.gov.uk/foreign-travel-advice/india/safety-and-security)). In Kathmandu, police say women were the main targets of unwanted colour and dirty-water balloons in past years. The police responses are in each section above: plain clothes among the Braj crowds, Mumbai's warnings, Kathmandu's appeal against forced colour.

What helps:

- **Go as a group,** agree a meeting point, and keep your phone charged and wrapped. Our guide to [planning a group trip](/blog/how-to-plan-a-group-trip) covers the rest.
- **Choose an organised celebration** if you're unsure: Rajasthan Tourism's Jaipur festival on 22 March, a hotel's own Holi, or a ticketed party.
- **Play in the morning.** Street colour is a morning affair: Delhi's metro didn't start until 14:30 on Holi 2026, and Hampi's play lasted about three hours from 10:00.
- **Don't take drinks, sweets or bhang from strangers.**
- **Numbers:** 112 for police, ambulance and fire in India ([FCDO](https://www.gov.uk/foreign-travel-advice/india/getting-help)), and the Ministry of Tourism's helpline on 1800-11-1363 or 1363, free and open 24 hours, in English, Hindi and ten other languages including Spanish, Italian and Portuguese ([Consulate General of India, Edinburgh](https://www.cgiedinburgh.gov.in/page/24x7-tourist-infoline-for-travel-in-india/)).

**Crowd crush:** the FCDO also notes stampedes at large religious gatherings. In temples, stand near an exit and leave if you can't move freely. Our [solo female travel safety guide](/blog/solo-female-travel-safety-guide-2026) covers travelling alone more generally.

## Colours, eyes, skin and phones

- **Use natural colours.** Rajasthan Tourism's Jaipur event allows only organic, herbal ones, and Baldev's colours in 2026 included flowers, turmeric and sandalwood.
- **Protect your skin and eyes.** Haryana's health department advised against synthetic colours, mud and cow dung, and recommended oil or sunscreen on the skin, covering up, sunglasses, taking out contact lenses and staying in open, airy spots (reported by [The Tribune](https://www.tribuneindia.com/news/haryana/health-dept-issues-advisory-for-holi-celebrations)).
- **If colour gets in your eyes,** rinse with clean water and don't rub. If pain or redness lasts, see a doctor.
- **Phones and cameras:** a waterproof pouch, or leave them at the hotel. Wear clothes you can throw away.

## Is bhang legal?

Bhang, a paste of cannabis leaves often mixed into a milky drink called thandai at Holi, sits outside India's national drugs law: the NDPS Act's definition of cannabis covers charas and ganja but not bhang, as the Jharkhand High Court confirmed in a ruling reported in July 2026 ([LiveLaw](https://www.livelaw.in/high-court/jharkhand-high-court/jharkhand-high-court-bhang-cannabis-ndps-act-539932)). States regulate it, and Uttar Pradesh licenses bhang shops under its excise rules ([TeamLease RegTech](https://www.teamleaseregtech.com/updates/article/40555/uttar-pradesh-excise-settlement-of-licenses-for-retail-sale-of-bhang-t/)). Charas and ganja are illegal, and the FCDO warns that even small amounts can bring a minimum six-month sentence.

**The risk:** the effects start late, so people take more. A Delhi cardiologist warned of anxiety, panic attacks and a racing heart, and the report advises people with heart problems or anxiety, pregnant women and anyone under 25 to avoid it ([NewsX](https://www.newsx.com/health/had-bhang-this-holi-delhi-cardiologist-shares-bhang-is-not-harmless-says-excessive-bhang-intake-can-lead-to-anxiety-or-panic-attacks-177036)). Never drive after it.

| Saturday 20 March | Tuesday 19 January |

| Tuesday 23 March | Friday 22 January |

On the opening day, only Aadhaar-authenticated users can book general reserved tickets on the IRCTC website and app, from 08:00 until midnight, a rule in force since 12 January 2026; station counters aren't affected (reported by [Deccan Chronicle](https://www.deccanchronicle.com/nation/irctc-extends-aadhaar-booking-slot-1929851)). Tatkal tickets, the last-minute quota, go on sale the day before travel, from 10:00 for AC classes and 11:00 for the rest (reported by [Business Today](https://www.businesstoday.in/amp/india/story/new-tatkal-ticket-rules-from-april-15-heres-what-irctc-has-to-say-471774-2025-04-11)), and online Tatkal needs an Aadhaar OTP (reported by [Scroll](https://scroll.in/latest/1083421/only-aadhaar-authenticated-users-will-be-able-to-book-tatkal-train-tickets-from-july-1)).

**Holi specials:** in 2026 the railways planned 1,244 special train trips between 25 February and 18 March, possibly rising to 1,500 (Railway Ministry, reported by [ThePrint](https://theprint.in/india/1244-holi-special-train-trips-planned-from-feb-25-march-18-railway-ministry/2864023)).

**Visitors from abroad:** the Foreign Tourist Quota on IRCTC opens 365 days ahead, with a few berths in First AC, AC two-tier and Executive Class coaches. You need your passport number and an international mobile number verified by OTP, you can pay with a foreign card, and half the fare is kept if you cancel, on top of the usual charges ([Embassy of India, Brussels](https://www.indianembassybrussels.gov.in/railtourism.php)).

**India's e-Visa:** the 30-day e-Tourist visa allows multiple entries within 30 days of your first arrival, so you can fit in Kathmandu and come back. Apply on the official site at least 4 days and up to 120 days before you travel. For most nationalities, including the US, UK, Canada, Australia, Italy, Spain, Portugal and Brazil, it costs US$25 from July to March (US$10 from April to June), the 1-year visa US$40, plus a 3% bank charge. The fee isn't refunded if you're refused. You must enter through one of 37 listed airports, which include Delhi, Mumbai, Kolkata, Jaipur and Bengaluru but not Agra or Udaipur, a listed seaport, or a listed land crossing, among them Raxaul, Rupaidiha and Jogbani on the Nepal border (but not Sonauli) ([e-Visa site](https://indianvisaonline.gov.in/evisa/tvoa.html), [fee table](https://indianvisaonline.gov.in/evisa/images/Etourist_fee_final.pdf)).

## How to plan a week around Holi

**Braj from Delhi, five or six nights.** Wait for UP Tourism's programme, then build around it. Base yourself in Vrindavan or Mathura and arrive a few days before the main day. Add Barsana and Nandgaon once their dates are out (in 2026 they fell a week before the colour day). Keep the evenings of Sunday 21 and Monday 22 March for Holika Dahan and the mornings of Monday 22 and Tuesday 23 for colour until the temples say which is which, and the day after for Baldev's Huranga.

**Rajasthan, six nights.** Bharatpur's Braj Holi Festival on Wednesday 17 and Thursday 18 March, then Jaipur from Friday 19 for the Khasa Kothi Dhulandi on Monday 22. If Udaipur confirms its palace bonfire for the evening of Sunday 21, make Udaipur the second half instead and play in its old city the next morning.

**Two Holis.** Kathmandu on Sunday 21 March, then a flight to Delhi and colour on Monday 22 or Tuesday 23 in Delhi, Braj or Jaipur. Indian citizens need no visa for Nepal. Everyone else needs both visas; the 30-day e-Visa lets you re-enter India, through a listed entry point.

Whichever you pick, put the fixed dates in first, add the ones still to be announced as options, and [lay the days out](/trips/new) in the planner so you can move them with one message when the programme comes out.

## What to book first, and by when

No authority sets booking windows, so this is our advice.

1. **Now:** rooms in Vrindavan or Mathura, Jaipur, Udaipur and Hosapete for 20–24 March. Apply for India's e-Visa if you arrive in mid-March, as the 120-day window is already open.
2. **18–23 January 2027:** trains, on the opening day for your travel date (see the table above). Visitors from abroad can use the Foreign Tourist Quota earlier.
3. **January–February:** watch for UP Tourism's Braj programme, a Visva-Bharati notice on Basanta Utsav, Rajasthan's and West Bengal's holiday lists, the Mewar palace's Holika Dahan date, Delhi's dry days and ticket sales for Delhi and Mumbai parties.
4. **The week before:** ask your hotel which morning the colours start, buy natural colours, and pack a waterproof pouch and old clothes.

## Frequently asked questions

### When is Holi in 2027?

Monday 22 March, on the panchang, with Holika Dahan on the evening of Sunday 21 March. The Government of India and Uttar Pradesh list Holi on Tuesday 23 March, and Kathmandu plays on Sunday 21 March.

### When is Holika Dahan in 2027?

On the evening of Sunday 21 March, between 18:33 and 20:55 IST in New Delhi, according to Drik Panchang. The central government lists Holika Dahan as an optional holiday, and Uttar Pradesh as a public holiday, on Monday 22 March.

### Is Holi a public holiday in India in 2027?

For central government offices in Delhi, yes, on Tuesday 23 March. Elsewhere it depends on the state: Uttar Pradesh closes on 22 and 23 March, and Rajasthan and West Bengal hadn't published their 2027 lists when we checked.

### When is Lathmar Holi in 2027?

Not announced yet. In 2026 it was on 25 February in Barsana and 26 February in Nandgaon, a week before the colour day. UP Tourism lists its 2027 Braj festival for 16–30 March.

### When is Holi in Nepal in 2027?

Sunday 21 March in Kathmandu and the hill districts, and Monday 22 March in the Terai, on the Home Ministry's holiday list.

### Can visitors join Basanta Utsav in Shantiniketan?

Not in 2025 or 2026: Visva-Bharati kept the festival to its own students, staff, alumni and residents and banned coloured powder. Nothing has been announced for 2027.

### Where should a first-timer celebrate Holi?

At an organised celebration: Rajasthan Tourism's Dhulandi Festival in Jaipur on 22 March, a hotel's own Holi or a ticketed party in Delhi or Mumbai. Braj is the most intense, and best on a second visit or with a local guide.
